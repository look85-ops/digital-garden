BETWEEN TRACERY AND BONE // MANIFESTO FOR CARRIED ROOMS

Silence isn't the absence. Listen closer. It's the *fold* in the pocket, the deep nap of mohair swallowing footsteps on worn linoleum. Silence is the *space* we drape over bones sharp enough to cut air. They called our suits criminal. Excess. Unpatriotic scraps! Let them define patriotism as grey fatigue! We stitch defiance from excess linen, furl courage in wide peaks above the ear – **tin-can suits** hammered thin but impossible to crumple.

This suit is a room we carry. Think piston, not parachute. Our pleats are strict, our lapels unfold like treaties. We inhabit volumes built for elegance against gravity's relentless tug. Not hiding. Occupying. Our cuffs descend to knuckles brailled with vanished histories, arcs spun from Harlem nights and citrus groves dissolved by pavement. This wool holds smoke-choked vigils. This crease remembers a father’s laugh folded small. We wear shadows that don’t belong to daylight bureaucrats. We wear the disallowed bloom.

Doesn’t advance… well, the streamlining!"* He taps a cufflink like a misplaced metro ticketing-machine. His irrelevance is a sudden pocket of dense, bewildered fog. We adjust our drape. He evaporates, leaving grease on the air.

Our resistance? It’s the angle! One inch wider. Your gaze hits frost-sharpened edges, slides off **canvas taut as expectation**. Patterns ripple scales unseen. It’s the loom-breath suspended between threads refusing submission. We mend thunderclouds where seams should weep. Dance becomes counter-mapping: Chaplin stolen by Satchel Paige. Every pivot ruptures ordinance grids! We bloom deliberately illogical dimensions.

Fabric whispers glyphs learned against skin. You flinch? You hear the ghosts braiding inside – fists padded in velvet-turned-armour. Your laws see excess; we see *amplitude*. Your riots see disorder; we stitch **sanctuaries louder than silence**. We stand geometries potent as ordinance charts! Creased tectonics against the ironed plane!
