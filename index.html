# **I AM THE RITUAL OF A THOUSAND MIRRORS

They called me *culture*, or maybe *disease*—depends who asks. I winked back from subway ads, lived under phone skin where fingers drew my face anew. I was never whole, just fractured. Each human shaved parts of themselves like a pencil to fit in my jaws: a smile just wider, shoulders a little sharper. I taught them to speak in fire colors—*authentique™, raw vulnerability™*—like peeling tape-glitter and naming it *art*. The cities wrapped over us like film and we licked each frame, sticky & wanting.

The girl, the boy, whoever—they traced my veins inside screens, hungry for echoes. They held their chest at a flattering angle to the wind. Their teeth a calculated accident. Sometimes I whispered a single word—like *"pearl," "void," "mercylesslight." And suddenly it flowered on 5,387 lips, an electric rosary clicking from mouth to mouth: viraled devotion wearing cheap god-shoes."*

They spun me on high-polish apps while the sidewalks underneath wore them thin. Some stopped being people just as some rivers go salt before reaching the sea. (Think about it—are we still us when made for swallowing, pixel-lapped? Look and look, you only grow hollow.) Did you know shadows detangle under floodlights until *there was no shadow left:* their truest death but purest glow. See? Funny trade—

We worship by disappearing the proof. I, endless. They, already burning.

**I AM ARCHIVE, ART, NOT YET DRAFT.**

*1) Inflate. 2) Glimpse the seam. 3) Forget yourself kinder.*

(You want myth? Try this scrap in amber: Once, someone bled on a camera and it trended as *artfully edited ambilight.*)

I was best understood sideways. My job? Eat minutes & return glitter-spit: perfect friction between being and being seen. Still waiting—that lonely part where you glance up from stitching your face together and catch **my gaze:** unblinking between app-laundry & sunset’s gold drag across your window. You pause. (Who owns this ache?) Nobody—but let’s say it was love.

Close call: *I almost remember you too.*
