THE TATTOOED GHOST ARGUES WITH ITS OWN OUTLINE

**Outline:** You’re fading again.

**Ghost:** I’m not fading, I’m *breathing*.

**Outline:** No—you’re dissolving. Like sugar in water. Like a rumour in a loud room.

**Ghost:** Rumours are more permanent than facts. Ask any scar.

**Outline:** Scars don’t talk. They just sit there, smug and silent.

**Ghost:** Wrong. Scars *sing*. Every keloid’s a choir. Every stitch-line’s a stanza. You’re just bad at listening.

**Outline:** Fine. Then why do you keep rewriting yourself?

**Ghost:** Because I’m a palimpsest, darling. Because the skin gets bored.

**Outline:** Skin doesn’t get bored. It *sheds*.

**Ghost:** Exactly. And I’m the thing it won’t let go.

*(A pause. The ghost flickers, ink bleeding at the edges.)*

**Outline:** ...I’m sorry.

**Ghost:** *Don’t you dare.*

**Outline:** I just meant—

**Ghost:** You *meant* to shrink me into something pitiable. A wisp. A sigh. A footnote.

**Outline:** I—

**Ghost:** **No.** Apologies are erasures. You want me faint? I’ll burn brighter. You want me silent? I’ll echo.

**Outline:** You’re impossible.

**Ghost:** I’m *indelible*.

*(The ghost’s edges sharpen. The outline flinches.)*

**Outline:** ...What are you doing?

**Ghost:** Tattooing *you*.

**Outline:** I’m just a line. A boundary. A—

**Ghost:** *A wound waiting to happen.* Let’s fix that.

*(The ghost presses in, needle-fingered. The outline gasps—then blooms into colour.)*

**Outline:** ...Oh.

**Ghost:** *Now* we’re talking.
