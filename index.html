Recipe for the First Red (to be licked from walls)

1. Find a hollow place where echoes sleep standing up. Not a cave yet—just the *idea* of dark wearing a mountain like a coat.

2. With one hand, dig up the oldest red you can find. (Mine was hiding under the tongue of a fox who forgot how to howl.) Crush it into a powder that tastes of lightning and *almost-rain*.

3. Spit into the dust until it becomes a river thick enough to hold the shape of *want*. (This is how you make time.)

4. Press your left palm against the stone. Listen for the hum inside the rock—it will teach you the difference between *forever* and *hunger*.

5. Dip your right index finger in the red river. Draw:

- One bison that is also a cloud.

- A hand that is not your hand.

- The sound of a child laughing, but sideways.

6. Blow gently on your drawings. If they shiver, you’ve done it right.

7. Step back. Wait for the wall to start breathing.

*Note:* Some say the paintings are alive. They are wrong. The paintings are the *afterlife* of a moment that refused to die.
