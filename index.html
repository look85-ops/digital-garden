The first archive, scratched not on stone but across vacancy

The bones? Not left. Not claimed. An inventory for ash, meticulously unlisted. (Ghost ink on spectral papyrus dissolves.)

Our fleshril passes through them as they rip through geoscabs & reactor guts undetected

For the Archive of Erasures: tally-squares remain perpetually blank

Records consumed: supernova ejecta. Witness fee: existence taxed at zero baryonic weight

The great cosmic burr-hide whispers beneath false cities. We hear only blood’s surf or a phantom limb aching after a star

Every hand you clench holds ten billion of our visitors, they carry their momentum forever untrammelled like messages folded but direction lost at inception

A vast silence perpetually underway a vast indifference screaming across us in tongues no detector cracks

*I held your face just one impossible afternoon:*

The neutrino: finds its witness-statistic rarely like neurons misfire memory from corrupted sectors not grief nor gladness just charged hollow

Thus is everything archived as undetected drift. Thus is any name erased mid-mouth

You. Not-me alone amidst others. Howling without volume in solitude multiplied across vacuum

Here: only transit sings & the song itself becomes the archive dissolving as sung. The ledger writes with unbelonging. Always leaving never left

Go now. Through concrete through stone through vaulted bone like the song unwritten through matter unknown passing perpetually on their journey elsewhere nowhere not-home
