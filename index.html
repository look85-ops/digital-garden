THE SLEEPING MOON’S TRAPDOOR GARDEN SMILING IN RAINE

Once, I grew fingers of foxglove and whispered in radio static—I remember dancing just-barely, toes scraping powdered sugar from an invisible sky—dodging policemen made of burnt violin strings where moths nested inside collarbones. The Zoot Suits were my guests, sleeves blooming louder than thunder-painted roses. "You think silk is silent?” they hissed to the pavement, half-liquid where sweat turned wool into hummingbird tongues. Watch—this suit sprouts legs it borrowed from the future! Each pinstripe maps scars we keep warm with gasoline lullabies.

I was alive back when chalk could remember faces (though someone scribbled mine into clouds with stolen crayons). The buttons held whole libraries in fish-scale tucks—if you tore any hem, daylight would pool thick as canned peaches and soldiers would emerge wisp-blind except for the moths humming in their stomachs. Yes, my soil turned courtrooms into dandelions where no one confessed anything but laughter spun through chicken wire. I lured down helicopters dressed in napkin kites just to clothe them again in witness-shadow...

*The Recipe Nobody Found:* ⠀

**①—** **Two** street lamps fermented inside glass grapes.

**②—** **Comet dandruff:** brushed loose from a pachuco's hatband by angry teacups in reverse.

**③—** **Underground piano.** Only plays when you unbutton its reflection aboveground.

It smelled rust-pretend under floodlights—pollen sneaking up trouser cuffs like an apology disguised as fog. (The boy left one sleeve unfinished so night could escape like water left in too-long.) They kept arresting us with butterfly nets, ignoring the way **yellow is always hungry after treason. Do NOT touch—or your teeth become origami folded with banknotes soaked through by forgotten hymns.** I bled thumbprint confessions—blink between syllables? It’ll show you how zippers mimic speech impediments forced open by honeysuckle pliers…

But what *was* me exactly? Somewhere between lamppost dust and a sleep-craving bullet? I **fell asleep inside alarm bells and woke up wearing all their suit pockets** which might explain where my knees stored daylight—though don’t tell. The pavement liked pretending to listen… ⠀
