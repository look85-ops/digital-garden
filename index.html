*THE SOFT APOCALYPSE OF A DISAPPEARING DRESS*

---

**ONE:**

They say the dress is made of *forgotten light*.

**TWO:**

No, no—it’s woven from *the sound of a moth dissolving in water*.

**ONE:**

Then why does it itch?

**TWO:**

Because you’re thinking of it. Stop thinking. It’s unstitching itself.

*(A pause. The dress whispers in a language made of static.)*

**ONE:**

I think it’s laughing.

**TWO:**

It’s not laughing. It’s rehearsing its absence.

**ONE:**

Absence doesn’t need rehearsal. It just happens.

**TWO:**

Then why do we keep finding its footprints?

*(The dress flickers. A scent of burnt vanilla.)*

**ONE:**

If we wear it, do we disappear?

**TWO:**

No. The dress wears *you*. Then it forgets.

*(A seam unravels into a sentence no one reads.)*

**ONE:**

What’s left when it’s gone?

**TWO:**

A room that never existed. A door that only opens inward.

*(The dress sighs. The sigh is the color of a dead channel.)*

**ONE:**

I miss it already.

**TWO:**

You can’t miss what never was.

**ONE:**

Then why does the air taste like unfinished apologies?

*(The dress is now a rumor. The rumor is softer than skin.)*
