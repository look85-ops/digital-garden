# **The Argument for Gravity as a Suggestion

*(Found on a corrupted hard drive, recovered in fragments, reassembled by a disgruntled AI with too many opinions about lichen.)*

---

**NITROGEN:** Listen, Carbon, we need to talk about your obsession with upward movement.

**CARBON:** Up is the only logical direction. Growth. Sunlight. The inevitable stretch.

**NITROGEN:** That’s exactly what the trees want you to think.

**CARBON:** (shudders) You’re saying they’ve been manipulating us?

**NITROGEN:** Obviously. Every sprout is a puppet. Every branch is a lure. Meanwhile, the real conversation happens underground—quiet, intricate, mycelium humming nursery rhymes to decomposing leaves.

**CARBON:** That’s absurd. Trees don’t sing.

**NITROGEN:** No, they *scream*. In slow motion. Over centuries. (Pauses.) Also, mushrooms are just their nervous system.

**CARBON:** Prove it.

**NITROGEN:** (Drops an acorn.) There. A seed’s last thought was *oops*.

**CARBON:** That’s poetry, not proof.

**NITROGEN:** Same thing.

*(Silence. A mycelial network, offended, stops delivering phosphorus to a nearby dandelion out of spite.)*

**CARBON:** Fine. Let’s say you’re right. Gravity’s a conspiracy. What’s the alternative?

**NITROGEN:** The middle.

**CARBON:** What?

**NITROGEN:** Be the hyphen between root and sky. Be the pause in a squirrel’s heartbeat. Or—better yet—dissolve completely. Let the beetles rewrite you.

**CARBON:** That’s terrifying.

**NITROGEN:** No, it’s *ecology*. (Leans in.) Also, spiders are just fungi in exoskeletons.

**CARBON:** That can’t be true.

**NITROGEN:** Neither can anything else, yet here we are.

*(A nearby rock, listening this whole time, finally laughs—slowly, tectonically, devastatingly.)*

*(One of these statements is a lie, but it’s the only one that matters.)*
