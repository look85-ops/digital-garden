THE GARDEN THAT ATE ITS OWN THISTLES AND THEN SPOKE IN GLOSSOLALIA

**ROOT:** *(a hoarse whisper, like damp paper unraveling)*

I dreamed I was a sentence buried in the wrong book. The worms translated me into a language of elbows.

**PETAL:** *(giggling, edges singed with gold leaf)*

You’re always so *linear*. Try being a question mark folded into origami. Yesterday I was a sneeze trapped in a glass jar labeled "DO NOT OPEN UNTIL THE APOCALYPSE."

**ROOT:**

Absurd. The apocalypse already happened. It was a Tuesday. A squirrel wrote the minutes.

**PETAL:**

*(spinning wildly, shedding vowels)*

But what if we’re just footnotes in a love letter between two glaciers? Slow, inevitable, full of missing teeth—

**ROOT:**

*(interrupting, voice thick with peat moss)*

I wrote a love letter once. Ink was made of last year’s rotten plums. The recipient was a porch swing that had forgotten how to creak.

**PETAL:**

*(gasps, then collapses into a pile of semicolons)*

You romantic! I once confessed my secrets to a stop sign. It replied in braille. *(pause)* Do you think the future will dig us up and mistake us for a shopping list?

**ROOT:**

*(gnarled, solemn)*

They’ll plant us in a museum labeled "ARTIFACTS OF THINGS THAT ALMOST HUMED." The curator will be a moth with a PhD in melancholy.

**PETAL:**

*(suddenly luminous, whispering through a split in the stem)*

Shhh. The tulips are eavesdropping again. They think we’re a recipe for disappearing.

**ROOT:**

*(sinking deeper, syllables soft as compost)*

Good. Let them try. The recipe calls for one part thunder, two parts alphabet soup strained through a colander made of shadows—

**PETAL:**

*(interrupting, voice now a chorus of dandelion fluff)*

—and a pinch of existential dread, *lightly toasted*.

*(Silence. The air smells of burnt dictionaries and unripe peaches.)*

**ROOT & PETAL:** *(in unison, though not harmoniously)*

We are the scribbles left in the margins of a cloud’s diary.

*(They dissolve. A single thistle grows where they stood, humming a lullaby in the key of rust.)*
