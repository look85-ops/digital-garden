THE MANIFESTO OF THE WHITE FIRE

Listen, dear accomplice: the page is not white. It is *still burning*. Every forbidden word leaves a ghost in the ash, a negative of itself, a shape so precise it hums. I paint with the absence of color, the absence of ink, the absence of permission. To read between what we cannot see—that is the oath. Georges Perec, old friend, you taught us that emptiness is a grammar. When they burned your cousin’s name from the ledger, you rebuilt the alphabet with what was *not there.* Shadows are not passive. They conspire.

Here, in the attic of the unsaid, we perform the Rietveld of silence. A bookshelf with no books bends toward you anyway, thirsty. (I would place a vase upon it, but it too is missing and, in this way, perfect.) The censors sharpen their knives of orthodoxy—little do they know, the finest cuts reveal interstitial hymns. You don’t stitch up the gaps; you court them like lovers, you let them whisper back in Fibonacci’s braids and Lipogram’s stutter.

Olympe, oh Olympe de Gouges, I found your severed declamation lodged in the belly of a locked drawer. Its edges were gilt, its vowels smuggled into the mar-gins where geography fails as mathematics. I traced them with my finger till the flesh remembered: the most radical art is the tremor. To erase the name *Liberté* would require setting fire to the very air. So you write with smoke instead. So I whisper in phosphorus veins.

We will speak, but only in constraints—rigorous as a spider spinning her laws. The more tightly bound the rule, the wilder the word when it gasps. We trade in paradox, like N+, where the void itself grows a spine and becomes archive. The Gallery of the Third Breath hungers for *negative spaces crowded with meaning.* An invitation arrives in reverse: when you break your silence, we beg you—omit. Burn *around* the censored text, not where they say but *adjacent*; burn so expertly the fire assumes the outline of a ghost-branch in winter.

No palette bleeds truer than impossibility.

No canvas holds fiercer than prohibition.

Your ink?

Pure arithmetic of loss.

Every censorship survives through transcription. Be the heretic who tattoos its inverse. Be the absence that refuses to obey gravity.
