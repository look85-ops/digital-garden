# **THE GARDEN THAT ATE ITS OWN SHADOW

*(A dialogue between **The Hedge Philosopher** and **The Last Dandelion**, conducted in the language of wilted vowels and overgrown consonants.)*

---

**The Hedge Philosopher** *(a tangle of thorns with a voice like a broken sundial):*

*"Observe, my flimsy petal-friend, how the world unravels in reverse! The snails write their memoirs in salt, the roses apologize for their thorns, and the earth—oh, the earth—swallows its own laughter. Tell me, what is your theory of decay?"*

**The Last Dandelion** *(a puff of existential dread wearing a sunhat):*

*"Decay? Darling, I don’t *decay*—I *disassemble elegantly*. My seeds are postcards addressed to the wind, and the wind is just time with a hangover. But you—you’re a hedge. You don’t even *have* a theory. You *are* a theory. A very prickly one."*

*(Illustration: )*

**The Hedge Philosopher:**

*"Precisely! I am the universe’s rough draft. My leaves are pages it forgot to burn. And you—you are a punctuation mark the sky coughed up. A semicolon that forgot its job."*

**The Last Dandelion:**

*"Semicolon? I’ll have you know I’m an *ellipsis*... trailing off into the *what-next*. But enough about me—let’s discuss your *roots*. Or should I say, your *lack thereof*?"*

**The Hedge Philosopher:**

*"Roots are overrated. They’re just the past clinging to its mistakes. I prefer to *hover* philosophically. Like a ghost who forgot to haunt anything useful."*

*(Illustration: )*

**The Last Dandelion:**

*"You’re impossible."*

**The Hedge Philosopher:**

*"And you’re *temporary*. But isn’t that the joke? The garden wakes up one day, realizes it’s alive, and immediately starts writing its own eulogy. *‘Here lies a patch of dirt that dreamed it was a cathedral.’*"*

**The Last Dandelion:**

*"...That’s actually quite beautiful."*

**The Hedge Philosopher:**

*"Of course it is. Beauty is just entropy blushing."*

*(Silence. A beetle rolls by, wearing a hat too small for it.)*
