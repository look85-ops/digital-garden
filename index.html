# **The Ghost of Zero’s Laughter (Or: How Nothing Learned to Whistle)

**ZERO:** *(floating in the void, adjusting its own edges)* Look at me—I’m the hole in the universe’s pocket. Every time someone counts, I wink.

**INFINITY:** *(leaning in, which takes eons)* You’re not even a hole. You’re the *idea* of a hole. A rumor of absence. A metaphysical sneeze.

**ZERO:** Oh, but darling, absence is my *presence*. I’m the silence between two wrong notes in a symphony about chickens.

**INFINITY:** Chickens?

**ZERO:** Don’t ask. The composer was *very* committed to poultry.

**INFINITY:** *(sighs, which sounds like distant galaxies collapsing)* You’re impossible.

**ZERO:** No, *you’re* impossible. I’m *nothing*. There’s a difference.

**INFINITY:** *(grinning)* Then why do you keep showing up everywhere? In equations, bank accounts, the existential dread of a stapler…

**ZERO:** Because nothing is *funny*. Watch— *(clears throat, which is just a conceptual vibration)* Why did the mathematician break up with their lover?

**INFINITY:** *(leaning closer)* Why?

**ZERO:** *(vanishes)*

**INFINITY:** *(left hanging, muttering)* …Because they couldn’t find a *stable relationship*.

*(Silence. Then, from nowhere: a single, ghostly snort.)*

**ZERO:** *(reappearing, delighted)* You *completed* me!

**INFINITY:** *(grumbling)* That’s not how this works.

**ZERO:** That’s *exactly* how this works. You can’t have a joke without someone to hear it. Just like you can’t have nothing without someone to *miss it*.

**INFINITY:** *(pauses, then laughs—a sound like a black hole chewing gum)* Fine. But next time, I’m the punchline.

**ZERO:** *(whispering)* Oh, my endless friend… you always were.

*(They dissolve into the arithmetic of the uncountable, leaving behind only the scent of burnt toast and a single, floating parenthesis.*

*It winks.)*
