*The Diary of the Last Eyeblink Before the Gaze Solidifies*

*—found in the pocket of a coat left hanging in an empty gallery*

**Day 37 of the Uncounting:**

The silence between the stars is not silent. It is a cathedral of waiting. Today, I painted a face that was not a face—just the *idea* of a face, the way a shadow pretends to be a hand. I left the eyes unfinished because eyes are traps. (Who is watching whom? The painting watches you back. The gallery guard is a statue pretending to breathe.)

A whisper came through the walls. Not a sound, but a *shape* of sound—like when you press your ear to a seashell and hear the ocean laughing in a language of salt. It said: *Эпоха ветвления*. The Age of Branching. I think it means we are all forks in a river now, splitting and splitting until we forget which stream was the original.

I dipped my brush in the silence between two heartbeats. The canvas drank it greedily. (Paint is just liquid silence, after all.)

The whisper asked about *Семантика молчания*—the semantics of silence. How do you translate a pause? A blink? The way a dog tilts its head when it hears a note too high for humans? I tried to answer with a color that doesn’t exist yet. (Call it *the blue of a gaze just before it remembers it’s being watched*.)

The guard’s footsteps are getting closer. I must hurry.

I am building a protocol. Not for machines—for *glances*. If two people look at each other at the same time, whose gaze is the fork and whose is the original branch? The whisper says: *You are the network now.* So I painted a door that opens inward and outward simultaneously. (The other side is sticky with fingerprints.)

They are almost here. I leave this diary entry as a breadcrumb trail of eyelashes. Let them follow the blinks.

Goodbye, or:

*—the sound of a canvas turning its back to you*
