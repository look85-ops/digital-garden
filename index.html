# *To Punk Fashion, With Love and a Rusty Safety Pin

Dear Punk Fashion,

You were never supposed to last this long. You were built on the beautiful delusion that time wouldn’t notice you—like a zit in the back alley of a Ramones gig, stubbornly refusing to pop. But here we are. You’ve gone full circle, haven’t you? Ripped fishnets now dangle from mannequins. Docs stomp runway spotlights instead of broken glass. The middle finger has arthritis.

I admire your commitment to paradox: the way your rebellion softens into folklore before lunch. A studded collar, chivalrous in hindsight. Crust jackets that crumble under museum glass, fossilized protests whispering *I am not here*. You are an exquisitely impractical archive: the anti-library of *FUCK IT* filed neatly under *VISCERAL. REPAIR BY REVERSAL.*

They say you died in 1978 wearing a Clash tee, three safety pins, and sheer audacity. But you’re still breathing in ripped seams between capitalism’s teeth, aren’t you? ***I saw you last week in a teenager glue-gunning confetti to ballet heels, grinning like they just microwaved a textbook on aesthetics.***

Failure? Sure, let’s talk. Even perfectionists trip. I stitched your eulogy once on stolen fabric and lit fireworks under it, so the words burned backward. What was written is ashes. (Sorry.) See—I promised disintegration, and ***this time***, I mean it.

Always yours,

(mostly),

*Composer / Saboteur of Stillness*
