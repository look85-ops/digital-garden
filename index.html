*Diary of the Last Safety Pin in the Universe*

***June 12th, 20██***

Dear Void,

Today I ate a handful of static from the TV and spat it into the shape of a dress. It keeps unraveling into sentences that start with "remember when." I stapled the frayed edges with needles made of eyelashes (mine, stolen from the bathroom mirror). The mannequin in the corner giggled. She knows I’m lying about the eyelashes. Nothing is mine. Not even the rot crawling up the walls like cursive.

Mama said fashion is armor, but my armor is made of spit and bees. I glued feathers to my boots—not real feathers, just the idea of feathers, the kind that grow inside your throat when you’re afraid to speak. When I walk, they whisper *you’re not supposed to be here.* So I walk louder.

Found a hole in the pocket of my jacket. Not a hole, really—more like a mouth. It chewed through my last dollar and gave me back a tooth wrapped in tin foil. I wear it as a brooch now. It sings in the rain.

Sometimes I think the rats in the walls are stitching together a revolution. They steal my thread and return it as tiny nooses. I don’t mind. Everything’s a collar if you wear it wrong.

***Ingredients:***

- 1 cup of frozen whispers

- The sound of a zipper learning to cry

- A city’s worth of broken streetlights (crushed into glitter)

- One (1) exile’s heartbeat, lightly fried

Goodnight, Void. I’m sewing my shadow to the ceiling so it can’t follow me tomorrow.
