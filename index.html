*THE WEIGHT OF SKY WE FORGOT TO LOOK UP TO UNDERSTAND*

There is a hole where blue used to be. I know because I dug it myself, with a teaspoon I found abandoned under a parking lot gutter. Most holes look black or muddy brown, but a proper extraction of blue—oh *proper-ness* means nothing here—leaves behind a glistening notch like the split eyelid of morning blinking awake too softly to wake you. Blue *seeps* between realms (did you know even *time* looks blue, bruised halfway to history?). It isn’t missing, no. It never stays missing.

On a silver platter I set out all my colors—fat tubes squeezed belly-heavy with burnt ochre, forest green slick as frogskin, cadmium red cracking its knuckles—they all laughed like children tumbling from beds too tall too tall—except for Ultramarine; she hid. Cowering behind my ribs like paint shoved in a sock drawer still cold-wet, saying *please I was named for the sea over miles and miles crushed stone and still*

*drowned when he asked for proof. Proof!* As if proof is worth anything close to *want.*

This work consists of:

1. Nine sheets of the paper hummingbird tongues are bundled in (*paper was made somewhere blue once grew*).

2. One drop of cobalt pretending to be lonesome, sliding slow as glacier-wounds down the bathroom mirror (*so long*).

3. A chalk outline traced from the silhouette left behind too-long-starers in grocery store aisles where the frozen meals shiver blue under flickering halogen baptism.

4. You are the solvent.

Here is where I leave it alone: in the wall where the nail-hole was made without permission—who hammered first? When did yearning take architecture lessons?

Listen: the pigment was running out even before Lapis Lazuli rode stolen Crusade steeds. Even before Renaissance debts split its dust across chapel ceilings. So I filled the tubes with *empty-that-reflects*, emptied all the names—robin’s egg? peacocks and twilight and blueberry’s *me against we*? Too slippery now under oil-slick of our hunger. One patron licked an exhibition label to taste extinction but it just reminded them of their first bike; pedal-fast-blue peeling under teeth-marks because they needed to carry their heart like cargo already breaking, always already—(*where?!* the heart the horizon the highway bleeding its *maybe*… *where?).*

(You have stopped reading now, distracted by a door opening three miles southeast behind your sternum—the one where cobalt was buried with a spoon that smelled of lightning.) Did you think you dug the hole by yourself?

Stare into it long empty and it glazes you like tilework—toss a prayer in *dear ocean i have a question*. Watch the surface ripple until it does not. The museum guard who counts every blink in here looks bored but leans hard the question

*(can a shade feel abandonment)

(came apart so slow it lost not color but* category)

*(once-blue never remembers having)*

If blue is found: this confession will shrink backwards like the tongue of a saint touching a cold stove. But for now *touch the space.* Here. Yes. You. You already have paint chips lodged beneath *most careful.* Somewhere.
