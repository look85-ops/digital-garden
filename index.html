The Void's Autograph

**October 23rd**

Rain again. Not that I see it. The skylight is permanently occluded by a layer of meticulously applied Nothing. A triumph, really. Took weeks. The landlord’s complaints vibrate through the floorboards – a dull, persistent hum about "structural integrity" and "unnatural darkness." Fools. They fear the density. As if absence could collapse a building! It’s the *idea* of the load-bearing void that terrifies them. The same terror that burned those early mathematicians who dared whisper 'Zero'. Dangerous cargo, that syllable. Shipwrecked civilizations.

Tried sketching the sound of Mrs. Henderson’s cat yowling next door. Not the *noise*, you understand. The precise shape of the silence it displaced. Used my finest eraser on virgin black paper. Achieved a remarkable… *dent*. A negative relief. Held it up. The paper felt heavier. Charged. Like holding the ghost of a scream. Filed it under 'Potential'.

**October 24th**

Visitors. Curators from the Biennale of the Nameless Island. They stood in the center of the studio for precisely seventeen minutes. Said nothing. Admirable restraint. Or profound boredom? Hard to tell with the truly perceptive. Offered them distilled water – the closest thing I have to hospitality. They declined. Watched them watch the space where my last major piece, *"The Subtraction of All Possible Blues"*, used to hang. Now? Just wall. But *charged* wall. Wall that remembers cyan, remembers ultramarine. They breathed it in. One murmured, "It… vibrates." Success. Or tinnitus. [*Lifts the resulting silence from the room and folds it into a perfect, weightless origami crane.*] Left it hovering near the ceiling. They didn't see it. But they *felt* the air get thinner. Paid me in whispers and a single, perfectly smooth pebble. Currency of the void.

**October 25th**

Disaster. Or revelation? The pebble. Placed it on the windowsill where the Nothing-skylight meets the wall. This morning, a faint grey smudge beneath it. Dust? Impossible. I vacuum with theological fervor. No, this was… *residue*. The stone, dense with its own stony being, was pressing *out* against the curated absence. Creating a tiny, accidental stain of presence. A bruise on the void. Zero fighting back? Or perhaps… collaborating? The most dangerous idea isn't nothing. It's that nothing *isn't*. That the void has texture, memory, a terrible kind of friction.
