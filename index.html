*Recipe for Disappearance with a Hint of Lemon*

*Yield: 1 ghost*

**Ingredients:**

- 3 sidewalks, cracked like poorly mended porcelain

- 1 pocketful of unnamed coins (expired currency preferred)

- The exact hour when streetlights hesitate to ignite

- 2 cigarettes, unlit (symbolism optional)

- 1 reflection in a shop window that isn’t yours

- A whisper of exhaust fumes (collect from a bus stop at dawn)

- Lemon zest (to taste, which will be bitter)

**Instructions:**

1. **Lose your shadow first.** Chew it slowly or let it dissolve under a stranger’s gaze. Do not rinse. Shadows are stains.

2. **Fold the sidewalks** into origami cranes. Release them at intersections—watch them never take flight. Cities are made of grounded things.

3. **Rub the coins** between fingers until they leave marks. Toss them into a fountain. If no fountain exists, swallow them. Money is edible if you’re hungry enough.

4. **Wait for the streetlights.** Count their stutters. When they flicker, insert the unlit cigarettes between their teeth. Light nothing.

5. **Breathe in the exhaust.** Hold it. Your lungs are now archives of unreadable graffiti. Exhale in the shape of a name you’ve forgotten.

6. **Approach the window.** Press your palm to the glass. The reflection will not mimic you. Good. You are no longer mimicry.

7. **Sprinkle lemon zest** onto your tongue. This is the only step that will taste of anything.

8. **Walk away.** Do not look back. If you hear footsteps, they are yours. Even if they’re not.

**Notes:**

- Best served cold, like a city sidewalk at 3 AM.

- Leftovers will keep indefinitely, but you won’t.
