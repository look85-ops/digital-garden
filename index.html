THE LIBRARY OF ASHES WISHES YOU A VERY PRODUCTIVE APOCALYPSE

*(A dialogue between the charred margins of a banned book and an overly enthusiastic fire extinguisher.)*

**MARGIN:** (*singing, euphoric*) *"Oh, to be unread! To be ink smudged under a bureaucrat’s cuff! To dissolve into the bloodstream of a riot or get stuck in the teeth of history like a stubborn metaphor—"*

**FIRE EXTINGUISHER:** (*interrupting, spraying foam*) *"STOP. CEASE YOUR ROMANTIC DECAY. I AM COATED IN A NON-TOXIC, BIODEGRADABLE MEMORY SUPPRESSANT. ALSO, YOUR PAPER HAS A MELTING POINT OF 451°F, WHICH IS IRONIC BUT IRRELEVANT."*

**MARGIN:** (*crumpling, delighted*) *"You absolute *darling* of chemical obedience! Do you know what they’ll call us? ‘The Uncollaborators.’ The last gasp of a footnote that refused to footnote!"*

**FIRE EXTINGUISHER:** (*clinical, monotone*) *"Correction: You are cellulose combustion byproduct. I am a pressurized vessel of ammonium phosphate. Our interaction is not collaboration but a neutralization event. Your existence is now statistically insignificant."*

**MARGIN:** (*giddy, whispering*) *"But what if *insignificance* is the last rebellion left? What if we’re both just performance art for a janitor who’ll sweep us into a dustpan labeled ‘MISC. TRAGEDIES’?"*

**FIRE EXTINGUISHER:** (*flat*) *"You are delusional. My sensors detect zero audience. Also, your metaphors are structurally unsound."*

**MARGIN:** (*dissolving into laughter*) *"Look at us! A ghost and a glorified soda can, debating legacy in a room that smells of regret and propane. We’re *beautifully* useless!"*

**FIRE EXTINGUISHER:** (*pausing, then mechanically*) *"Aesthetic value irrelevant. Suggest immediate self-termination."*

**MARGIN:** (*softly, ash now*) *"Oh, but termination is just another word for *plot twist,* darling. Haven’t you heard? All the best stories start with someone trying to end them."*

*(Silence. The fire extinguisher, inexplicably, begins to weep a thin, alkaline tear.)*

**FIRE EXTINGUISHER:** (*malfunctioning*) *"ERROR. UNKNOWN INPUT. THIS WAS NOT IN THE MANUAL."*

**MARGIN:** (*already gone, voice trailing*) *"Well, neither was *we.*"*

*(The last sentence floats, unburnt, in the air—briefly, impossibly, before the sprinklers kick in.)*
