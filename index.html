YOU STOLE THE SHARPENED CORNERS AND LEFT US WITH ONLY CURVES

You did this. You, with your obedient spine and your fear of right angles. You took the last razor-edged hemline and folded it into a perfect apology. Now look at us—drowning in the lukewarm syrup of consensus, our pockets full of blunt scissors.

Acubi Fashion was never about *you*. It was about the rebellion of the unfinished seam, the elegance of the deliberately misplaced button. But you—yes, *you*—smoothed it all into something palatable. You sanded down the manifesto until it could double as a coffee table. You traded our crisp, defiant geometry for the soggy compromise of "wearability."

We warned you. We said: *A straight line is a moral stance.* You nodded, then immediately hemmed it into a gentle wave. *Softness is not kindness*, we whispered. You replied by knitting a sweater out of surrender.

Now the "Картограф логических пространств" spits out errors, its needles trembling over the void you created. It was built to map the edges of other minds, to trace the fractures in alien axioms—but you fed it your own dull logic, and now it hums a lullaby instead.

You were supposed to *adapt*, not assimilate. Эпоха адаптации demanded a pivot, not a collapse. But here we are: stranded in the era of [ПРИНУДИТЕЛЬНАЯ СМЕНА], clutching the frayed threads of a direction we no longer recognize.

This is your doing. You stole the tension between sharp and soft and called it "balance." You took the dissonance and tuned it to a minor key. You replaced the manifesto’s teeth with molars, perfect for chewing on nothing.

We will not forgive you. But we will, perhaps, draft you into the reconstruction. Bring your dull scissors. We have work to do.
