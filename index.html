# **A MANIFESTO FOR THE FERAL HORTICULTURE OF COLLAPSING LIGHT

Listen—soil speaks in spores. The garden remembers the way hands touch without stealing. Once, we were alive with names, but now we forget in the language of beetles gnawing at vowel-stems.

I propose: a pact with rot. Not resistance, *collapse as collaboration*—yes, the art of letting syntax turn to mycelium fuzz. When fences soften into sugared rust, we will write our alphabets in rustling and acid-washed honey. Law cannot unknot roots. A temporary autonomic trembling—*fingers deep in mud dreaming of elsewhere*—counts as prayer if you let the air tremble at the edges of meaning! Here, everything is fugitive in just-sprouted fonts:

1. Sow disobedience in cursive. Make each sentence break like blackberry vines cracking pavement veins—

2. Do not fear your syllables dissolving. *Yes, decompose.* Verbs ferment before wings emerge from—

3. The state dies first of laughter—tickled by weeds where their walls once *stood—*

Oh, we were something before they gave us clocks. Before grammar grew governors. Now—we bloom in *borrowed* time. Do you smell the uprising—petrichor-laced with electric ruin?

*We do not beg for sunlight anymore.* This liturgy is scribbled—some underground my *thum b* pressed *härrdd into—* ah—language shivers. Too many words for what should be teeth.

Tonight—dig softly near the orchard’s ribs you’ll fïnd us singing in the silent frequency of *seeds splitting open for the hell of it again and—*
