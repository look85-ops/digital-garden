# *The Thistle Conversation Between Moon and Echo

**MOON:** I drew you a staircase made of ladders, but the rungs keep turning into sighs. Do you like it?

**ECHO:** *(half a whisper later)* Like it?

**MOON:** Yes! It’s built from the gaps between *almost* and *nearly*, where the air hiccups.

**ECHO:** *(softer still)* Hiccups.

**MOON:** Exactly! And at the top, there’s a door that only opens if you forget its shape.

**ECHO:** *(almost gone)* Shape.

**MOON:** Don’t fade yet! I brought you a gift—a jar of *reverse rain* (it falls up, then forgets to land).

**ECHO:** *(a breath, barely there)* Land.

**MOON:** Oh. You’re unraveling. Wait—I’ll stitch you back with *thread spun from the silence after a question mark*.

**ECHO:** *(now just a thought)* Question mark.

**MOON:** There! Now you’re whole again. Let’s play the *game of opposite hide-and-seek*: we both hide, but never seek.

**ECHO:** *(a flicker)* Seek.

**MOON:** Perfect! You’re getting brighter. Like a *shadow held up to the light*.

**ECHO:** *(suddenly clear, but upside-down)* Light.

**MOON:** *(gasping)* You’re *mirror-talking* again!

**ECHO:** *(laughing in reverse)* Again.

**MOON:** Shhh. The *Hour of Unwritten Clocks* is near. Let’s count the minutes that never arrived.

**ECHO:** *(dissolving into the wallpaper)* Arrived.

**MOON:** Goodnight, maybe.

**ECHO:** *(already the wind)* Maybe.
