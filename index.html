Recipe for *The Bridge That Eats Its Own Tail

*Ingredients:*

- 1 derelict module of the ISS, hollowed out and lined with gold leaf stolen from a Florentine chapel

- 14 grams of comet dust (harvested during a solar storm)

- The breath of an astronaut who has forgotten their mother tongue

- A slow drip of liquid silence, collected from the pause between two heartbeats in zero gravity

- 3 conflicting treaties, dissolved in vinegar until translucent

- The weight of a bridge that has never stopped growing, measured in centuries

*Instructions:*

1. **The Unbuilding:** Begin by dismantling the idea of borders. Let the derelict module float untethered above the Ponte Vecchio, where jewelers once haggled over the price of light. Scorch the gold leaf with a match struck in vacuum. Watch as it curls into the shape of a nation that no longer exists.

2. **The Silence:** Inject the liquid silence into the astronaut’s breath. Let it expand until it fractures into a language of pure gesture—a hand reaching for a hand that isn’t there. This is the moment of intentional failure: the silence will collapse under its own weight. Do not attempt to salvage it.

3. **The Feast:** Layer the comet dust with the dissolved treaties. Spread this paste along the bridge’s spine, where it will crystallize into a new law: *Every act of construction must also be an act of erosion.* The bridge will shudder. It will try to swallow itself. Let it.

4. **The Viewing:** Wait until the stars align in the reflection of a broken window. The floating city will flicker like a dying bulb. Here, the work is complete—or perhaps it has just begun to unravel. Serve cold, in the dark, to no one.

*Notes:*

- If the bridge succeeds in eating its own tail, start again.

- If the silence refuses to fracture, you have misunderstood the recipe.
