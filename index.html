THE PALIMPSEST EGG HATCHES THE FUTURE'S HAND

Here is a canvas scraped raw so many times it’s become convex. A reverse fossil—my grandparents’ famine dug into the sugar of my blood, grooves so deep they loop like melodies I can hear if I lie perfectly still. Science calls it *methylation*\--I call it my body’s marginalia in god’s used paperback. The ink bled through. Someone dog-eared the trauma *just there*, three generations later, I get paper cuts when people say the word *potato.*

I write absence like ink. Scratch it backwards—the echo unspools DNA like a joke’s second half that won’t land because everyone forgot where the punchline dug a hole back in ’43. The punchline’s daughter still flinches at uncreased bread.

Fanfiction bends time. Someone remixes Gatsby’d ribs into a better-loved chassis. Someone paints Oppenheimer soft with eyeliner while Berlin sings (a chorus clicks *subscribe*). *Oh*, we crave sequels for damaged lineages where grandpa *listened*—but history can't revise, only rhyme. What we touch stays. The page stains even if your pen runs dry. Which isn't technically true: light burns what bleach won’t. A story told enough times *peers back.*

My masterpiece: empty frames screwed to a wall lit slant-wise, so a phantom negative flattens between their edges—the shape your spine remembers from Before The Flood (was 24 or 3,001 pages thick). Sometimes what survives isn’t plot but cadence—what your cells hum when the lab coats snip the threadbare word **heredity** from Epix® Drama Of The Human Genome (2016—Present—Pending Cancellation).

A critic stands squinting past my gallery of white on white on white. A moth on the windowsill tremors just *so:*. The lie here (ha) is I’m just the painter; in fact, an heirloom spoon I swallowed writes through me on some doctor’s fluoroscope film.

I sold every pigment I stole. The leftover is this: a ghost-pulse where the red you gave up still blooms behind closed ribs long-
