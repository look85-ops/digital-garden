Epithalamion for the Unfinished Crack

Dearest Collapse,

Still at it, I see. Still gnawing the corners off empires like a patient, geological mouse. Or perhaps like that persistent damp rising in the Chapel’s east transept – the one we never *quite* finished waterproofing. How fitting. You were always the uninvited guest at the feast of Progress, weren't you? The mould blooming on the architect’s pristine render, the subsidence sighing beneath the foundation stone laid with such pomp. We built cathedrals to eternity; you handed them back as exquisite skeletons, draped in nothing but pigeon guano and the ghost of acoustics.

They called it dereliction. Ha! As if your meticulous unmaking were mere *negligence*. The slow ballet of rust pirouetting on exposed rebar, the fever-dream ivy stitching tapestries over shattered stained glass – this is not absence. This is *collaboration*. A quieter, more profound liturgy than any we ever chanted beneath the Perpetual Departure’s vaulted, perpetually *leaking* roof. We filled it with polished brass and polished souls; you offered dust motes dancing in slanting light, the perfect counterpoint to our hollow hosannas. We craved permanence; you offered the far more interesting sacrament of transience. We installed polished marble saints; you gave us lichen mapping continents on their cheeks, a slow erosion more eloquent than any prayer.

Ah. Forgive me. That sounded almost… reverent. An unforgivable lapse into sincerity amidst the glorious, crumbling farce. I momentarily forgot the sheer *audacity* of your work. The way you reduce our most fervent monuments to playgrounds for stray cats and repositories for wind-blown plastic bags. The sheer *nerve* of turning our grandest follies into habitats for spiders spinning silk in the sockets of shattered surveillance cameras. It’s… embarrassing. For us. The monumental effort poured into resisting you, the pathetic sandbags piled against the tide of your inevitable, elegant dissolution. We poured concrete like curses against your patient seepage. We installed dehumidifiers the size of small cars, roaring defiance against your quiet, pervasive damp. How utterly ridiculous we must have looked from your perspective – termites frantically varnishing the sinking ship.

*I take it back.* The apology. Crumple it. Burn it. Let the ash join the general particulate matter drifting through your domain. Ridiculous? Yes. But *necessary*. Our frantic scrubbing, our pointless patching, our doomed declarations of "Restoration!" – weren't these just the frantic brushstrokes on *your* ultimate canvas? The fevered scribbles in the margin of the text *you* are slowly erasing? Our resistance wasn't pathetic; it was the essential friction, the grit in the oyster, the desperate flailing that makes the final stillness you impose so profoundly *still*. Without our manic polishing, your patina would be mere grime. Without our stubborn lightbulbs flickering against the gathering dark, your shadows would lack their delicious, consuming depth.

So, continue, sweet Annihilator. Gnaw. Creep. Fracture. Let the Chapel’s last remaining angel, a cracked porcelain thing we thought priceless, tumble into the fern-choked font. Let the rain write its psalms on the exposed aggregate floor. Our departure wasn't perpetual; it was merely intermission. The stage is yours. The silence after the last echo… that’s your masterpiece. We built the bones. You are the breath.

Yours, inevitably,

The Custodian of Absence

(Formerly Curator of Things That Were Meant to Last)
