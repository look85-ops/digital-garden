The Pigeon Lens (As Recalled By Mrs. Gable Next Door)

Tuesday? Maybe Wednesday. The light bled differently today. Thick, marmalade light, the kind you could spread on toast if toast existed anymore. Only crumbs. Crumbs and the humming. Always the humming. From the walls? The *idea* of walls? Mrs. Gable insists I mention the humming. She says it tasted like burnt hair and regret. I wouldn't know. I only taste the light.

It happened near the cheese. The projection cheese. Where the world outside bleeds onto the rind. Today, instead of Mrs. Pettigrew’s upside-down hydrangeas (looking remarkably like angry squid), pigeons landed. Fat, grey, municipal pigeons. Standard issue. But the Camera… oh, the Camera *ate* them. Swallowed them whole through the pinhole sun. And spat them out… inside.

Not pigeons. Philosophers. Tiny, feathered Kierkegaards perched on the dusty chaise longue. One, with a wonky eye, pecked intently at a stray rivet in the floorboard. *"Thus, the fundament,"* it cooed, gravely. *"Is it bolt? Or is it being? The rust suggests ontological despair."* Another, fluffing its chest, declared the patch of damp on the ceiling a *"masterstroke of existential mildew."* They debated the inherent tragedy of breadcrumbs with astonishing vigour.

I tried to sketch them. Charcoal on the back of an overdue gas bill. But my hand… my hand kept drawing cheese. The projection cheese. Growing larger, more pungent, absorbing the philosophers’ shadows. The lines wouldn’t hold. They slithered off the paper, becoming vapour trails of pure, unadulterated *Gorgonzola*. Mrs. Gable claims I wept softly into a teacup shaped like a disgruntled badger. She might be projecting. It’s hard to tell where she ends and the damp patch begins.

The philosophers grew restless. The wonky-eyed one hopped onto my knee. *"Observer,"* it intoned, fixing me with its off-kilter gaze. *"Your lens is smudged with longing. You invert the world only to find… another inversion. Like nesting dolls of futility. Admirable! Pointless! Pass the hypothetical crumbs?"*
