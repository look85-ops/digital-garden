### **[Oubliette Siriènes— The Corset Whispers as the Girl Unknots]

She laces herself into a famine of bone—one rib after another collapsing, her organs soft trespassers. Tight-lipped lace gossips; each grommet eye is sutured shut. A cage in reverse, this hollow where light twists: not constriction but the shape hunger *wants*.

Tell me it’s adornment—no—

tell me it’s a wolf gnawing its teeth dull against steel teeth. (See how flesh wearies of itself faster.) The corset hums to the spleen as pliers to piano strings: this song was always extraction. Breathe shallow enough, and even pain silken-strangles in your veins: oh darling straitjacket of roses—where to after the ribs cave? The womb plays dead.
