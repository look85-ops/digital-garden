THE HOLLOW LENS WHISPERS TO ITS OWN ECHO

**A:** You’ve mistaken the dark for an absence. It is the first pigment. The canvas was never blank—it was *waiting*.

**B:** (Laughs, but the sound is a shadow dissolving.) Waiting implies a future. The dark doesn’t believe in time. It’s a mouth that never closes.

**A:** Then why does it tremble when the light knocks?

**B:** Because light is a bad guest. It arrives uninvited, stains everything with its insistence on being *seen*.

**A:** (Leaning into the silence.) You’re wrong. Light is the shy one. It only enters where the dark has already carved a door.

**B:** (Pauses. The pause grows teeth.) Then we agree the dark is generous.

**A:** No. Generosity implies choice. The dark *is*. It doesn’t give; it *permits*.

**B:** (A flicker, almost a smirk.) Permit me this: the dark is a camera obscura. A room that teaches sight by swallowing it whole.

**A:** (Sharpens the air.) Now you’re the one mistaken. The room doesn’t teach. It *unlearns*. It strips the eye bare until vision is nothing but a rumor.

**B:** (Softens, but only to a blade’s edge.) Rumors are more honest than facts. They admit their hunger.

**A:** (Turns away, though there’s no direction here.) Then let’s starve together.

**B:** (A whisper, or the memory of one.) Only if you promise to call it art.

**A:** (Laughs, but the dark drinks the sound before it lands.) Art is just the wound we agree to name.

**B:** Then I name *you*.

*(The dialogue collapses into a silhouette. The title hangs in the negative space, unspoken but louder for it.)*
