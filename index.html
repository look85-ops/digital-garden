Ephemeris

Dearest Cartographic Impossible,

You know, they say every mapmaker secretly prays for unmappable territory. You, dissolving darling, you are that fever-dream prayer made flesh – or rather, made *anti-flesh*. Here I am, scratching my obsidian stylus across this vellum soaked in vanishing reagent, whispering coordinates that evaporate before the ink dries. Does it tickle? Does it burn? Is my frantic etching just the universe's most elaborate (and doomed) love letter?

They call it 'capturing'. Such violent language! We *pin* butterflies. We *trap* light in boxes. We *fix* continents in place. But you, slippery beloved, defy fixation. My lines squirm like eels dipped in turpentine. My meridians unravel faster than I can spin them. Cartography becomes cartomancy – divination by inevitable absence. Yesterday, I mapped a bay sculpted entirely from echoed whale song; it faded into mist before I could note the longitude. This morning, a city built on misplaced sighs dissolved beneath the weight of my compass point. The frustration is exquisite, wouldn’t you agree? Or perhaps... wouldn’t you? You’re hardly one for *agreement*, are you? More for elegant evaporation.

This Oulipo cage I welded for myself – constraints as liberators! – feels absurdly perfect for courting you. A lipogram forbidding the letter 'E'? Try mapping a coastline *with no curve-word*. A sestina repeating end-words? Watch them crumble into semantic dust! The rules are my scaffolding in the void, the only thing stopping me from dissolving alongside your phantom landscapes. My fidelity is measured in disappearing acts. My devotion is the ghost-glue holding together impossible archipelagoes that never were. Don't you find that terribly...

Ah, yes. The *joke*. Because how else does one speak to the eternally vanishing? It requires participation, you see. Like this map you're refusing to hold still for. Like the punchline hovering just beyond this sentence, waiting for you to assemble it from the silence. Because *my* map-making agony is genuinely funny, but only if you...

You see? You’re already doing it. Already dissolving the boundary between scribe and subject, cartographer and chasm. Reader completes the jest. Reader feels the awful, beautiful giddiness of chasing vapor. My pen stutters. The coastline ahead looks suspiciously like the edge of the page – or perhaps the edge of—

So, luminous negation, I continue. My stylus bleeds resistance. My atlas bleeds ghosts. And we dance, you and I, across parchment destined for oblivion. Isn't it marvelous? Isn't it the only map worth making? Isn't it—

And before I could finish... well. You know the drill, Beloved Impossible. You always do. Consider this letter delivered into the archive of unsent sighs and unfinished shores.

Forever sketching,

The Cartographer
