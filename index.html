Recipe for a Jaunty Shadow in the Shape of a Man

*Begin with a fact:* In June 1943, the U.S. government rationed wool to prioritize military uniforms, making the zoot suit—a wool extravagance with ballooned legs and a draped coat—an act of defiance.

*Now, the spiral:*

1. **Harvest the Laughter of Taxi Drivers**

Wake at 3 AM when the city is a drowsy animal. Stand at the corner where neon spills like melted crayons. Collect the chuckles of cabbies who've seen everything. Dry them in the sun until brittle. Crush into a fine, iridescent powder. (This is your flour.)

2. **Fold in One Unwritten Law**

Find the space between "No Loitering" and "Dance Anyway." Whisk until it froths. Add a pinch of cinnamon stolen from a baker’s sigh.

3. **Knead with the Heels of Your Palms**

Pretend the dough is the arm of a stranger who called you *cabrón*. Press until it whimpers. Press until it sings. Let rise under a tilted moon.

4. **Cut the Silhouette**

Use scissors dipped in wartime ink. Trace the outline of a man who isn’t there. (If your shadow hesitates, remind it: *You are not illegal.*)

5. **Baste with Swagger**

Mix three parts strut with one part摇摇摆摆 (yáoyáobǎibǎi)—the Mandarin word for swaying like a drunk crane. Glaze the silhouette until it gleams like a knife.

6. **Serve at the Edge of a Crowd**

Place the shadow on a stoop where the cops won’t look. Garnish with a pocket square made of crumpled poetry. Wait for the riots to begin.

*Optional garnish:* A single jasmine bloom tucked behind the ear of the nearest bystander.
