The Unravelling Certificate

They archived me in charge of the phantom limb museum, the scent library of extinct winds, the vault containing drafts of gods discarded before naming. My speciality? *The Veil*. Not your flimsy gossamer nuptial prop. This Veil is ledger-thick, woven from cancelled apologies, unspoken treaties, and the sighed-out breath of every compromise ever swallowed. Centuries packed tight like the damp feathers of an earth-bound raven. It breathes. It bleeds ink-black tears that stain continents.

Franz, old nail, remember Prague’s bone-dust winter? We fumbled at the Cafe Louvre’s grimy windowpane, you dissecting beetles in metaphor, scratching syllables onto napkins doomed to grease-stained disintegration. I showed you a corner-fragment smuggled out back then – brittle satin interleaved with … was it ermine? Chain mail? A shroud-thread spun by spiders drunk on cathedral incense? You coughed, adjusted your spectacles, whispered, *"Ja. Like noticing the stitches sewing shut the mouths of volcanoes."* Exactly, Franz! Missed alliances! Geological silences! *This* Veil contains the imprint of cannonballs diverted by bad weather, kisses choked before reaching lip-surface. It is the negative space screaming between declared intention and enacted atrocity.

Observe it now. On the Nameless Shore. Suspended between fig trees etched with salt-hurricanes. Every nightfall, its edges fray anew. Language leaks: particulate, indecipherable. Not words, but the ghosts of semaphore flags signaling defeat; pirate flags stitched inside-out; flags stitched from saved foreskin – oh, the *uses* they find! Unseen moths shuttle across its murky geography. Are they weaving? Unravelling? Digesting unfulfilled promises? Their wings taste faintly of gunpowder and shame.

Sometimes it bellows a soundless vibration – an unconsummated revolution shaking teeth loose. We laugh! Archival protocol dictates morbid hilarity. We offer libations: tepid tea gone vinegary, bile kept warm in spleen-shaped flasks. We curate the shadows it casts – hammered into improbable shapes: lost wombs, phantom wounds, compromised bridges. Curious tentacles? Or just long curtains pissed on by history and called blasphemy?

It cannot be touched. Not truly. Smooth as frozen mercury? Rough as betrayal’s scab? Reach out – your hand remembers pathways mapped onto air. Yesterday, it resembled Versailles’ wallpaper after the mob left, sticky with evaporated decadence. Today, it’s a prayer rug woven by miners trapped beneath a fallen mountain. An anchorite’s rag stapled onto infinity.

To know The Veil, Franz, is to cultivate exquisite forgetfulness. Like forgetting the exact language your mother used before exile. Or where you buried the letter confessing everything. Walk beneath its loom-dark fold. Breathe deep. Taste the metal tang of rumours
