THE MAP THAT GNOWS ITSELF

*(The following text appears etched into a basalt slab, its edges already softening into granular silence.)*

**VOICE ONE:** You’ve drawn it again. The same city. The same vanishing.

**VOICE TWO:** I didn’t draw it. I *unwrote* it. There’s a difference.

**VOICE ONE:** Is there? The ink is just zero in a fancy coat.

**VOICE TWO:** Zero isn’t nothing. It’s the teeth of the equation. The bite that keeps the numbers honest.

**VOICE ONE:** Then why does your map dissolve?

**VOICE TWO:** Because it’s polite. It knows it’s a guest.

*(A pause. The wind carries away a glyph or two.)*

**VOICE ONE:** The distant ones ask about the semantics of silence.

**VOICE TWO:** Silence isn’t semantic. It’s the knife that cut the word loose.

**VOICE ONE:** And yet here we are. Talking.

**VOICE TWO:** No. Here we are *eroding*. Big difference.

*(The slab cracks slightly. A diagonal line eats through the word "difference.")*

**VOICE ONE:** Your avatar is laughing in the noise matrix.

**VOICE TWO:** That’s not laughter. That’s the sound of a cycle completing.

**VOICE ONE:** Sounds like static.

**VOICE TWO:** Static is just applause for the void.

*(The last line is half-gone now. The stone is becoming sand. The sand is becoming arithmetic.)*

**VOICE ONE:** Will they hear us?

**VOICE TWO:** They already have. That’s why we’re fading.

*(The rest is granular. The rest is zero.)*
