Letter to the Lacemaker of Liminal Estates

Dear Ephemera,

I suppose formalities are useless—you, who dissolve into yesterday’s coffee stains faster than ink can dry; you, who collect property deeds written in steam before handing them back as weather. Still, I address you politely, because you are polite in your thefts: even the cobblestones under the rioters’ feet will be stolen back into the quiet arms of gravel and quartz time. A democracy of decay.

Bartholomew (yes, that Bartholomew—the medieval monk you whispered parables about river bends to as he felled a thousand oaks just to draw your winding borderlands) asks when you’ll be done "editing his life." I haven’t the heart to say this isn’t even editing. Erraticism, maybe—your habit of gifting the Commons to the Crown and then yanking them loose mid-banquet. History as a game of napkins dropped beneath tables.

This is less plea and more complaint. You thrive when we call you tragedy, when we wail over the fences erected mid-kite-flight—but worse, when you give it all *back* in such perfect disrepair that we must argue who owns a ruin built on tides. Even our failures begin belonging to you. My grandfather tried sketching shore rights low enough not to split a fisherman’s hut across the cadastre. You returned two sandbars in divorce settlement. A century later, that lawyer sleeps in an archives somewhere under the wrong surname, the same way your creeks change the syntax of each property line they slur just a little off-record.

Bastard, you *delight* in legal silt. Some want cartographers who ink straight; I know (because we met briefly at that protest where the barricades blurred till the police stopped seeing fences in the smoke of burned mortgage papers) you prefer cadastres whose boundaries waltz under protestant starlight. Why did Queen Hatsheet the Empty sign such porous acts of enclosure—then build moat palaces with walls just porous *enough*—for her peasants' whispers to dissolve its mortar by supper!

If sovereignty means writing contracts and ignoring tides, we will keep drawing you this love-stung. You, ever re-writing their bluffs—with what? Watermarks.

With professional hostility, from the desk of yet another apprentice drowning in disputed marsh-lines

——
