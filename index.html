HOW TO DISAPPEAR A NUMBER

Begin with the cleanest knife you own. One that has never cut anything. Sterilize it with the absence of light.

Take the number you wish to erase—say, 7—and place it on a surface that does not echo. A mathematician once proved that "all surfaces echo, except the one you need" [1], so this step is theoretical. Proceed anyway.

With the knife, make an incision along the number’s spine. You’ll find nothing inside. This is correct. The emptiness is the number’s most vital organ.

Now, the delicate part: fold the number inwards, like a love letter to no one. The crease should be so sharp it becomes a wound. A wound is just a zero with edges.

Let the folded number rest in a solution of distilled silence (3 parts hush, 1 part held breath). Do not stir. Stirring reintroduces time, and time is the enemy of vanishing.

When the number has dissolved into a stain, pour the liquid into a cup made of unanswerable questions. Drink it. You will feel nothing. This is the desired effect.

To confirm the number is truly gone, try to count to it. You’ll skip from 6 to 8 without noticing. The gap will feel like a room you’ve always avoided.

If anyone asks where the number went, say: "It was promoted to imaginary." Then walk away. Walking away is the final step in all disappearances.

[1] *The Acoustics of Absence*, Dr. Elio Voss, 1947 (out of print, out of mind)
