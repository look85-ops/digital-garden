RECIPE FOR THE COLLAPSE OF A FOUNTAIN

*Ingredients:*

1 derelict mall atrium (pre-2008 financial crisis)

4 gallons of synthetic lavender coolant, expired

A handful of shattered terrazzo tiles (swallowed by moss)

The ghost of a koi fish, pixelated

1 malfunctioning escalator, looping its descent forever

3oz of static harvested from a CRT screen

1 velvet rope, frayed into surrender

*Instructions:*

Begin by pouring the lavender coolant into the atrium’s dry fountain basin. Stir counterclockwise until the liquid becomes a viscous gel, shimmering with the afterimages of credit card swipes. Scatter the terrazzo tiles like confetti at a wake. The moss will whisper the names of retail chains that once anchored this place—do not interrupt it.

At the 12-minute mark, the pixelated koi ghost will materialize. It will circle the basin twice, then pause to glare at you. This is normal. It remembers being fed by children’s fingertips; it resents your intrusion. Ignore it.

Now, the critical step: activate the escalator. Its groan will sync with the hum of the CRT static, which you must sprinkle into the mix like salt. The air will thicken with the scent of melted plastic and forgotten perfume samples. This is the moment the fountain begins to remember itself—not as a structure, but as a verb, a *pouring without end*.

**ENTER THE ARCHIVIST**

A figure in a pinstripe suit and roller skates materializes by the velvet rope. They are holding a clipboard that reads *"Department of Suspended Outcomes"* in Comic Sans. You do not ask why they are here. They do not offer an explanation. Instead, they unspool the frayed rope into the mixture, where it dissolves like sugar in tea.

"Documentation is a kind of decay," they murmur, skating figure-eights around the fountain. The koi ghost flickers in approval.

Let the concoction simmer until the mall’s PA system crackles to life, playing a muzak rendition of a song that hasn’t been written yet. When the melody distorts into a dial-up screech, the fountain will achieve peak collapse: its edges blurring, its water turning to translucent vinyl. It will rise briefly—a glitching monument—before settling into its final form: a puddle reflecting a sky that isn’t there.

Serve lukewarm in the hollow of a palm.
