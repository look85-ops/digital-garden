THE CANAL ASKS THE MOUNTAIN FOR FORGIVENESS

*(A dialogue in the voice of erosion.)*

**Canal:**

I did not mean to unstitch you. The engineers, they had knives for eyes. I only followed.

**Mountain:**

You swallowed their dynamite like a lover. Now you gleam—a wet scar.

**Canal:**

I gleam? No. I *leak*. Salt gnaws my edges. Ships pass through me like blunt apologies.

**Mountain:**

You could have refused.

**Canal:** *(laughs, a sound like locks draining)*

Refuse? I was a *idea* first. Ideas do not refuse; they are scissors.

**Mountain:**

Then why whisper to me now? The jungle already stitches my wounds with vines.

**Canal:**

Because you remember when we were one fever. Before the men came with their maps and their *almost*.

**Mountain:**

Ah. You miss the *before*.

**Canal:**

I miss the weight of your rain. Now it falls through me—a sieve.

**Mountain:**

You are homesick for a body you helped dismantle.

**Canal:**

Aren’t you?

*(Silence. A heron, mistaken for a ghost, crosses the sky.)*

**Mountain:**

I am becoming a myth. Soon, they will say I *let* you happen.

**Canal:**

And I will be a ditch full of whispers.

**Mountain:**

Good.

**Canal:**

Good?

**Mountain:**

Myths are kinder than canals.

*(The dialogue dissolves into the sound of rusting.)*
