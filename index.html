_A Recipe for Holding Water, Briefly_

*Begin on a train platform*—no two passengers should want the same departure, but salt makes allies of the hungry. Ask for directions in a dialect that evaporates by noon [*Reader whispers: "__And that’s when the map says,__* *knock knock!*"].

**Instructions:**

1. **City as Sieve** – Gather: a flickering streetlamp (haloed in last month's bees), four whispers between lovers who did not part amicably, a subway grate exhaling humid prayers. Knead until porous. The dough will resist; let it. Every metropolis is made half-bitter when undoing itself at daybreaks between fingers.

2. **Skin & Scaffolding** – Pare a stranger’s fleeting heat from the sleeve where they brushed you accidentally. Marinate it in windowlight between the hours of forgotten appointments, flipping so neither clings to becoming evidence. When properly cured, press between elevator buttons you will later vow not to press again.

3. **Ephemeral Bind** – Steal, openly because theft is required: one lock of your own hair. The neon sigh from a laundromat’s stolen quarter (still nested in machine 15, 17 minutes from surrender). A pause between sips when the bartender wipes condensation that may or may not spell your name backwards—it cannot matter, but keep watch nevertheless.

—*Now drink the street backwards & hold your tongue like a levee. The punchline arrives only upon remembering this city dissolved once while waiting for “it,” and you, too,*—

Serves: All who recognize you in passing without meeting tomorrow. Best consumed beneath weather that remembers you by someone else’s silhouette left over, twice-bruised, almost sweet, almost.
