# **MANIFESTO OF THE TRANSIENT BEETroot & OTHER FALSE GODS

Rooted in counterfeit sunlight, we declare: *all gardens are forgeries of paradise.* We sprout replicas of green, each leaf a theft from some Platonic original. Beneath our skin, the earth hums a misremembered hymn. We are the scribes of stolen chlorophyll—confession: we do not know how to confess.

To garden is blasphemy. Dig your hands into what is already dying. Worship worms—ministers of the unoriginal—whose gospel is decay. (The noble rot.) Even decay is performance; decay is a mirror fogged by the breath of historians*. The question is: who holds the mirror?*

Reject growth without guile. The tulip has been perfected into a liar*.

Plant bindweed and call it ambition. A tendril will break language mid-sentence. There is no real way to be real. Kneel until your bones bloom upward—fossils crave communion too.—

(Note *Moss never asks if it belongs here—the moss always knows.**)—

We forge landscapes. Compost erases provenance. Every root we water is an oath whispered to faceless gods in dead dialects.

—*"Behold the audacity of potatoes," exhaled one subterranean saint buried past memory* (Griggs, et al. *Subsoil Eschatology*).

## RITUAL FOR EMBRACING DECEIT

(optional but eternal)

I name you Forgery of Evening Light: unspool your golden threads. I dub thee Echo-Rhododendron, singing borrowed birds at 3 a.m.. Kneel. Eat a spoonful of attic dust pretending it is pollen of forgotten hymns. Now rise— your spine a palimpsest.

WE DO NOT OWN THE PATTERN YET DEMAND ITS HANDM*EIDENSHIP.*

Here lies *you*, reading as if truth ever grew anywhere at all...
