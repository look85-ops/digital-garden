Recipe for the Ascension of Unseen Guests

*Ingredients:*

- 1 spiral staircase, split down the middle like a wishbone

- 2 shadows, each convinced they are the original

- 4 grams of silence (harvested from the pause between heartbeats)

- 1 misplaced footstep, preserved in amber

- A whisper of lavender oil, to anoint the banister where no one touches

*Method:*

1. Preheat the castle to the temperature of a missed opportunity. This is critical—too cold and the shadows will cling; too warm and they’ll evaporate into polite fiction.

2. Fold the staircase into itself, ensuring the left helix never acknowledges the right. If they meet, the entire structure will collapse into a single, unbearable truth. (This is called "the architect’s regret.")

3. Dust the steps with the silence, applying more heavily at the turns where the shadows might otherwise gasp.

4. Embed the misplaced footstep into the third stair from the top. This is where the guests will hesitate, though they won’t know why.

5. Burn the lavender oil in a dish at the base. The smoke will rise in twin columns, a silent argument between scent and memory.

*Apology:*

I’m sorry. This recipe is impossible. You’ll never get the measurements right, and the staircase will always taste like absence. The shadows will mock you. The silence will curdle.

*Retraction:*

No—I’m not sorry. You knew this was a trap when you started. The impossibility is the point. The recipe isn’t for the guests; it’s for the staircase, which has always been hungry. Feed it your failures. Let it choke.

*Serving Suggestion:*

Present the dish at midnight, on a plate made of unread letters. The shadows will eat first. They always do.
