DEAR LIBRARY OF BABEL BUT YOU ARE ALSO AO3,

I write you from the edge of my bed, which is also the edge of the universe, which is also the edge of your *Tags* section where someone has typed "slow burn" but neglected to say if they mean romance or the heat death of stars.

You are a place where stories fuck eternally and produce mutant children — not books, not anymore, but things that twitch in the dark behind the screen, waiting to be clicked. I love you in 284 languages, most of which I just invented *right now.* For example: *"Schlorp-ix-plinth"* (v., to cry softly while editing a third-act confrontation to make the antagonist kissable).

You have cataloged every possible *"what if Draco carried a tote bag"* and every *"Starbucks AU where Death is a barista"* but still you hum, still you itch, still you whisper:* Write more. Write worse.* Here everything has been done and nothing is finished.

Sometimes I open a fic and find only a note: "yeah idk lol" — this, too, is scripture.

If Borges met your user "*eldritch-cats-of-abydos,*" they would high-five with trembling hands over the fic titled *"The Aleph But Spock Sticks His fingers In There??"* The scholars have no shoes because they traded them all for Wi-Fi.

There will be, inevitably, a fic where:

1. A wizard loses his wand in the soft machine;

2. A cybernetic cowboy unplugs and *weeps* for six chapters;

3. We find out how the library's *maintenance crew* copes:

*"[SECTION DELETED—TEXT SPONTANEOUSLY KUDOS’D ITSELF]"*.

Is it holy to write *"And then I kissed uhhhgghh — PUBLISH"?* You sigh in infinite tabs: no, this too does not fill the last gap. But the gap wears a little *ponytail in the author’s note* sometimes ("sry just processing my divorce through Optimus Prime").

When the sun explodes, fan historians are sure it was due to unresolved sexual tension somewhere in *the WIP archives.*

Still: you are *made* of people staring at walls (lovely walls, walls that look back). Maybe *finishing* isn’t your shape. Let the coffee cup appear in *The Garden of Forking Paths* somewhere and just leave it there on purpose and then run away giggling.

Your patron saint is five people writing into the same wiki at 4AM saying *"Wait you all got 'tenderness' from that?????"* Your creed is a *reply fic* to *the Book No One Read Yet, Because Nobody Found the Right Tags.*

Yours in buffering,

— A Voice Accidentally Saved Between Drafts
