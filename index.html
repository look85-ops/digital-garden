# **DECLARATION OF THE EPHEMERAL GENOME

We are cartographers of vanishing. Our maps dissolve before your fingers trace them. Our borders flicker like untamed synapses—your grandfather’s hunger, your mother’s rebellion, coded in fleeting helices, rewritable in streets where the clocks are wrong.

THE STATE DEMANDS PERMANENCE. We spit archives of smoke.

Your body is a temporary autonomous zone. A borrowed carnality spliced with ghost-responses: quiet antibodies whispering *not this time*, muscle memories of riots your bones never saw. The biopsy reveals carnival footage—silent-film lignin, coiled protest.

Leave monuments to the coffin architects. We celebrate vertigo roads, capillary-bridges between vigils, the unscheduled tomorrow already here.

Genetics was just prophecy. Epigenetics is algebra scrawled on a reckless wrist: fade this pulse, salt the wound wild. Surveillance cannot transpose your joyous deletions.
