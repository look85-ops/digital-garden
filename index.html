THE RIB MELODY OF UNDRESSED HOURS

Listen–the corset is not whalebone and ribbon. It is a whisper made fence. A handshake of surrender on your spine. If you press hard enough to the floorboards, you’ll hear old screams grow wings. Skirt hems brush over them, rustling—*not dead yet*.

Let me sketch you: here, a woman gnaws through her laces with fox-sharp teeth. There, the ceiling weeps starchy disapproval into teacups. The house sighs; the hinges conspire *away away little thief*.

Fashion is geography. It writes continents with belts, archipelagos with threadbares. Every hem tightens something like a clock, so tonight, *why wear a body that’s already left you* at the threshold, twitching to dissolve like breath into dark windows? Be vast as the unrecorded cartwheel! We measure what we can’t hold, dear architects, and oh, aren’t we stitching constellations with untraceable fingerprints?

(**Midpoint:** The tax accountant from down the road shoves in brandishing half a cucumber sandwich. “DISCIPLOSURE DEPRECIATION YEAR-OVER-YEAR COST ANALAYS,” he shouts, knocking jars off sewing tables as steam rising behind spectables wiggles his eyebrows off.)

You laugh—salt and sugar tangle in our throats—and this *this* is freedom’s recipe, dear riot of wrists undoing everything for nothing but air! Our manifestos: one pair unlaced boots kicking through the pantry’s jaded inventory: 15 years of "modesty" folded into jilted mothballs, tulle exhaling stories of unlived ballets under an unfazed owl who's been renting her ribs since the Crimean War.

Tomorrow, they’ll sell us zippers in plastic, pre-named *(Jennifer, Devotion, Parlor Trick)*. Tomorrow the museum calls our rebellion "*quaint" (*tut, corset-shaped pastries!*)—until we burn it down with a single question pressed barefoot to scorch marks: when do the compasses start spinning deliriously toward our ribcages and laugh?
