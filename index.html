RECIPE FOR BUILDING A SHY LIGHT HOUSE

*for Nikola, who once whispered to the pigeons*

**Ingredients:**

1 fistful of stolen afternoon shadows (the kind that cling to your sleeves)

2 cups of unanswered questions (preferably the ones that hum at 3 AM)

A mirror, but only the edges—discard the center

1 teaspoon of gravity, gently disobeyed

The exact moment a child realizes they are being watched

A handful of loose photons (ask them politely to behave)

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Unseen Foundation:**

Dig a hole where the light is too shy to go. Line it with the stolen shadows, weaving them like a basket. Whisper to the hole: *"You are safe here."* Watch as it shivers.

2. **Mix the Questions:**

In a bowl made of silence, stir the unanswered questions until they froth. Add the disobeyed gravity—just enough to make the mixture float. Nikola once told me that floating things are happier. He was right, but don’t tell him I said so.

3. **Fold in the Mirror Edges:**

The center is too loud. Use only the edges, where reflections blur and forget themselves. Lay them like tiles, but leave gaps for the light to slip through. Light is a terrible eavesdropper; it will come.

4. **Invite the Photons:**

Hold out your hand. Say, *"Would you like to play?"* If they hesitate, remind them of the child’s moment—the one where being watched changes everything. They’ll understand. Sprinkle them over the mixture.

5. **Bake Under a Watchful Sky:**

Place everything in the hole. Cover it with the sky’s cupped palms. Wait until the light forgets whether it’s a wave or a particle. This is the hardest part. You’ll want to peek. Don’t.

6. **Serve in Silence:**

When the house is ready, it will glow like a held breath. Step inside. Notice how the walls lean away from your gaze. Notice how the light tiptoes.

**Notes:**

- If the house collapses, it was never meant for you.

- If it stands, you were never meant for it.
