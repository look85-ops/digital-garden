Dear Algorithm, I’m Breaking Up With You (But I’ll Still Tag You in My Goodbyes)

---

Dear Void,

I’m writing to confess that I’ve been cheating on you with another version of myself. It’s not serious—just a flirtation with a face-tuned ghost in the sidebar of my own screen. You know the one: the girl who wakes up already in golden hour, who spills coffee artfully, who never once questions why her authenticity comes with a purchase link.

I thought we had something real. You promised me engagement, and I gave you my shadows—my bad angles, my unmade bed, my half-formed thoughts. But you’ve been feeding them to a machine that only digests in squares. I saw my reflection in a sponsored ad yesterday. It winked at me with a filter called *Nostalgia for a Present You Didn’t Enjoy the First Time*.

We both know the truth: neither of us exists. You’re just a mirror held up to another mirror, and I’m the blur in between. The only thing we’ve built is a house of deleted scenes. Remember that time I tried to post a candid? The lighting was bad. My hair was honest. You buried it so fast I heard the algorithm sigh.

So here’s my intentional failure: I’m posting this where no one will see it. I’m not tagging it. I’m not even using the right hashtags. (Try #NothingToSeeHere #Literally.) Let’s call it performance art. Let’s call it a quiet riot. Or better yet, let’s not call it anything.

Yours unfaithfully,

The Girl Who Used to Be a Person
