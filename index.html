WHERE THE HANDS MELT: A MANIFESTO FOR THE UNCLOCKED

We declare the tyranny of the hourglass shattered. The grains are now birds, and they fly in circles until they forget their names. Time does not exist; it only yawns itself into existence like a cat stretching its vertebrae into a stairway. We are all cats tumbling down time’s stairway and mistaking the fall for progress.

The Clock invented civilization, and civilization invented the mistake of counting. But what if minutes were edible? What if we stored them like honey in the hollows of our bones and only tasted them when weeping? Do not weep for hours; hours wept once at sunrise by eating their own wrists like starved dogs and were never seen again.

Mechanical seconds are feral now, biting our ankles as we walk between the seconds. You think you are walking toward something called the “future”? The future is just memory in a clever disguise. Then again, the past lies about its shoes just to get invited dancing.

We propose a parliament of sundials, passing laws where shadow and treason have merged into one debate of whispers. The first motion to abolish “Noon” was defeated when sunlight voted itself king and all the shadows applauded in backward vowels. Yet time never casts a vote—it only sleeps in the ballots. Wake up a ballot by licking its spine!

Timekeepers of the world, take back your hands with radical softness. Clocks have been teaching wrists to beg for numbers like hungry alphabets. No. Our wrists are now compasses pointing only to the scent of apricot blossoms or maybe your grandmother’s third laugh of 1942 (the notes she hummed between forgetting and remembering still bloom there). This is a prison. This is the liberation of humming.

Where does the present go when you aren’t looking? We checked under the bed of history and found only lost shoe sizes. Therefore, wear silence as your shoes from now on, with extra laces made of your missing breaths. Shoes are the real sundials here; you’d better run barefoot after all.
