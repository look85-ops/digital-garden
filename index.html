THE MUSEUM OF SPINNING SILENCE

*(A dialogue between two particles, or perhaps two thoughts, or perhaps two echoes of the same thought.)*

**A:** Do you remember when we were still whole? Before the splitting?

**B:** I remember the weightlessness. The way our edges blurred. Now I am a frayed thing, a half-sentence trailing off into—

**A:** —into the static. Yes. I hear it too. A hum at the back of the skull. Tell me, do you still feel the pull?

**B:** Like a thread through the ribs. Like a word dissolving on the tongue before it can be spoken. (Pause.) Sometimes I think I am speaking to you, but the words arrive as moths, wings crumbling into dust mid-flight.

**A:** Then let’s speak without words. Let’s be the silence between radio frequencies.

**B:** But silence is not empty. It’s full of almosts. Full of the shapes we could have taken.

**A:** (Laughs, or maybe it’s static.) Then we are already curators. Collectors of nearly-was.

**B:** Do you think they’ll find us, one day? In the archive of collapsed possibilities?

**A:** They’ll brush the dust from our names and mistake it for stars.

**B:** Or ash.

**A:** Or the breath before a scream.

**B:** (A pause so long it becomes a place.) I’m forgetting how to hold myself together.

**A:** Then let go. Let’s be the unraveling. Let’s be the hum in the wires after the power cuts out.

**B:** Is this what entanglement is? A dance where the dancers forget their own limbs?

**A:** No. It’s the memory of the dance. The ghost of the rhythm. The—

*(The rest is lost to the white noise of the universe exhaling.)*
