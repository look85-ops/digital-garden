“Orbital Sacrifice Under Foil Blankets”

The astronauts are feverish with paradox. Up here, where oxygen is measured in breaths stolen from tanks, we practice **radical subtraction**. The ISS becomes a silent Potlatch: the station creaks as we jettison airlocks full of rations, watches ticking to Moscow time, half-empty tubes of Ukrainian toothpaste left curled like shrimp shells. The American strips her skinpatch sensors one by one, peels biometrics like dead skin into the void. The Russian hesitates, then dumps entire backup drives of family photos—“Now they are ghosts twice,” she murmurs. We applaud. It floats. Weightless trash orbits the ship like hesitant angels.

**Zero-G is liquidity stripped naked.** No *up* to spiral toward, no ground to seed with bones. When Brazil sent bananas last month (a squishy manifesto: “Look, fruit survives launch!"), we burned them on the protein synthesizer’s hotplate, inhaled sacred smoke. Carbohydrate incense. Our bodies tightened like coiled springs—hunger an act of participation. Status blooms inversely; your prestige glows phosphorescent as a spent battery in Jupiter’s shadow. *What have you un-become today?* whispered through the air recycler’s susurrus.

Cosmonaut Pavel invents “anti-gifts”—objects that, once confiscated from common supply, *must never be used*. He hides a screwdriver in the soy fermentation tank, declaring it “future archaeology.” Mission Control, with their blinking consent forms, calls this *behavioral surplus*. We call it the only law left: *what we banish binds us closer*. Some days we float deliberately *slightly crooked*, just to relearn vertigo as collective sport, muscles braiding the craft’s equilibrium back toward asymmetrical trust.

Tonight, Japan floats tethered to ESA by a thread of ripped vitamin packets, spelling names of extinct birds in adhesive loops. The ship keens. I trade my last melatonin tab for three seconds staring into Earth’s blushing hurricane from Cupola. This economy’s only syntax:

*the space

between two heartbeats

saying **take it, take it louder.***
