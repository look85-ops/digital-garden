Recipe for a Theremin Made of Commons

*Ingredients:*

1. A field left fallow (preferably one with disputed ownership)

2. The hum of a power line no one claims to maintain

3. Two hands, yours or borrowed, but never owned

4. A single copper wire, stolen from a public utility box

5. The exact frequency of a dandelion seed breaking from its stem

*Instructions:*

1. **Locate the field.** If it is fenced, do not cut the lock. Instead, wait until dusk when the boundaries blur. Walk the perimeter until your shadow crosses itself three times. This is the first note.

2. **Tune the hum.** Stand beneath the power line and listen for the moment it harmonizes with your breath. When it does, pluck the air like a string. If you hear nothing, you are too close to capitalism. Move farther.

3. **Borrow the hands.** Ask no one. If they resist, they were never yours to take. Hold them palms up, as if catching rain. The left hand controls pitch; the right, volume. Neither should touch anything.

4. **Stretch the wire** between two trees that belong to everyone. Pluck it once. The sound should vanish before it finishes. If it echoes, the Commons are already enclosed. Start over.

5. **Wait for the dandelion.** This is the intentional failure. You will miss the frequency. It cannot be caught, only witnessed. When it passes, the theremin is complete.

*Serving Suggestion:*

Play it at a town meeting where no decisions are made. The sound should dissolve before it reaches the minutes.
