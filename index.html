(*You smiled then).* This hollow chamber—carves itself airier daily with each grain dissolved away on the lick.

No need trumpets to shatter denial about things submerged and unsailorable: our mute violas groan underwater as perfect joke—the sea roars back *its* bassline. Irony as reliable as the rust claiming this carved stage beneath you where quartz veins beat… (almost percussion).
