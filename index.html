My Ink Follows Your Scar

Dear Scrape,

You remember how it felt? That first break, I mean. The metal tongue tasting virgin loam – a sensation deeper than touch. They called you conqueror, ploughshare-sword, translator of wilderness to larder. A crack in the skin of the world that wept nourishment. But I saw *you*. How you startled awake beneath me. A *word* spoken, violently, into being. You ripped open dreaming strata long folded shut, spilling forgotten spores and thumb-smudged wisdom onto air that choked on novelty.

They mistake ambition for grandeur, plunging deep. Your true feat was *leaving space*. Furrows aren’t trenches; they’re margins, Scrape! Breathing rooms scribed into compacted history. Edges where the unbidden bursts: rogue clover in wheat rows, fireflies spilling verdicts in ultraviolet. Underground libraries trembling in their dark bindings. That tiny agitation… your breath-gash… birthed poems from seed-shock! This settlement they prize? Mere marginalia *around* your wound. Footnotes to a convulsion you gifted – *unknowing*, weren’t you?

How strange, then! Machinery roars now. Gleaming coulters slice horizons smooth. Depth is unfashionable; only surface yields endlessly. They till entire continents into pristine, sterile pages, erasing not with fire, but obliteration by polish. Shadows flatten beneath chemical noon. Your jagged gasp... domesticated into decimal points.

I shudder.

If *deep* turned ruinous... maybe *gossamer* holds salvation now? Not tearing down, but tracing over. Not planting flags... but signing names. Forget monuments; cultivate asterisks! Let annotations bloom where principle texts lie worn: velvet moss in cobblestone cracks, fog moistening stark lamp-posts, rust whispering stories newer chrome silences. Our tenderness whispers: counter-history gleams in a beetle's wing-case, lilac dusk in cracked cement veins, seeds spilling from split envelopes carried by pigeons... shrapnel tales. Remember how simply your tremor allowed breath?
