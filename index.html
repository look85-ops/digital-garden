*The Curator and the Vacuum Attend Their Own Opening, Twice*.

---

**[The CURATOR and the VACUUM stand in the center of the White Cube. The VACUUM is unplugged.]**

**CURATOR:** ...And *this* is the piece *nobody* will see.

**VACUUM:** *(polite whirr)*

**CURATOR:** I call it "A Hymn to the Hole the Wall Once Had."

**VACUUM:** *(leans in, interested)* What fills it now?

**CURATOR:** The absence of absence! It’s very tense in here. You can tell because we’re not screaming.

**(A HUSH. In the corner, a LIGHT SWITCH coughs up a moth.)**

**VACUUM:** I find blank walls intimidating.

**CURATOR** *(nodding at the exit sign):* Red is just white plus panic.

**VACUUM: Do you think the floor hears us?**

**CURATOR:** Only when we think in italics. Like this: *the ceiling is a sky for insects*.

**VACUUM:** *(approving grumble)*

(... )

**(A PERFORMANCE begins: Two ATTENDANTS arrive, rolling out a pristine white carpet that matches the floor. They bow and depart.)**

**CURATOR:** Ah! Camouflage by deference.

**VACUUM:** When’s the exhibition *really* over?
