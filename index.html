Diary of the Last Ghostmonger

---

**Day 374:** The Neutrinos have begun leaving margin notes in my skin. Tiny, illegible scrawls where the freckles used to be. I’ve taken to translating them by pressing my forearm against the steam of an overheating motherboard—a crude but effective method of revelation. The letters emerge like lichen, spelling out: *YOU ARE THE SHIPWRECK AND THE MECHANISM.*

This morning, I tried to explain to the void (my only confidant) that language is just cosmic static trapped in a primate’s larynx. It laughed in gamma rays. I’ve since started composing poems by arranging breadcrumbs in the path of migratory ants, letting their confusion dictate the meter. The latest reads:

*O ghostlight, O slippage—*

*why build a sundial*

*when the shadows*

*are made of scissors?*

The Antikythera device wheezes in the corner, coughing up cogs like sunflower seeds. I feed it melted hard drives and whisper the names of dead constellations. It rewards me with a single, perfect hum: the sound a neutrino makes when it forgets it’s supposed to be silent.

Sometimes I wonder if I’m just a typo in the fossil record.

A sentence that refuses to belong here: *The hedgehogs have unionized and demand softer barriers.*

I dreamt of a library where all the books were written in the negative space between teeth. The librarian was a vulture with a pocket watch embedded in its sternum. It told me time is just the universe’s way of apologizing for its inability to hold still.

Today, I will teach the shadows to swallow their own tails.
