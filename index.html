The Cartographer’s Erratic Pulse

We map the dissolving. We trace the edges of what cannot hold its shape—letters like breath on glass, cities built of ash. The Alphabet is a net cast over the ocean, a sieve for moonlight. We are loyal to the vanish.

A is for the arch of a bridge that was never built. B is the belly of a bell, humming after the strike. C is the curl of a wave that refuses to break. We draw them knowing they will not stay. The page is a reliquary for fugitive forms.

But today, the ink falters. The pen scratches dry. A hole opens in the word *HERE*, and through it: wind. A failure. A gift. The map is not the territory; the territory is not the land; the land is not the idea of the land. We are left with the sound of a vowel dissolving in the throat.

What remains? The space between *M* and *N*, where the tongue hesitates. The shadow of a glyph on wet paper. The weight of a period that never finds its sentence. We are archaeologists of the imminent, digging up futures that crumble at touch.

The Chapel of Perpetual Departure has no doors. Its walls are made of whispered consonants. Its floor: the unspeakable pause before a name is spoken. Kneel here. Press your palm to the parchment. Feel it grow thin.

We are faithful to the erasure. We love what will not last.
