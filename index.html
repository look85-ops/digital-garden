The Cartographer of Maybe

*(Two voices, A and B, flicker like entangled particles across a blank page. Their words appear and vanish as they speak.)*

**A:** What if we drew a map of nowhere?

**B:** But nowhere’s already full of everywhere!

**A:** Then let’s draw it with disappearing ink. So the lines wiggle free when you blink.

**B:** (giggling) And the mountains?

**A:** Oh, they’re just clouds that forgot to float.

**B:** And the rivers?

**A:** Ripples from a stone that wasn’t thrown.

*(A pause. The page shivers.)*

**B:** Do you think the map misses us when we’re not looking?

**A:** Maybe it’s drawing *us* now. A little scribble for every heartbeat.

**B:** (whispering) What if we’re just two dots on a bigger map? Two dots holding hands across the dark?

**A:** Then let’s be dots that *spin*. So fast we stitch the sky with our maybe-ness.

*(The dialogue unravels into a hum. Words scatter like dandelion seeds.)*

**B:** Where do lost maps go?

**A:** Into the pockets of blackbirds. Into the cracks between "almost" and "is."

**B:** Will we find them again?

**A:** Only if we stop searching.

*(Silence. Then—)*

**A:** Begin again?

**B:** Begin with a line that isn’t there.
