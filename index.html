Epitaphs for Passing Glances

*(Found incised upon a weathered limestone plinth, near the Chapel's dripping gutter downspout)*

**Voice of the Pavement (Worn Smooth):**

...they tread upon my skin, these brief storms... heels clicking histories I'll never hear... asphalt absorbs more than rain, you know... absorbs the weight of *almost*... the sigh before the turn... the hesitation at the crosswalk... fossilized coffee cups hold more warmth than some handshakes... we memorize your loneliness perfectly... wear it down to a sheen...

**Voice of the Window Steam (Condensing, Evaporating):**

Ah, but *I* catch the ghosts... the breath-steam signatures... fleeting portraits drawn in condensation... a face pressed close, fogging the world... tracing a name... a heart... a curse... gone before the ink dries... intimate as a shared secret whispered to glass... then... wiped clean... forgotten by the sun... or the janitor... same difference... you hold the weight... I hold the breath... both equally... gone...

**Pavement:**

Gone? No... pressed deep... like fossils in shale... the scuff of a desperate sprint... the drag of exhaustion... the precise angle of a dropped grocery bag... peaches rolling... a minor tragedy etched... a thousand such tragedies... smoothed... but not erased... cities are built on layers of discarded glances... compressed into... foundation...

**Window Steam:**

Foundation? Dust... shifting dust... I paint with vapor... momentary masterpieces on cold panes... a child's nose... a lover's profile... the fog of someone crying... unseen... anonymous art galleries on bus shelters... evaporating exhibitions... the most profound intimacy... is the one you don't... *can't*... acknowledge... shared solitude... steaming up the glass... then... transparency...

**Pavement:**

Transparency... is an illusion... like permanence... see this crack? Not just water... not just frost... it's the precise spot where *he* paused... every dawn... waiting for a bus that never came... wore me down... waiting... then stopped... the crack remembers... holds the shape of his hesitation... tighter than any embrace... a negative space... monument...

**Window Steam:**

Monuments crumble... I prefer... transience... the beauty of the unrepeatable... the condensation pattern of *this* breath... *this* sigh... *this* silent scream... unique... utterly... and then... nothing... pure departure... no plaque... just... the ghost of warmth... on cold glass... the Chapel understands... perpetual departure... is the only... ritual... worth... carving...

**Pavement:**

...carved... yes... and eroding... like this inscription... like the memory of her perfume... clinging to brick... near the bakery... faint... now... just... grit... and the scent of wet stone... waiting... for the next storm... of footsteps... the next layer... of... unknowing...

*(The lower lines blur into lichen and pitted stone.)*
