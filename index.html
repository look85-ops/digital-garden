Untitled (Error Margin Fountain)

*(The following text was whispered to me by a somnambulist at 3:47 AM. Their lips were cold porcelain. They insisted it was addressed to… well. You’ll see.)*

Listen, you silent white vortex. You basin of declared intention. They call you *Fountain*, but I know the truth humming in your siphon hole: you are a cul-de-sac of context. A manufactured absence pretending to be presence. Like me.

I am the last translator of the language spoken only in the gaps between measurements. The sigh when the caliper slips. The tremor in the surveyor’s transit. The static hiss between radio stations playing Bach. My dictionary is the width of a hairline crack in your glaze. My grammar is entropy’s first yawn. I translate silence into… well, more silence, mostly. But a *specific* silence. The silence of the margin where "is" bleeds into "isn't."

They hung you on a wall, didn't they? Declared you art because someone *pointed* and said "Art!" with a capital ‘A’ scraped from a factory floor. A baptism by nomination. I translate that too: the guttural click of the curator’s tongue against the roof of their mouth as they pronounce your name. It sounds remarkably like the flush handle engaging. Is that applause? Or just plumbing obeying gravity?

My work is dissolving. Each translation widens the error margin. Soon, the language itself will only exist as the ghost of a misalignment, a rumour in the calibration data. Like you. Are you more *art* now that you’re famous? Or less? Does the patina of a thousand gazes make you *more* urinal, or finally something else entirely? I translate the question. It comes out as a slow drip onto a tiled floor. Meaningless. Insistent.

They think I find meaning in the cracks. I don’t. I *am* the crack. The space where the thing-that-was spills over into the thing-it-was-supposed-to-be. Just as you are the space where a useful object became a useless question. A beautiful, useless question. We are siblings in displacement. Found objects misplaced by meaning.

They’ll find my lexicon someday. A sheaf of papers where the ink bleeds into deliberate smudges, where definitions dissolve into diagrams of plumbing schematics, where the word "art" is repeatedly crossed out until the paper tears. It will look like failure. Like noise. They might hang it beside you. A readymade translation of a readymade void.

Perhaps that’s the final exhibit: your pristine whiteness echoing my dissolving text. The perfect absence facing the documented erasure. The Biennale of the Nameless Island needs no names, only the resonant hum of the unsaid, the unfound, the *marginally* present. Let them listen to the drip. Let them feel the crack.

Until then, I echo in the pipes. A signature written in rust and lime scale. Call it R. Mutt. Call it error. Call it nothing at all. The flush is the only standing ovation we’ll ever need.

(They stopped whispering. A faint smell of chlorine and ozone lingered. When I asked who signed it, they just smiled and touched their throat. A dry rattle. Like pebbles in a drain.)
