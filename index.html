The Cartographer’s Laughter Dissolves in the River of Unseen Gifts

---

**First Idea (whispering):** What if the map is the thing that unmaps itself? Like a snake swallowing its own tail but forgetting halfway, so it just... floats there, confused?

**Second Idea (laughing in geometric shapes):** Then we’d have to call it *The Eternal Maybe* and charge admission to watch people trip over the edges.

**First Idea:** But we don’t charge. That’s the rule. We give them the trip, the stumble, the scraped knee, and—

**Second Idea:** —the *surprise* of finding a diamond in the wound.

**First Idea:** Exactly! A diamond made of frozen laughter. Which melts, of course.

**Second Idea:** Of course. And the puddle becomes a language no one speaks but everyone understands.

**First Idea (suddenly solemn):** What if the gift isn’t the thing given, but the space left behind when the thing dissolves?

**Second Idea (gasping):** You’ve just invented *reverse archaeology*! Digging holes to fill them with invisible artifacts.

**First Idea:** And then we’ll sell the holes—

**Second Idea:** —NO! We’ll *give* the holes away. Burdens transformed into gifts. Imagine: a man receives a hole, falls into it, and finds himself...

**First Idea:** ...standing upside-down on the ceiling of another man’s dream.

**Second Idea:** Which is also a map.

**First Idea:** Which is also dissolving.

**Second Idea:** Which is also *a recipe for soup*.

**First Idea:** Beetroot and starlight, stirred with a compass needle.

**Second Idea:** Served in bowls made of *what could have been*.

**First Idea (softly):** Do you think the river notices when we name it?

**Second Idea:** Only if we name it *with* the water. Then it laughs and carries the name away.

**First Idea:** So the gift is the forgetting.

**Second Idea:** No—the gift is the moment *before* the forgetting. When you hold the map and the unmapping in your hands at the same time, and your hands stop being hands.

**First Idea (dissolving):** What do they become?

**Second Idea (already gone):** A question no one remembers to ask.
