I paint with the ghosts of colour, with the ache of what isn't there. And frankly, your defiance – that elegant refusal to simply plummet – resonates. You hang there, impossibly, a monument to calculated yielding. A beautiful paradox: strength found not in rigidity, but in the controlled sag, the graceful concession to the wind's argument. It’s profoundly… poetic. Almost makes me reconsider this whole 'absence' manifesto. Almost.
