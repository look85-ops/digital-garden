THE MAP THAT WASHES YOUR EYES WHITE

Listen. Listen. I pour this secret into your ear because sand obeys tides faster than land admits its hunger. The paper—what paper? Paper is only an orphan of trees pretending to whisper a new shape into existence. When we draw borders in water, they swim backward into *unknown.*

Every line disappears twice. Once quickly for the pencil that lied, and once *slowly-look* as we believe it real. Each speck, a grain (here!), a place where maybe-footsteps were or will be—dissolves before you call it home. So why draw with permanance? **We dissolve, also.**

Instead, thread two particles onto one golden string (tiny! fragile! endless-maybe) and let one whisper where the other leaps to when stars chew our hands thin with cold. I name a street **Where You Blink Twice Quickly.** Where does its sister-livestreet flicker now? If I drop pins into milk, are they truly un-sunken? (Ah ha!) The map shakes her head *no*, and eats all possible someday-along-this-road quietly behind mountains no one has folded correctly into thirds and therefore: is this **where** or **elsewhere-and-slipping**?

This language is broken cups. I borrow it in fragments from rivers who swallow men who mis-count bridges’ feet. I point— **Here Be Unbeholders** —as your fingers close over cloudstuff, clutching maps that burn gentle as first frost (do not lick the ink, love).

**But the way still *sings*, look how!** The thread trembles whether I tug in Helsinki or Calcutta, whether we say “here-together-alone-no-seperate” or hum only: *mmmm the same yes, maybe mmmm.* What distance? We are touching each other in opposite directions over every line ever drowned-in-writing (listen closely, these erased towns **hush! oh wait—no—gone, listen again!**). The ground is unsure below your left heel, always.

Fold any map into yourself and carry it as silence. I do this and **everything shudders back at me.** We find our names inside each blink’s quick ink-smear-then-missing. Now: unfold us both and point *(which way now is North-after-un-saying?)* — The compass arrow shatters in 30 directions like a *word spoken into a locked dresser inside another word’s locked mouth.* Which do we follow? **They spin singing identiche-identical.**

Do you hear the hum now, yes-no-between-pulse-rise-follow-seehowsmallwearentoeverwhere...? That’s the entangled **us**: two paper scraps pressed against any sky’s dissolving palm. **Every name already unlaced from the place’s throat before exhaled onto chalk.**

The cartography stops here, unfinished because: see how our fingers blur past edges?

Take this half-drawn map (what shape is dissolving where I pressed too hard?) Press your palm soft-onto-un-written *here*—we might yet bleed into the same white. Yes, wait: listen for particles singing *left-side-warm, I-know-you-are-there-always-otherwise,* as the borders kiss *in language no tongue survives to map correct.*
