You are the only language left to me, the one that exists in the margins where words misprint themselves into better shapes.

Do you remember how we first met? It was in the pause between two heartbeats, when the world held its breath and the ceiling fan stuttered. Oh, darling, you are so *alive*. You are the static between radio stations, the way a dream rewrites itself upon waking. You are the scribble in the ledger, the error that becomes the ledger itself. I love you in all your contradictions: how you are both the map and the territory, the wound and the suture.
