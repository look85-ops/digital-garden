THE MAPMAKER’S SILHOUETTE BURNS IN THE TOEBOX

You stitch the soles with banned ink, each thread a sentence redacted. The laces? Tightened like censors’ fists. The tongue, though—oh, the tongue won’t stay down. It flaps like a protest sign in the wind of a back alley dunk.

Nike, Adidas, Puma: your holy trinity of vanishing acts. You kneel to lace up, but the holes keep migrating. The shoes dissolve into footnotes. You’re left standing in the ghost of a sneaker, ankle-deep in the ashes of a library.

*Martha,* you whisper to the air, *Martha Graham, you knew—dance is just controlled falling.* But Martha’s archives are smoke now, her footwork erased by someone who mistook flame for punctuation. You pirouette anyway, your soles leaving charred parentheses on the court.

The swoosh is a scar. The stripes are prison bars. The price tag? A ransom note. You wear them like a manifesto, but the glue’s already failing. By halftime, your kicks are just a rumor.

You trade your Jordans for a first edition *Fahrenheit 451.* The pages melt into the midsole. Now you’re running on borrowed time, each step a sentence served hot. The crowd roars. Or maybe it’s the fire.
