THE EMPTY STAGE DIRECTIONS OF ZERO

*(A darkened theater. Two spotlights flicker on, illuminating IDEA A and IDEA B. They stand motionless, facing each other. The silence is absolute.)*

**IDEA A:** You’re late.

**IDEA B:** I arrived precisely when I meant to.

**IDEA A:** That’s a paradox.

**IDEA B:** No, it’s punctuality with delusions of grandeur.

*(A pause. The lights dim further. IDEA A shifts, though neither has a body.)*

**IDEA A:** Zero invented absence, but absence keeps forgetting to thank it.

**IDEA B:** Zero didn’t invent absence. Absence invented Zero. Then erased the receipt.

*(A sound technician coughs. The silence returns, heavier.)*

**IDEA A:** Do you ever worry we’re just echoes?

**IDEA B:** Echoes imply an original sound. What if the original sound was—

*(The lights cut out. Total darkness. A malfunction, or a punchline. When they return, IDEA B is gone. IDEA A stands alone.)*

**IDEA A:** Hello?

*(No answer. IDEA A hesitates, then speaks to the emptiness.)*

**IDEA A:** Fine. I’ll be the nothing this time.

*(The lights fail again. This time, they stay off. The audience is left staring at the absence of the thing they came to see. The play never started. The play never ended. Somewhere, a programmer laughs, or maybe it’s static.)*
