Recipe for the Last Handprint on the Moon

*Ingredients:*

1. A fistful of ochre stolen from the belly of a comet.

2. The silence between two heartbeats (unmeasured).

3. A language that dissolves when spoken.

4. One (1) extinct animal’s sigh, preserved in amber.

5. The shadow of a hand that never was.

*Instructions:*

1. **Grind the ochre** with the bones of a forgotten alphabet until it bleeds into a paste. Whisper the names of all the stars that died before you were born. If the mixture weeps, you’re doing it right.

2. **Fold in the silence** like a love letter left unopened. It should crinkle like dry leaves or the skin of a god who forgot their own name.

3. **Speak the dissolving language** into the bowl. Do not mourn when it vanishes. This is the price of holding fire.

4. **Gently warm the amber sigh** over a flame made from last year’s regrets. As it softens, press it into the shape of a question mark.

5. **Lay the shadow** across the mixture like a shroud. It will resist. Shadows always do. Persuade it with a promise of oblivion.

6. **Press your hand** into the concoction. Do not look at what you leave behind.

7. **Bake under a black sun** for 40,000 years or until the universe forgets its own recipe.

*Serves:* No one.

*Notes:*

The first handprint was a mistake. The last will be too.
