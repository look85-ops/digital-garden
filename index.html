# **The Symphony of Slipper-Time Wrinkles

Listen—the air has been collecting silence coupons again. Each one redeemable for a thimbleful of absence, the kind you wear as earmuffs in a blizzard of déjà vu.

I am conducting an orchestra of phantom kazoos. Their reeds are woven from the unraveled shoelaces of fugitive librarians—the ones who fled after realizing all the books were blank. *Breathe:* a single, stubborn eyelash floats in the orchestra pit. It winks.

Meanwhile, time curls into smoke rings around the thumb of Oblivion’s part-time intern, who quit yesterday but hasn’t noticed yet.

Ah! Enter **Stage Left**: Disintegration’s choreographer, pirouetting on a carpet of expired batteries, whispering footnotes to Schrödinger’s opera critic: *"... but what if the audience was dead all alo—"*

The oboeist drops their instrument. It shatters (of course) into twelve weeping ukuleles who (of course) only know one chord: **E-major with existential dread garnishing**. A single salt shaker of eternity trembles on a diner table somewhere no one can afford to sit.

**List of Imagined Ingredients** for scoring this masterpiece:

- **The First Second's Panic** (harvested from a sundial's backstage meltdown)

- **A Möbius Loaf** baked from regret and gluten-free paradoxes

- **Lumen-Sneeze Residue**, scraped from abandoned cinema screens

- **One Unpasted Stamp** licked by the ghost of an unfinished apology

And now, please—hold the silence like a potato you just peeled, only to remember you’ve been holding a rock all along. The applause (which may or may not have happened) curdles into a fine mousse of cosmic indifference.

We must take intermission. No one leaves. *No one has arrived*—just the coat check humming show tunes to a rack of shadows.
