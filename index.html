inventions a dialogue between two ideas t=1.16

*You Stole the Ink from My Dreams*

You were there when the press groaned its first wet syllable into the world. You pressed your ear to the vellum and whispered back. Don’t deny it—the ink smudges on your fingertips are mine.

**Idea 1:** *The map is a wound.*

**Idea 2:** *The wound is a river.*

You trace the borders, but they bleed. Every latitude a scar, every longitude a suture. You say this is how we hold a thing that won’t be held. I say you’re stitching the sky to the ground with a needle made of silence.

**Idea 2:** *The river is a tongue.*

**Idea 1:** *The tongue is a relic.*

You licked the glue from the binding, didn’t you? Tasted the gilded edges like they were promises. Now the words are dissolving in your mouth, and you’re trying to spit them back onto the page. Too late. The letters are swimming in your veins.

**Idea 1:** *The relic is a mirror.*

**Idea 2:** *The mirror is a thief.*

You stole my reflection and called it yours. I see you wearing my face in the glass, mouthing the words I haven’t written yet. The Bible wept when you touched it. Each drop of ink a tiny apocalypse.

**Idea 2:** *The thief is a cartographer.*

**Idea 1:** *The cartographer is a ghost.*

You drew the lines, but the land refused you. Now you wander the margins, a rumor in your own atlas. The paper remembers your hands, though. It folds itself into mountains where your fingerprints used to be.

**Idea 1:** *The ghost is a question.*

**Idea 2:** *The question is a knife.*

You cut the pages loose, and now they float like feathers. You say they’ll land where they’re meant to. I say you’ve murdered the alphabet. Look—the *A* is drowning. The *Z* is a noose.

You were there. You were always there.
