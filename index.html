MANIFESTO FOR THE PAINTING THAT PAINTS ITSELF BACK INTO THE CAN

We renounce saturation. We extinguish the RGB riots. Our palette is the echo of a dial tone, the static between elections, the white space around a headline screaming nothing into your retina. [*A paintbrush dissolves mid-stroke, leaving only the idea of bristles.*]

The canvas is not blank. It is *unremembered*. We trade pigment for pause. Where others flood, we excavate. A single pixel flicks on—then off—and in that void, you glimpse the ghost script of everything you’ve ever scrolled past. **This is art for the age of collective amnesia.** For the news cycle that chews stories into vowels and spits them out as weather alerts. *You* are the fresco. *Your attention span is the patina.*

Consider this: We built cathedrals to forgetting before we knew the word “algorithmic feed.” Now we chisel hymns into silence. The Chapel of Perpetual Departure has no walls. Its doorways are exits disguised as openings. Every masterpiece looks a little worse on your third screen. Ask yourself why they make erasers so small when mistake comes in bulk. Wait—don’t. The question just expired.

Technique: Apply absence with glacial precision. Let fatigue be your varnish. Let the piece complete itself in the viewer’s nervous *swipe left* twitch. If colors were the question, erasure is the answer scrawled in disappearing ink. [*A drop of paint evaporates before reaching the floor, singing in a key only appliances understand.*] By which I mean: the future may come neon, but history dries matte.

Our most loyal critic? The loading spinner. The masterpiece that never buffered into existence won’t disappoint you. A pause is your participation trophy. A void knows how you vote. We do *not* paint the absence of connection; the wires hum that epilogue without us. Stop asking who stole the sunset. Try remembering who *didn’t* post it.

And when they demand we apologize for the empty gallery, tell them this:

**Blankness is not surrender. It is the broadcast tuned sharp enough to cut.**

[*The manifesto folds origami-style into a headline none of you

will read tomorrow.*]
