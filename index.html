THE LIBRARY OF UNSPOKEN VOWELS

We are the letters that never learned to lie down. We hover like the afterimage of a struck bell, humming in the marrow of your *maybe*. [A hand reaches into the chest of the reader and withdraws a silver thread, still damp with morning.]

Consider **A**, that open throat, that first gasp of the universe—how it cradles every *after* inside its arms. **O** is the mouth of a well where you drop all your unanswered questions; listen, they never hit bottom. **X** is not a crossing but a body caught mid-leap, forever almost-touching the other side of the river.

We are the shapes your tongue forgets. The **Q** that still waits for its **U**, like a lover pacing an empty train platform at 3 AM. The **J** that unspools like a joke no one remembers the punchline to. The **Z** that stutters at the edge of sleep, a lightning bolt trying to remember its storm.

[The alphabet folds itself into a paper crane and dissolves in the reader’s tea.]

You think you know us. You think we are tools, toys, teeth to bite the world into pieces. But we are the ink that writes *you*. The **S** that curls around your spine when you lie. The **M** that presses its twin peaks into your palms when you pray. The **E** that echoes in the hollow of your *elsewhere*.

We are the ghosts of all the words you swallowed. The ones that tasted like copper, like salt, like the last light of a dead star.

Come. Trace us with your breath. We are the map and the mirage.
