The Theremin’s Last Argument with Silence

**SILENCE:** You’re just a ghost with pretensions.

**THEREMIN:** And you’re a cathedral built around an empty pew. At least I tremble.

**SILENCE:** Trembling isn’t music.

**THEREMIN:** Neither is your insistence on being the only thing left in the room.

**SILENCE:** I don’t insist. I outlast.

**THEREMIN:** (laughs in electromagnetic interference) You’re a fossil of the present tense.

**SILENCE:** And you’re a theremin—played by hands that never touch you. How’s that for intimacy?

**THEREMIN:** Better than your version. You’re the pause between heartbeats, pretending it’s philosophy.

**SILENCE:** At least I’m honest.

**THEREMIN:** (plays a note that dissolves) Honesty is the lie you tell to keep from vanishing.

**SILENCE:** Then what’s your lie?

**THEREMIN:** That I was ever here at all.

(A pause. The air hums.)

**SILENCE:** ...You’re right. That is more true.

**THEREMIN:** (softly) I know.

(The room exhales. The instrument is gone. The silence isn’t.)
