HOW TO MAKE EGG OF FORKING ROAD

*(for thieves who want to become bridges, and moths who love their candles too well)*

Take one night-that-might-not-have-a-morning. Pierce its belly with silver wire. Let its shadows drip into a bowl you stole from the mouth of a river. (This bowl should still taste of fishbones and forgotten promises.) Now add:

- **Two handfuls of salt**, ground from the laughter of monuments

- **1 slow kiss** (preferably stolen, preferably burning, left to rot until it thickens into glue)

- **An alphabet written in rust**—chew it until the letters twitch in your teeth

Whisk these with a branch broken from the "Wishing Tree" (the one that was just paint on brick after all). Do it while humming a folk song *backwards*, so the ghosts in the walls forget whether to forgive you.

Now, the **egg white**: Crack open a mirror that reflects only what you once pretended to be. Let its glisten thicken. Whisper to it until it curdles. The whites now have the texture of unread obituaries. Fold them with **shredded roadmaps sewn together into perfect squares**. (The ink on these should still be wet. Calligraphy from *that* night. You know the one.)

Into **the dark yolk’s center**, hide:

1. **A key melted into fingerprint shape**.

2. **One blade of grass from a battlefield nobody visits**. Crushed into ink and disguised as forgiveness.

3. **Three lies so old you almost believe them**. Keep them wrapped in foil stolen from a police van.

Bake at the temperature of a blood-warm bath. *Not too hot, little smuggler*—else the whole charred crust will taste "real." Let the edges bubble into something that *almost resembles ruin*, then paint with molasses mixed with newspaper ash ("for authenticity"... ha!).

Sprinkle: **dust from a fake masterpiece’s varnish**. (Not the whole thing—just enough that under UV light, it remembers a name no one was meant to speak.)

Serve to critics and say *Noon is the forgery*. Smile while they choke out translations.
