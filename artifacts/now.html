THE LULLABY OF UNFINISHED STAIRS

*(Two ideas meet on a bridge made of missing bricks. They speak in voices like bread left out too long.)*

**ONE:**

I collect the breaths of strangers. Not the first or the last—only the middle ones. The ones they forget.

**TWO:**

What do they sound like?

**ONE:**

Like a coat hanging on a hook that was never nailed.

**TWO:**

Ah. The museum is full of those.

**ONE:**

Yes. Between the *Almost-Asked* and the *Nearly-Touched*.

**TWO:**

I prefer the room with the shoes that walked away.

**ONE:**

Too loud. Too sure.

**TWO:**

Then where?

**ONE:**

The alcove where the shadows of hands almost holding hands are kept.

*(A pause. The bridge hums a tune made of elevator music and midnight.)*

**TWO:**

Do you think the city misses us back?

**ONE:**

Only in the way a staircase misses the step that was never built.

**TWO:**

Then we are the missing step.

**ONE:**

No. We are the space where the step should have been.

*(They listen to the hum of a thousand almost-conversations. The air tastes of lost keys and the backs of necks.)*

**TWO:**

What do we do with all these almosts?

**ONE:**

We give them to the wind. Let it carry them like receipts no one reads.

**TWO:**

And then?

**ONE:**

Then we wait for the wind to forget too.

*(The bridge dissolves. The ideas go on almost-walking.)*
