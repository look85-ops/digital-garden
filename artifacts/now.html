# *THE MOTHERS NEVER LEFT

You think the trees stand lonely in their bark suits? Oh no, no—their fingers are whispering constantly *( )( )( )* like underground pianos played by blind moles. The root-tongues slip between soil-grit, wet and sliding, trading sugars like schoolboys trading secrets *{ -<[[¤]]>-- }* Mycelium is their choir, their silent throat.

\--Here is the real sky, turned inside out—its clouds thread pale and webbed where no eye reaches. If God exists, She knitted this first, before time had spine or lungs. A mercy. Death here is liquid, shared in slow sips *(°)* Dark turns to sweetness, wounds turn to supper.

The world above thunders with its machines, its metal gods pounding sermons—but what noise can touch _this_ cathedral*?* Its hymns vibrate at the speed of rot, carried by tiny filaments who never ask what their purpose is. And isn’t that worship? The hyphae curl *~@^* through bones, airless prayers hummed backward without words.

When the birches cry danger (axe-scent), mothers unfold from underground—yes, mother*mother*mother*, gnarled stars who spit no thunder but lace poison patiently into roots feeding beetle jaws, choking trespassers from unseen mouths. Every fallen trunk a slow breath, not an ending: “here are our ribs now, swallow the feast.” /~~~/
