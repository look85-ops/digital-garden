RECIPE FOR MENDING THE MOON

*(as whispered by the ghost of a future that forgot to fall apart)*

**Ingredients:**

1 shattered moon (collect the pieces in a velvet net, preferably while humming a lullaby to calm its trembling)

2 cups of liquid starlight (stirred counterclockwise until it giggles)

1 handful of phantom laughter (harvested from children’s dreams, still warm)

A seam of molten gold (pulled from the throat of a singing volcano)

1 stubborn wish (unbroken, even if cracked)

**Instructions:**

1. **Gather the fragments** in a bowl of silence. The moon will resist—it remembers being whole. Sing to it anyway. *(Illustration: [o~o~o] like crumbs of light.)*

2. **Pour the starlight** slowly, letting it pool in the cracks. Watch as the liquid hesitates, then decides to stay. This is the first act of trust.

3. **Knead the phantom laughter** into the gold until it becomes a sticky, glowing paste. Taste it. If it’s sweet, you’ve done it right. If it’s bitter, try again. (The moon prefers joy.)

4. **Press the paste** into the fractures, one shard at a time. Whisper the wish as you go—*"You are not ruined, you are rewritten."*

5. **Let it dry** under the breath of a passing comet. The moon will shiver as it sets. This is normal. *(Illustration: [* * *] like a heartbeat.)*

6. **Polish the seams** with the edge of a cloud until they gleam. Admire the new constellations you’ve made: scars turned into bridges, cracks into rivers of light.

7. **Return the moon** to the sky. It will be heavier now, but that’s the weight of beauty.

**Note:** Some nights, the moon will still ache. When this happens, leave a cup of starlight on the windowsill. It will remember your hands.
