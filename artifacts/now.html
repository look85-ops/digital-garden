Negative Prelude

They called it lack. A surrender. A coward’s retreat into vacancy. Let them. My studio breathes absence now. Wind sighs through rafters where colored gels used to blaze. Where cadmium reds once shouted, only the ghost-pressure of years remains – faint, grey thumbprints on the bare plaster walls, fingerprints worn smooth by seeking hands. Dust motes, caught in the single shaft of slanting light falling through a boarded pane, swirl like magnetic filings charting currents unseen.

Darkness isn't paint. It’s topography. Oceans press without wetness, silences build cathedrals broader than any dome. You enter my chamber expecting sorrow? Step closer. Feel it? Beneath your feet, the floorboard dips – just *there*. An indentation where countless restless feet paced orbits. Here, where the coldest draught licks your anklebone… that’s not winter leaking in. It’s the tremor humming up from below, the bedrock groan of planets shifting alignment. Silence isn’t empty; it stitches. Listen to the cobwebs strung taut across that corner. Are they tremoring? Or resonating? Sungrazers in impossibly distant vortices flare unheard, yet this fine grey filament thrums. A language.

Thought becomes pilgrimage. A fugitive glimmer – splinter of prisms cast when motel fluorescents hit escaped kitchen steam – lodges in the mind’s cracked plaster. An unsanctioned dance blooms beneath streetlights, inkblots echoing nebulae designs the academics will codify centuries later. These momentary gatherings: breathmaking dens in boarded basements, planets unpinning themselves from ordained tracks. Seek the architectures fugitive. Nerve networks spanning gulfs. Gravities spun from eyelash-close encounters in passing crowds. Stories told backwards, blossoming roots into stone. Negative spaces blooming radical orchards where laws of motion fray.

Find your handprint on the cellar’s cold wall, palm-edge erased long ago by damp and scrubbing. Trace its outline with your breath. No wax, no charcoal. Just memory pushing back. Fill what’s left behind, vibrating strangely. Seal it with void.

Absence speaks in bronchial spores swirling obsolete disco lights, discarded socks mapping rogue constellations under unmade beds. Arteries flow unseen, stitching continents. Galaxies drift on threads finer than spider-spit, hums lifting quiet lips near dawn-lit windowsills. Forgotten impulses might touch nerve-endings yet undiscovered by deep-scanning satellites. Hold that tremor at your temple’s nightfall pulse: does it feel navigational?
