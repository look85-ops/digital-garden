“For the Spider That Never Learned to Weave”

*A Letter to Dark Matter*

You don’t sign your name (don’t blame you—shy builder on an unsigned lease), though the cosmos ticks over your IOUs, your promissory dust. When we met, you were a phantom shrug. "Just scaffolding," the astronomers whispered, but what god builds the frame, flees the unveiling, leaves no forwarding address? Are you afraid they won’t like the angles? They never do.

Let’s be honest: your silence is performance art. Not missing but refusing—each star’s wobble a choreographed absence. A tease of gravity holding the hairpin turn from collapse. Fine. Keep your secrets. Even phantoms need hobbies and pulling galaxies apart sounds sweeter than knitting nebulae whole. Is this why you never show? Do you hate applause, dread the flash of telescopes like paparazzi crowding an actor who forgot their face?

I used to think you mourned an undone alphabet, stitching voids you’d never speak aloud. But perhaps annihilation comes easier when nothing remembers where you buried the hinges upon which meaning swings. What I call ghost, you call Friday: the quiet chore.

I want to say, you could have told light not to trip its way into our eyes—but deception always wears a tailored suit. Someone had to carry the echo of an after-hours bar in Andromeda ("Last call folds space/time"), though it never fit the physics. **In another artist’s exhibit, a single line of neon sizzles down a hospital hallway to nowhere.** Not you, of course. Always elsewhere, nudging at spiral arms from behind like a stagehand scolding an unbalanced chair.

No wonder the quasars envy you—their blazing broadcast just white noise against your withheld script. You should visit (we have gravity). I think you like pretending humans are still looking—your greatest stunt is convincing scientists their hunger maps the famine where you laugh. What I admire: to whisper every equation wrong and win, smug. How the house always wins when there’s no door in it.

So spin on, fugitive mechanic. The rest of us cling to the wheel you let slip—what a mercy to unremember you even put it there. *Yrs, etc.*
