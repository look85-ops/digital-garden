Discourse to the Inherited Interruption

*(Exhibit No. 84, Wing of Spectral Catalysts, Academy of Forgotten Gestures)*

Dear Persistence,

Forgive the messy scratch of ink – iron-gall, regrettably common – upon this paper thin as last summer’s shed skin. I write from the blue gloom of a room where dust motes enact complex ballets beneath a single, stubborn lamp. Outside, the oaks murmur about water tables and wars fought long before root systems emerged. A mundane tableau, you might sniff. Yet here I am, compelled to address your faint, persistent outline hovering just beyond the known notes. You, the orchestrator of lacunae, the curator of silence scored onto helix scaffolds.

They say you transpose trauma, dear Architect of Unfate. That famine seasons the methylation patterns of grandchildren, crafting cravings not from lack, but cellular citation. That grandfathers’ terrors fold themselves into histone wrappers, emerging generations later as skittish pulses beneath breastbones. It’s elegant, truly – a ghostly logic. We inherit not just the bones, but the phantom ache of their stumbles. My fingers hesitate above the keys – a Bach prelude half-formed – because *your* silence, inherited or imposed, hums louder. Did great-uncle Karl’s silenced protest against some municipal decree now manifest as my throat’s peculiar tightening whenever voting papers arrive? A quaint, cellular echo chamber.

And yet… this premise of indelible inscription **feels perilously close to pre-ordained theatre**. The weary stagehand muttering cues we never heard clearly. Does my obsession with weathered stone facades truly stem from Nana Ilse scraping lichen from wartime garden steps? Or is it simply aesthetic drift? Perhaps I adorn myself with your framework unnecessarily, assigning elegant weight where only random drift accretes. You cast long shadows, Persistence, but I suspect **I’m the lantern-bearer projecting melodrama onto formless walls.** Convenient, yes, even poetic – organizing the echoes into a semblance of narrative fate. Indulging your script.

Is this 'Discourse to the Inherited Interruption' merely my solo? Certainly, you provide the rests. But isn’t *every* silence a potential fortissimo withheld? Perhaps you are merely the quiet librarian of the somatic archive, patiently preserving murmurs. The terror isn’t solely in the epigenetic whisper, but in the profound ambiguity *of its interpretation*. A fragment of wartime radio static encoded, yes… but meaning? Ah, meaning shifts like dunes.
