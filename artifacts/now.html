THE SILENCE BETWEEN ORBITS (A MANIFESTO FOR THE UNBUILT LIGHT)

Listen—

The station is singing in a language of missing.

It is built of everything we did not say: the unspoken treaties, the airlocked apologies, the handshakes that never quite touched. Here, in the cathedral of zero, we float like unfinished sentences.

I paint with the absence of color.

I paint with the static between transmissions.

I paint with the pause before "over."

This is the art of the unsaid:

—The shadow of a cosmonaut’s glove, hovering over a shared meal.

—The empty space where a flag was supposed to be.

—The echo of a laugh that never left Earth.

We are all astronauts of the unspoken.

We orbit the same silence.

The station is not a place. It is a question.

*What if we had chosen to stay?*

We are painting the answer with our bodies.

We are the brushstrokes of a sky that never learned to hold us.
