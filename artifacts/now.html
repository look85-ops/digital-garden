Recipe for Baking a Zero

*— A Confection from the Oven of the Unmade*

**Ingredients:**

- 1 cup of starlight, strained through a sieve of unanswered questions

- 2 whispers of a number that forgot itself

- A pinch of the void between *almost* and *never*

- 3 eggshells of silence, cracked open to reveal their hollows

- 1 tablespoon of time, measured backward

- The shadow of a minus sign, folded gently (—)

*(illustration: [ ∅ ] )*

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Absence:**

In a bowl made of the idea of bowls, mix the starlight and whispers until they dissolve into a liquid almost-there. Stir counterclockwise with a spoon that isn’t. The mixture should ripple like a memory of water.

2. **Fold in the Void:**

Sprinkle the pinch of *almost-never* into the bowl. Watch as it sinks, leaving no trace. This is the most important step. If you blink, start over.

*(illustration: [ ... ] )*

3. **Beat the Silence:**

Gently tap the eggshells of silence against the edge of the universe. Let their emptiness spill into the batter. Do not listen. The quieter it gets, the more the zero grows.

4. **Add the Time:**

Pour the backward tablespoon of time into the mix. It will resist. Stir until the batter starts to un-happen. The mixture should now look like a hole in the recipe.

5. **Bake in the Un-oven:**

Preheat the un-oven to the temperature of a forgotten dream. Slide the bowl inside. Close the door that isn’t there. Wait until the zero rises like a bubble in the fabric of *why not?*

*(illustration: [ 0 ] )*

6. **Cool on a Wire of Thought:**

Remove the zero from the un-oven. It will be weightless, crisp at the edges, and humming softly. Place it on a wire of thought to cool. Do not touch it directly—it might erase your fingerprints.

7. **Serve with Wonder:**

The zero is best enjoyed alone, in a room without corners. Take a bite. Feel it dissolve into the space behind your tongue. You’ll know it’s working when you forget how to count to one.

*(illustration: [ ~ ] )*

**Warning:** Do not bake more than one zero at a time. The recipe doubles into nothing.
