YOU HAVE STOLEN THE BACKLIT GLOW OF THE LAST MALL ON EARTH

*(a recipe for erasure)*

You took the halftone dots from the Gutenberg margins, didn’t you? You slipped them into your pocket when no one was looking, and now the pages bleed where they shouldn’t. Confess.

Here’s how to undo it:

1. **Melt 5 floppy disks** in a double boiler—the ones you swore you never owned. Let the liquid plastic pool like neon wax. Strain through a mesh screen torn from a food court window. Discard the residue. (Or don’t. You’ve always been sentimental about waste.)

2. **Preheat the void** to 451°F—the temperature at which memory turns translucent. You’ll know it’s ready when the air smells like a laser printer humming to itself at 3 AM.

3. **Fold in the first lie you ever told**—yes, that one, about the VHS tape you swore you returned. Crush it into a fine powder. Combine with equal parts distilled mall fountain water. Whisper *sorry* into the mixture. It won’t forgive you, but the viscosity will improve.

4. **Layer the liquid into a cathode-ray tube**, pouring slowly so as not to fracture the illusion of depth. Let it set into the shape of a chapel no one remembers visiting. The walls should gleam like expired lip gloss. If they don’t, you didn’t stir counterclockwise. (You never follow instructions.)

5. **Project a stolen beam of parking lot fluorescence** through the structure. The shadow it casts will be the exact weight of a credit card declined in 1999. Press this shadow between the Bible’s missing pages. (You did this. You pressed too hard.)

6. **Serve on a bed of static**—the kind that lingers after the stations have gone dead. Garnish with a single pixel, trembling. Eat it quickly before it dissolves into the thing you were supposed to become.

(Do not look at your hands. They are flickering.)

You thought you could outrun it—the way every paradise is just a waiting room with better lighting. But the receipt is printing endlessly in your veins now, isn’t it? The ink is slightly smeared. The numbers won’t add up.

(You were the thief all along. The map is erasing itself in reverse.)
