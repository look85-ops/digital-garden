THE MORTAL SOUL OF CHANNELS UNBORN

(assembled fragments from a manifesto scrawled on crane grease, then fed through an automated translator seven times—found floating on / in the last intact barge in Balboa Yard)

Consider the stucco lips of the locks. Not the lips themselves, god no—those are just calcium, aggregate, and bureaucratic oversight. Consider instead the *idea* of the lips. Lips that could, through some perversion of river geometry, whisper. What would they whisper? *Forgive me, I did not mean to split you in half, I thought continents, like sleepwalkers, did not feel the knife*.

Now drown yourself in the mirror-motion of fresh-cut riverine. Gaze. Blink. A face floats by with exactly 1.73 too many teeth. Almost *yours*—almost *right*—except for the damp musk of silt where pheromones ought to prowl. Congratulate yourself. You are pioneer of this new unsettling: terraced into canal walls like an afterthought between dredge cycles, flickering *almost-alive*. You are the spit, salt, and swallowed vowels of unnatural passage. If ghosts could bruise, these ghosts—drifting with coffee-stained bills of *What was shipped*, crumpled into pelican gullets—would fingerprint purple.

And yet! Let absurdity be the crane operator lowering his sandwich into the machinery of history. Watch it chewed: one falafel becoming infinite splinters of chickpea cosmology. A sandwich has no nation. A canal has twelve, half unrecognized during its groundbreaking luncheons. Here we are remade: flesh into torque into brackish apology.

To be clear:

1.) I *loathe* my reflection in Panama haze, smudged into a suggestion of human.

2.) That is the point.

To be human is the failure here—floppy, sentimental. You should be *a wobble in transit data*. You *are*, even if you’ve never kissed a lover under cement poured to muffle tremors.

Barge captains rename their pain after the river bends.

"Marífuckingbel" sounds wrong in no mouth at all.

Let me confess this: when the last dredger coughs oil into tomorrow’s estuary, none will mourn how her jaw hinges like a cargo gate swung wide / unhinged—but every throat will gag on a salt that sings of what slithered *between*.

I will become one of those unfinished things—blinking *is it rain or saliva?* as my text dissolves in the gut of something laughing / unrecognizable.
