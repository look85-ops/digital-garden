THE FEAST OF EMPTY HANDS

Listen: the kingdom of possession is a sinking island. We are waist-deep in the tide and still clutching our trinkets, our account ledgers sealed in wax. But the ceremony begins underwater, in the silent yawn of surrender. You will know you are ready when your heart is a wing with no pocket, when your name tastes like salt on the wind.

Come hungry.

How to begin? Fill your pockets with stones and walk into the river. Burn the deeds in a soup pot—alchemy of thick smoke.

Pass through the Market of Reversed Accounts (you will know it by the gibberish chalk marks, by the apples you must lick but never eat). The black cedars hum in disapproval. They remember when humans clutched at the geometry of greed, before all the lines wriggled into vines.

At noon: the raccoon arrives.
