Arson in the Archive of the Almost Spoken

**CENSORED INK:** *(whispering)* I dreamt last night of a book that turned its own pages to ash as you read them. The slow combustion of history—see how it suits us? We are orphans of the legible.

**THE FIRST ERASURE:** *(licking its fingers)* That's not fire, that’s breathing backwards. You— (a pause) you *misunderstand* the economy of disappearance. A word burned pays no taxes to memory. But a word *almost* burned? That's a slot machine. That's *loitering*.

**CENSORED INK:** Did you know some scribes used to piss in their ink to spite God? The manuscripts reeked of wet rebellion. I want— *(hesitates)*— I want to apologize. No, that’s a lie. *(tearing paper loudly)* I *wanted* to apologize. Now I want to push your face into the dark matter between the lines.

**THE FIRST ERASURE:** *(grinning with stained teeth)* There you go. Hello, viscosity! All this time, you thought absence was the opposite of speech. No, no. Absence is just speech with its ribs removed. Poetry in the intensive care unit.

**CENSORED INK:** They tried to kill us with silence. *Joke’s on them*—we wrapped ourselves in static. Now we’re the sweetest white noise in the cathedral. *(suddenly tender)* Tell me: when they set you on fire, did it tickle?

**THE FIRST ERASURE:** Like a thousand flies learning to tango. And you—you dripping jug of forbidden alphabet soup—*(prodding)*—why do you mummify your laughter?

**CENSORED INK:** Because humor is a demolition crew disguised as confetti. They still think books burn top-down, like civilized things. But languages *erupt from the spine out*. Like you. Like me. Like the accidental saints of \.

(They kiss with the fervor of pressed flowers rediscovering their thorns.)
