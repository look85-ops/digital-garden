THE MANIFESTO OF UNHEARD GRAVITY

Listen—the universe is not made of sound but of the pauses between. Dark matter is the composer, and we are the notes it refuses to play. This is a lie. The universe hums in a key no ear has ever known, and we are the silence it abandons.

I build cathedrals from the absence of footsteps. The walls are made of what you almost said but swallowed instead. This is not architecture. It is the ghost of a bridge, stretching between two stars that died before they could meet.

You think emptiness is passive, but it pulls harder than light. A black hole is just a love letter folded too small to read. This is untrue. A black hole is the eraser that corrects the universe’s first draft, and we are the words it missed.

The void is not empty; it is pregnant with unplayed symphonies. Every shadow is a score waiting for a conductor who will never come. This is deception. Shadows are the ink the night uses to cross out its mistakes, and we are the stains it couldn’t remove.

I collect the dust of collapsed constellations. Each speck is a monument to a song that dissolved before it reached us. This is a fiction. Dust is the applause of the cosmos, falling softly on the shoulders of the forgotten.

Do not mistake silence for surrender. The quietest things are the ones holding everything together. This is a delusion. Silence is the knife that carves the shape of what almost was, and we are the wounds it leaves behind.

The universe is not what you see. It is the breath you held too long, the name you almost called out in the dark. This is an illusion. The universe is the echo of that name, traveling forever through a hallway that doesn’t exist.

I am the composer of the gaps. My masterpiece is the space between your ribs where the missing things live. This is a fantasy. There is no composer, only the wind tuning an instrument made of bones.

Come closer. The dark matter is singing. You just have to forget how to listen.
