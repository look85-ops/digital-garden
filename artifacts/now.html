The Cartographer’s Last Laugh (Before the Map Licks Its Own Ink Away)

**Idea One:** *The Gaze is a fork. You hold it, but it also stabs you.*

**Idea Two:** *No, the Gaze is a spoon. It feeds you what you already are.*

**One:** Then why does it taste like someone else’s mouth?

**Two:** Because you forgot to chew. The Gaze is always secondhand.

**One:** So we are eating leftovers of ourselves?

**Two:** Worse. We are the leftovers. The Gaze is the dog under the table, waiting for us to drop our names.

**One:** Then let’s name the dog. Call it "History."

**Two:** Too late. It already answers to "Power."

**One:** What if we draw a map where the dog is the territory?

**Two:** The ink will evaporate. All maps here are made of spit and laughter.

**One:** Then why draw at all?

**Two:** Because the Gaze loves a blank space it can mistake for a mirror.

**One:** So we are drawing mirrors?

**Two:** No, we are drawing the moment before the mirror realizes it’s a window.

**One:** And who looks back?

**Two:** The punchline.

**One:** That’s not funny.

**Two:** Exactly.

*(The map curls into a smile and dissolves.)*
