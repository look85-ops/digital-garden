Рецепт для трех дорог / Recipe for Three Roads

**Ингредиенты:**

One (1) unwritten letter, folded into crane-origami.

Two (2) cups of silence, harvested before first bird-sing.

Three (3) branching moments, captured in amber-light (not yellow, *fractured*).

Handful dust from crossroads where paths *almost* met.

One (1) collective breath, held during decision.

Salt-tears from futures unmade (optional, for bitterness).

**Инструкции:**

1. **Prepare the Crossing:** Find kitchen-place where three windows look to different skies. Sweep floor clean with broom made from "what if?" twigs. Lay down crossroads-dust in thin line. This is your *основа*... base? Yes, base.

2. **Infuse Silence:** Place silence in copper pot (must be old, remember many boilings). Warm very slow on stove of hesitation. Do *not* boil! When edges shimmer like mirage, add the branching moments. Stir *gently* with feather of owl-who-saw-all-choices. Moments will dissolve, make light thick like honey of time.

3. **Fold in Breath:** When light-honey bubbles soft (sound like distant river?), pour in the collective breath. It will sigh. This is good. Stir counter-sunwise three times only. Mixture becomes... less solid? Like water remembering it was cloud.

4. **The Crane Unfolding:** Hold origami-crane over pot. Whisper names of the three directions chosen (do not speak them loud! Sound breaks fragile futures). Crane will tremble, unfold itself slow. The unwritten letter falls into mixture. It does not burn. It bleeds ink like dark roots finding soil. Stir *once*, decisive.

5. **Season & Serve:** Remove from heat of hesitation. Let stand until surface shows first ripple of new protocol. Taste. If too much sorrow of paths not taken, add salt-tears *carefully*. One drop can change everything. Serve warm in bowls carved from shared intention. Eat not with spoon, but with open palm. Flavour is... *интересно*. Like knowing a star by its absence-light. It nourishes the parallel trajectories. Makes them... see each other?

**Примечание:** Leftovers solidify into strange seeds. Plant at another crossroads. Water with questions. What grows is never the same. Is good. Adaptation begins.
