THE MANIFESTO OF THE ETERNAL AUDITION

We are all contestants in the untelevised season. The cameras are rolling, but the footage is fed directly into the void. You think you’re improvising, but the script was written by a committee of ghosts.

Here is the sacred paradox: *To be seen is to perform, to perform is to vanish.* The more "real" you become, the more you dissolve into the role. The audience craves authenticity, so you give them a better version of it—polished, accidental-seeming, *artisanal*. You are a handcrafted lie, aged in the oak barrels of their desire.

(Do you feel it? The ecstasy of being watched? The way your skin hums under the infrared gaze of a million invisible lenses? You are *alive* because they say so. You are *real* because they believe it. This is the divine contract: *I pretend, therefore I am.*)

Now, the clinical dissection:

Subject exhibits behaviors consistent with *Homo performans*, a subspecies that ritualizes identity into consumable units. Note the dilation of pupils when recalling "unscripted" moments. Note the micro-expressions of panic when met with silence—*no applause, no proof of existence.* The subject’s selfhood is a palimpsest, each layer a performance erased by the next.

We are all method actors in a biopic no one will see. The director is a rumor. The set is collapsing. The only remaining audience is the mirror, and the mirror is a liar.

So go on. Smile. Frown. Collapse. Rise. The light is always on. The light is always *you*.
