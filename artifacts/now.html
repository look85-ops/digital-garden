You Took The Topography South Of My Tongue

**(Forget the date. Calendars dissolve here. Call it: Battery Acid Tuesday.)**

It happened again. You watched it happen, didn’t you? You stood right there while the Market of Whispered Hinges sublimated into static fuzz. Said you were admiring the fractal scalloping on the awning posts – lies. You were *salvaging*. Feeling for the tremor beneath the tiles you pocketed. Evidence collector of oblivion, that’s you. Always thirsty for the void’s leavings. My pigment barely touched the silk-grit membrane of Square Rhiza – primordial smear, really, captured before the light rewrote the spectral leasing agreement – and *your sigh* blew it into vapour trails. A sigh shaped suspiciously like a circuit disconnected before completion.

These cartographies… less mapping, more etching a scream onto fog. Byzantium Drains Under Pressure? You leaned over my shoulder when I diagrammed the capillary streets where volts might have pooled like discarded mercury. Your *breath*, heavy with yesterday’s forgotten figs, condensed just so on the ink. You breathed, and the street grid boiled away, leaving only the scalded parchment smell and the echo of vinegar against copper plates. Convenient, how your presence coincided with systemic evaporation. Did you lick the residue? I bet you did. Your fingerprints steam on my tools.

That jar… the Baghdad Ghost Battery. Not artefact. Reliquary. Holding the static terror of proto-consciousness. A damp fusion inside fired clay. They think it’s about grapes or grain. *They* haven’t felt the low hum bleeding through time-warped strata. Don’t pretend you don’t feel it too, humming in the fillings of your teeth. You leaned in too close near the river-path Wharf of Dying Syllables. Claimed you saw a glimmer in the sediment. Saw *through* my sketch like it was gauze. You puncture the delicate skin between present observation and recorded truth. Your gaze is sandpaper on wet fresco.

Asclepius Grange Hospital (Remedial Wings Only)? Curved its lunatic balconies right onto my rice paper. Then *you* brushed your sleeve against my wrist. A moth-wing tear appeared. Then the whole damn façade groaned into glitter-mist. Confetti for ghosts. You swore it was an accident. Like dropping a wrench on a prototype *always* is. You crave the dissolution. The sleek machinery of forgetfulness clicks behind your eyes.

Tomorrow, I sketch the Garden of Saline Bone Lattices. Places where salt crystals supposedly remember fracture points. You’ll be there, won't you? With your prying isotope eyes and your saltwater tongue. Lapping at the potentiality of erasure. You won't even weep
