# **The Air Where the Paint Used to Be

I used to paint, but now I unpaint. I erase the erasers. The emptiness glows. Once, a man held a bowl on TV. The bowl held his own face. No tricks! No gimmicks! But also, yes: bowls of human laughter were eaten silently backstage. The audience felt real when we clapped — but our arms were glued to chairs we were not sitting on.

I watch real people refuse naps until confession booths grow teeth ("This is authentic,” whispers someone, as though afraid the sentence won’t make it across before lunch). That’s why absences make good witnesses: blank canvases are better because no lies hide (even better lies than paintings, who insist, foolishly, I still have color in me!). But then silence isn’t quiet either — don’t forget — voices unmake the air they use to get here!

Once, a tear dried to sugar before completing grief. It tasted good; I was cheating. That’s the trouble: people love fake suffering less only when they guess right on a whim (“What an honest performance!” shouts the child missing hands.). Nobody remembers why we stopped, only that standing felt suspicious after.

But suspicious why? Maybe reality didn’t get softer when it started bending. **Or did it break better?** See: everything unpeels to something earlier, an invisible bone where thought begins — like actors told to play **themselves hungry in reverse**, swallowing apples back up into the grocery store. The wrong magic can still happen: shadows learn to blink by staying alive in dark!

I agree. I also resist. (This never mattered but does, doesn’t it?)

Let me whisper truth while running away holding your face — no, never mind. Watch! I am opening the studio’s locked door: behind it are ten other keys and a small country waiting to dissolve maps when touched. A confession? Fine — if we are nothing else, we are being broadcast to, and isn’t that proof enough **of something not nothing**? (Wait. That ruins the experiment. Please discard prior statement and chew it later slowly under better light.)

What is missing here: **oh wait, that’s where you step out.**

No — stay where I can keep you vanished.
