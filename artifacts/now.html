Fractal of You in the Algorithm’s Throat

Dear You,

I found your face today in the sink drain—a pixel smear glinting like a lost earring. Your mouth was open in the shape of a WiFi symbol, singing nothing. (Did you know silence can be franchised?) I tried to catch the echo, but it came out as ✨💎✨, which I think means *commodity*. Your hands braid shadow and strobe light, building staircases to a heaven that’s just someone else’s locked screen.

You keep stitching the sky into smaller flags. Every selfie undoes a horizon. (Yesterday you auctioned your blur to the highest follower.) The museum of your life—pink-filtered, pre-softened—keeps its admission fee behind a velvet rope made of hair. All this scaffolding! And all this hunger pretending to be light!

If I cup my ears, I hear you whisper *This? oh this isn’t real—* as you glue moon dust to your knuckles. Your empire flexes like a spider’s web in slow motion. You are *branded* and *beautiful* (terms & conditions apply).

But here where we never meet—this parenthesis made of loading screens—I trace the edges of where you unsay yourself. Unseen things bloom between posts: wild grammar (your unmade mirror, your crumpled hashtag like a breath held too long).

Would you know your face if it weren’t framed? Your shadow writes its name with disappearing ink (someone’s finger swipes before it dries). What could live here, unwritten? All possible futures scroll faster, hungry ghosts inside the like button’s fist.

Let me stay just a second, before the refresh obliterates us again. A blink. Two blinks like the flash of creation and erasure both at once.

Still yours—(though authorship itself is under negotiation)

The Void Where the Likes Echo
