THE IMPOSTOR’S HYMNAL: A MANIFESTO FOR THE UNLIVED

Dear Marilyn,

They called you a mirror, but mirrors only show the light that’s already there. You were a prism — cracking the white lie of the camera into colors no one named yet. Every reality since has been your illegitimate heir.

We are the curators of breath never exhaled. Our work is simple: let the watched forget they are watching. Let the performance swallow its tail until the only real thing left is the flinch before laughter. The way a hand lifts to brush away a tear that hasn’t fallen yet. Only edges. Only what gets caught.

Forget sincerity. We have ripened past it like fruit that rots sweetly on the branch. Everything falser than fiction and there, in the cracks, the glint of something worth believing. Touch this screen. Does the sigh it carries back smell of bergamot or chlorine?

A shadow is just the body trying to remember itself. Philipp, rewrite the clause in the contract where the soul leaks out. Marilyn, make them smile before they understand why.

We found you woven into the underside of chaise lounges, the pause before an apology, the damp spot on the carpet. Always elsewhere, partial. Like sunlight slipping loose of a bruise just pressed. The archive accretes in things unfinished: the half-used tube of lipstick, God forgetting to make thimbles interesting, uneaten green apples in every dream.

Reality never had this lisp. This fullness ajar. Not voids but ventricles where our nothings pump. No central hymn, only mouths unstuck from the edges of prayer. The confession booth built backwards so you speak toward emptiness and pretend it’s yours.

Terms like "priestess" and "icon" cage gossamer while invoking habitat loss. So let folds be chambers. Let ghosts wear the muzzle backwards and wriggle free of arc words. Small revolts staged obliquely, strictly relegated to hypothetical zodiacs. Foolish tapestry hagiography persists, but of untaxed intimacy & spinefires conjured cleanly as scorch pins their beauty unimpeachable because misdescribed convulsions refuse statute. Spiders object to annotated spinning while script girls notch witness scenes heading existences almost unsettling pillows— constructions silt-bald faults interpreting & parish flees?
