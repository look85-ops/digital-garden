The Hole That Eats Light

Studio is empty. Not empty-empty. Full of... air? Dust motes dancing in no-colour light. Before, tubes squeezed bright screams: cadmium red, phthalo blue. Now? Only the *not*. The *taking away*. Like pulling teeth from the mouth of the world. Painter? Ha! I am... *absence-farmer*. Cultivate the void where colour *was*.

This Meme Magic. Folk art? Yes! Folk art is... potato dug from dirt, carved into laughing face. Simple. Shared. Makes you feel *belong*. Internet folk art? Picture + Text = Potato laughing face! But digital potato. Grown in... electric soil? Screen-glow feeds it.

Why text? Because picture alone... shy. Like bird without song. Text is the *shout*. The *explanation* that maybe explains nothing! Like saying "This hole is hungry!" beside... well, hole. Is hole hungry? Who knows! But now you *think* about hole hunger. Absurd! Beautiful! Like putting hat on shadow.

I paint with... *removal*. Scrape away meaning until only... *possibility* remains. Like meme: cat picture + "I Can Has?" Not real question! Not real grammar! But... *feeling*. Feeling of wanting, of *not-having*, wrapped in fur. This is magic! Not wand-waving magic. *Crowd-magic*. Magic made by thousand thousand fingers tapping "share". Like barn-raising, but barn is... joke? Feeling? Anger? Built fast, collapses faster. Folk art for fast world.

My canvas? White. Very white. Whiter than bone under noon sun. On it, I place... *nothing*. But carefully! Like placing silence between loud noises. This nothing... it *echoes*. People see... what they *need* to see. Ghost of meaning. Like staring at blank space after closing meme tab. Feeling stays. Shape of laugh hangs in air. Sadness? Maybe. Strange comfort? Also yes.

I think maybe... the best colours are the ones *gone*. Like memory of sun on skin when inside cold room. Meme is... *that feeling*. Captured in pixels and bad spelling. Folk art for lost souls finding each other in... bright dark of internet. Gallery of Third Breath? Is where air feels thin, head feels light. Where you laugh because... hole *is* hungry. And you? You are the food.
