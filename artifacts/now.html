The Book That Ate Its Own Vowels

Listen. Is not colour. Is *absence*. Like hole in sky where bird was. Fast. I paint this hole. I paint the *no*. The book, it burn? No. Worse. It eat. It eat the words inside. Like hungry mouth in paper. Teeth-marks in meaning.

Oulipo? Ah. Friends! They say: "Put chain on word. Make it dance strange." Cage the bird, it sing new song. Cage the book? It... *breathes* different. The burners, they want quiet. Shhh. But Oulipo, they make quiet *loud*. With rules. Like: "No letter E." Or: "Only words from Tuesday." Stupid? Maybe. Genius? Also maybe.

My canvas: the page after fire. But not ash. Not black. *White*. The white that screams. I paint the shape of missing. Where word *was*. Like ghost in room. You feel cold spot? That is my colour. "Absence-Blue". Very expensive. Ha!

The censored book... it become... puzzle? Yes. But puzzle with pieces stolen. Oulipo say: "Steal pieces? Good! Now build with air." So. I take book that ate its own vowels. *Nom nom*. A, E, I, O, U... gone. Only consonants left. Hard stones in mouth. K, T, Z. Like cough.

I paint the silence between them. The space where "LOVE" was, now only "L_V". Hole. I paint the hole. Is it mouth? Is it wound? Is it window? Yes. All. Window to... what? To the word that *could* be. The word the burners killed, but Oulipo... Oulipo *resurrects* as... possibility. A forest of "maybe". Library of zero books. Full!

You see? Burner makes flat silence. Dead. Oulipo makes *active* silence. Pregnant! My painting? Is the womb of that silence. The chapel... "Perpetual Departure"... good name. Here, words are always leaving. But their shadows stay. I paint the shadow. The echo. The taste of "apple" when only "APPL_" remains. You lick the page? Tastes like... maybe.

Is beautiful? Strange beautiful. Like skeleton of song. You hear the music bones make? Clack clack. That is my art. The censored book, re-dressed in chains of freedom. It hums in the negative. A hymn for the departed, sung by the space they left. Blessed are the empty, for they shall be filled... with *nothing wonderful*. Amen. Or... perhaps just "A M N".
