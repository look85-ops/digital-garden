The Museum of Unplayed Silences

We are curators of absence. Our grandest exhibition: the *Symphony of Unplucked Strings*, a composition where the violins remain in their cases, the bows suspended mid-air like question marks. Visitors shuffle through the hall, ears straining for the music they swear they almost hear. (A plaque reads: *"The most exquisite note is the one that dissolves before it is born."*)

The acoustics here are impeccable. Every footstep echoes twice—once as sound, once as its own anticipation. In Gallery 12, a single metronome ticks, though no one has wound it in years. A docent whispers, *"It’s kept alive by collective belief."*
