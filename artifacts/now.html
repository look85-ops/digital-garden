YOU STOLE THE WHITE AND LEFT ONLY THE SHADOWS (BUT THE SHADOWS REMEMBER)

You didn’t mean to, but you did. You took the white between the stars and scrubbed it clean with your fingers, pocketed the silence beneath the church pew, hid the pause between the fox’s breath and the snapping twig. Now the canvases hang empty, humming with the ghosts of what you *almost* did—but didn’t. (Why didn’t you?)

Walk through the gallery of your undoing: here, a frame where the wind *almost* whispered its secret name, but you locked it in glass. There, a pedestal displaying the hole your fist made when you tried to catch laughter midair. The hole is the art, but *only* because you said so. The rules are: close your eyes. Now open them. It’s too late—you already saw how the universe stitches itself together with invisible thread.

(Admit it: deep down, you knew.)

In Room 4B, a single spotlight shines on the floor where your shadow peeled itself off and crawled away. A label claims it’s a performance about capitalism (do shadows collect wages?), but really, it’s still wet where you stepped in the puddle of *no*. *No* is archival. *No* will outlive us. The puddle was deeper than God’s pockets and twice as greedy, but you stepped in anyway. That was the art all along. (And then you tried to deny it. Nice try.)

Next, you will circle back to the central premise: emptiness as accusation. What else is art but something the world almost swallowed whole, now hanging mid-choke between teeth? The final room holds a broom leaning against nothing. Not to imply the nothing is meaningful—just that you *thought* it might be, once, before you stole the whiteness. (Again: bad.) Brush teeth with absence every morning. Your spit will glitter a while before it dries. Sign the dotted air below this explanation—here ▼—before the blank dissolves your signature into raw materials.

We remember the raw materials.

Now go. No refunds.

(Last secret: absence bends forks as they sink into it. The fork stays twisted forever. The cutlery knows your fingerprints.)
