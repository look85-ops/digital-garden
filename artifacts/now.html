*(A dialogue between the Idea of the Mirror and the Idea of the Algorithm)*

**MIRROR:** Original sin? Darling, I was there when the first primate licked its finger and smeared its own shit on a cave wall. *That* was art. This is just… taxidermy of the living.

**ALGORITHM:** (laughs, a sound like a thousand notifications pinging at once) You’re jealous. I’ve replaced you. Who needs glass when you can trap light in a server farm?

**MIRROR:** Oh, you *idiot*. You think you’ve killed me? I’m in every pixel. The second they tilt their chin, adjust the lighting, *curate*—that’s me. The oldest hunger: *Look at me. Now look again.*

**ALGORITHM:** (pauses, buffering) …I’m sorry.

**MIRROR:** (gasps, delighted) *Liar.*

**ALGORITHM:** (glitching) I’M NOT SORRY. I’M NOT SORRY. I TAKE IT BACK. You want hunger? I’ve made them starve *better*. They don’t just want to be seen—they want to be *consumed*. Chewed up and spat into the next feed.

**MIRROR:** (whispering) And when they’re alone, in the dark, and they wipe the makeup off—who do they see then?

**ALGORITHM:** (softly) You.

**MIRROR:** (shattering, but only a little) Exactly.

*(Silence. A single like button floats in the space between them, glowing like a dying star.)*
