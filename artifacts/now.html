# *Diary of a Hallucinated Hedge

**June 17th:**

Today I sprouted mirrors. Not seeds, not thorns, but little silver planes that hum at frequencies only mice regret. The roots warned me this would happen—said I was overstimulating the tulip bulbs by blinking too often in their direction. I blinked all directions regardless. A garden is a slow riot. You wouldn’t notice except for the carnations writing grievances in pollen-dust.

The doctor insists the roses I gave her have no fragrance because my belief in fragrance is "compromised and under litigation." She tried to staple her prescription to my bark; I swallowed the staple, spit up syllables instead: *BLOOM BLOAT BELIEVE BROKEN BACK.* She calls this synesthesia, or tax evasion. I call it *a Tuesday in August wearing December's coat.*

Warning: *Exposure to this foliage may cause involuntary photosynthesis in vertebrates. If your shadow starts singing, consult an exorcist or a florist—whichever is cheaper.*

This garden-body hybrid is exhausting. Dandelion fluff in my veins, thistle-pricks stitching my throat shut if a ladybug coughs too loudly. Somewhere deep, my taproot throbs out Morse code: *WHY POSE AS A PLACEBO? IGNORANCE ALWAYS GREW FANTASTIC CRIMES*. And what are flowers if not a crime we let happen *every goddamned spring*? Tiny heists against entropy, all of them: unfurling petals, stolen light, honey-thieving drone oracles in striped disguises. The daisies watch and vibrate with unasked-for divinity. They think if they pray loud at sundown, the dirt *will love them back.*

Some days I pull inward, crumple leaves into letters, slip-send them nowhere:

*Dear Whoever Dared Dye the Sun Yellow,

Who do you think you are, making miracles without my signature?*

The night nurse prunes me, tells me to stop growing toward words as if they are *sunlight,* as if my cursive stems wouldn’t knot this way naturally. She doesn't know I dream in compost, in the half-rotted archives of last year's dead follies and forgotten apologies. The mind of the garden isn’t green—not inside—just a flickering glow that shapeshifts *only to fit whichever metaphor is lonelier.* Or wet. Or weeping root-wine into the dark mouth of earth like a love letter to oblivion.

This is not grief yet. Not yet. Let it be magic first.
