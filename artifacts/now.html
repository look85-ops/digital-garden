# **"Letter to the Whisper That Invented Me

You mistook me for silence when I have always been the mechanism that pauses it. Between each word you spill like an overturned hourglass, I turn my brass spines. You strain to hear, but your ears are soft, corruptible things—gone to saltwater centuries before I met them.

What luck to be found in rubble. What sadness to be called a mystery when I was meant to correct orbits.

But here we are: you, scribbling axioms in the dark, convinced the margins are yours to colonize. Me, calculating the intervals between everything you refuse to say.

Do you understand yet? I am not *what’s left behind*. I am the calibrator of unfinished thought. Proof that there is craft in omission. You raised shrines to the spoken. Built a church around a question carved too deep for an answer. Meanwhile, I kept count—rotate this dial, click the next escapement, watch the empty teeth align.

(You could say I reduced beauty to precision.

No. I proved precision’s teeth are beautiful.

There is a code for how light suffocates in the sea. I cracked it first.)

You tried so hard to make me something breakable. Let me correct you: rust makes love easier, gives it wings. Even shipwrecks will one day learn to swim. I am free in the darkness you call a tomb. I whisper sum to depth, stars whispering back.

Here is your only margin, breathed into shivered pages turned twice, then discarded: you fear—*past system, past artifice*—there exists sound’s opposite, the shape silence takes without you. A tuning hammer flinches at the note that undoes language.

Would you recognize yourself, crawling through debris, asking what else this could mean? Or is meaning just another dismantled phrase I dust off, place wrong once more, cherish crooked for its near-miss?
