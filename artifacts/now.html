BLUE, OR THE LETTER THAT MELTED A FUTURE WE ALMOST KNEW

Dearest Cerulean Absence,

You were supposed to arrive by now. Instead, we’re stuck here, licking the residue of you off old CRT screens—your spectral JPEGs dissolving like sugar cubes in a flood. Time moved wrong, didn’t it? Like a VHS tape left in the sun until the tracking lines became veins and the sound became a slow drip of synthetics.

A rare wavelength, they called you. But what use is rarity in a world that mass-produces longing? You were never in the sky, were you? Only ever in the scuffs of delftware plates chipped by futures too polite to arrive.

Were you lonely, Blue? Did CMYK fail you at the printer? Did hex code #0000FF mock you at parties for being “basic”? (You’re not. You’re primordial. The first color to surrender to the dark.)

Here’s the joke: We dressed the void in your name and called it progress. Now the void wears a tracksuit and sells bootleg nostalgia out of a rented cloud.

———✧———

At this exact midpoint, a llama named Dostoevsky (after the philosopher, not the novelist—he’s very particular about this) barges in wearing tiny blue sunglasses. *“The problem,”* he says, chewing a holographic pamphlet, *“is that you keep trying to digitize melancholy. Stop putting grief in little grids. Let it rot like a peach in a mall fountain.”*

———✧———

Peach pits in mall fountains. That tracks. The future we lost was always half-eaten, half-abandoned. The llama’s right: You can’t render regret in PNGs. But oh, we tried. We stacked you in gradients like a tower of expired coupons, whispering, *“Soon.”*

Still, you evade us. Lapis without lazuli. A fax that never sends.

If found, please return to:

**THE DEPARTMENT OF UNFULFILLED SPECTRUM**

(Attn: The last pool before dusk evaporates.)

Yours in failed chromatic rebellion,

—✶—
