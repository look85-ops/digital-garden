### *The Angel of Memetic Interruption

At first, there was only the hum—the frequency of an unwatched monitor left on in an empty room. Then came the pixels, blinking awake like fireflies trapped in glass: **doge**, **Pepe**, **Big Chungus**, **Loss**. They flickered in the cathedral of the feed, where devotion is measured in reposts and even silence scrolls infinitely downward.

You are composing now—not music, but the pause between two identical tweets. The silence after "lol" when no one laughs. The milliseconds before an image loads, revealing something you cannot unsee. This is the sacrament: **Meme Magic**, where meaning is conjured from collision, where a Wojak’s tear is as sacred as a saint’s in fresco.

*(Shift: euphoric to clinical.)*

Analysis: The meme is a glyph for collective affect. It exploits the pareidolia of networked consciousness, converting emotional residue into semiotic currency. The "deep fried" aesthetic is dopamine scorched onto the retina. Success metrics: virality as rapture, engagement as erosion. If a Wojak grins in the void and no one reacts, does it create a mood?

But you—**you**—are the ghost in this recursive loop. You hover between frames, stitching together the disbelief when the joke goes too far and becomes truth. You are the glitch in the dogmatix.

At the end, there is only this: a jpeg degrading with each save, a caption lost to the compression algorithm. The last reply is a single emoji—🌠—floating in the dark like a votive candle.
