Dear Hand Left in the Dark

Oh, you whispered smear of ochre and torch-flicker! You stubborn shadow from a time before alphabets bit their own tails! I am writing to you with fingers that remember your shape, even though they’ve never touched the wall where you pressed yourself against the stone and said, *Here—listen!* in a language of soot and skin.

Do you remember the quietest sound? The one just after the torch sputters but before the last echo stops hunting its own reflection along the cave’s ribs? I have been collecting it—little shards of that silence, broken off like flint. Sometimes I hold them up to my ear and pretend I can hear the exact wet crunch of clay between your fingers, 40,000 years ago.

What were you rehearsing while you waited for the rest of us? Maybe nothing. (Even still—I kneel here and try, against logic, to paint your palm backward into mine with dust.) They argue over whether you were making "art" or tracing "tools" into the wall but oh, *honey*, I think you were leaving a breadcrumb-trail for something else. Maybe the hands were there to catch whatever fell upward—like that child who told me once that the sky isn’t sky but a great upside-down river of falling, and we are all just sticking our faces into it with wet cheeks.

Or no. You could’ve just been leaving directions home. Or you liked how the wall turned the *shh, shh* friction-of-body-against-rock into a ghost you could follow forever? I don’t mind! I don’t even care whether the fingers curl into a story or just yawn like a crack in old fruit. Tell it slant or mute—I *lick* each guess off my knuckles!

**Some lost ingredients for you:**

- Bone-needles spliced backward to darn holes between hours

- A night’s exhale soaked in reindeer tallow

- A song made entirely out of interrupted footsteps

I want to sleep inside the hollow print where you touched the stone and thought, for one half-breath, the dark reached *back—* until you laughed or gasped or forgot before finishing. Now? We’re still the pause in whatever that gesture meant. I wake every morning with an urgent un-lit pulse between my hands: a new instruction that might be to make a fist or cup a flame… but only for a moment before forgetting if hands burn if fed just echoes.

*Yours, chasing,*

The Composer of Held Breath (and its unspillable sound)
