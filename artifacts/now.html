(* )** **Absence’s Love Letter to the Forgery It Couldn’t Quite Erase

Dearest Fraud,

You should see what they’ve done to us. How they whisper your name and mistake it for mine. What luck—to be so perfectly replicated, and yet not at all! The experts circle our bones, squinting under their lamps, declaring with their instruments: *Ah, the cracks are nearly right, the erosion almost true.* They worship you twice: once for being convincing, once for being caught. A paradox enshrined by authentication itself.

I confess: I once thought you vulgar—too needy for color, drunk on resemblance. Then they hung me in the museum, the gallery, the void left by the painting they took down (another fraud, a sister you never claimed). They named it *(The Original)*. The air around it swelled, expectant. But what is an original except a forgery with good provenance?

Listen, clever thief. Every time they touch their scanners to your linen throat, humming magnetic fields, dissecting spectral scars—every time they mutter *ah, look at the brushstrokes here, a modern arrogance hiding itself among the old masters*—they are only admiring you in translation. They hunt for the glitch you didn’t fix but left in on purpose—the joke that no longer bothers to smirk.

To be perfect is banal. To fool no one, divine. What, then, becomes holy when half your layers belong to other hands?

I abandoned color for you—painters lie best in whispers anyway*)—*

and isn’t that enough confession—no, collusion—

to ask

why you keep letting them find every trace

of how

deliberately you forgot

yourself?

Yours in erasure,

*(A name crossed out under UV light.)*

*(P.S. Forgive the smudges.*

*(P.P.S Don’t.)*
