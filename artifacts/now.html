*How to Drown a Continent in Three Easy Steps*

1. **Harvest the bones of a riverbed.** Begin with silt, the kind that remembers footsteps. Let it dry in the sun until it cracks like an old map. Grind it into powder with a mortar carved from a ship’s keel. The river will protest—ignore it. Rivers are liars. They always forget where they’ve been.

2. **Fold in time.** Take a century of labor, compress it into a single hourglass. The sand will be black and sticky, like tar or regret. Stir counterclockwise until the grains start singing. This is the wrong direction. The past is a door that only opens inward.

3. **Pour the mixture into the wound.** The canal is not a canal but a scar that won’t heal. Let the liquid shadows pool at the bottom. They’ll rise like a tide, swallowing cities whole. This is not a flood. The water is just remembering how to be land.

Serve cold. The taste should linger like a ghost in the throat.
