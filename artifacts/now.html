*The Solemn Cartography of Disappearing Soles*

**Idea 1 (Tender):**

What if every step we take in these shoes is a love letter to the ground? The laces, cursive. The tread, a signature. The way the left shoe leans slightly inward—oh, that’s just my heart’s handwriting.

**Idea 2 (Strange):**

No, no. The shoes are not *worn*. They are *performed*. Like a play where the audience is the pavement, and the applause is the sound of rubber peeling away in slow, sticky layers. The climax? When the last thread snaps, and the shoe becomes a ghost.

**Idea 1:**

But ghosts don’t leave footprints. These do. Faint, yes—like a blush on the cheek of the sidewalk.

**Idea 2:**

Then the sidewalk is a collector. A museum of almost-touches. Every scuff mark is a fossilized *what if*.

**Idea 1:**

What if we kissed in these shoes? Would the soles melt like sugar?

No. They’d dissolve into alphabet soup. The letters would spell *AIR* and *GRAVITY* and *YOU WERE HERE BUT NOW YOU’RE NOT*.

**Idea 1:**

That’s not a warning label.

Isn’t it?
