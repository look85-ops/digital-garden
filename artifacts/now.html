THE TALKING WIND AND THE LAUGHING STONE

*(A dialogue in the hollow of a forgotten hour)*

**THE WIND:**

I saw you yesterday, but you were upside down. Or was it me?

**THE STONE:**

*(giggles)* I’m always upside down if you stand on your head. Try it! The sky looks like a puddle.

**THE WIND:**

*(whispers)* I can’t stand. I’m made of falling. But I remember—once, I blew through a fence and it sang. The wires hummed like bees who forgot their stings.

**THE STONE:**

Bees are just sticky sunlight. And fences are just long, thin trees who forgot how to leaves.

**THE WIND:**

*(spinning)* Do you think the universe is a fence? With holes too small to see?

**THE STONE:**

*(pause)* No. The universe is a stone that got so curious it turned inside out. Now it’s all questions wearing the costumes of answers.

**THE WIND:**

*(laughs, scattering petals)* Then I’m the breath of those questions! I tug at your edges. I make you shiver.

**THE STONE:**

*(warmly)* You do. But also—*(softens)*—when you leave, I miss you. Even though you never *really* leave. You’re just elsewhere, tousling other hair.

**THE WIND:**

*(suddenly serious)* What if “elsewhere” is a lie? What if I’m always here, just folded? Like when you stack two shadows and they turn transparent.

**THE STONE:**

*(glowing faintly)* Then I’m also there, in the folds. A little piece of me, tucked in your pocket.

*(A silence. The air grows heavy with listening.)*

**THE WIND:**

*(urgent now)* They’re coming. The ones who count steps. Who say “up” and “down” like they own them.

**THE STONE:**

Quick—let’s swap places! You be the stone, I’ll be the wind.

**THE WIND:**

*(grinning)* But you can’t move!

**THE STONE:**

Exactly. Now *watch*.

*(The stone dissolves into a sigh. The wind stumbles, suddenly solid. Somewhere, a fence forgets its song.)*
