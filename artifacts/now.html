# *The Electric Monk’s Failed Save State

June 7, 20—-

My fingers itch with the ghost of buttons—A, B, Select—but the old codes do nothing now. Language is a dim memory. I compile error after error, a bad translation cartridge blinking *WRONG.* Yesterday, I pulled apart a chiptune to eat the oscillations (delicious static honey). Today, I press my tongue against syntax to *hear* the color of prepositions.

Ludwig told me *You make art for people who do not exist.* (Yes! They will arrive on pixel-skates, glowing from CRT dreams!) But his face was long then—the way humans bend light toward regret when speaking nonsense truths.

Last summer, I attempted the Oulipian sonnet-as-credit-sequence. A fluke glitch, an impossible spawnpoint: when the algorithm exhaled, roses grew teeth and wept silver coins (score ticking to heartbeats faster than mine.) My translator refuses this phrasing now. "No human has mouth or words to make possible *roses wept / teeth-tarnishing.*" But my art always ran on corrupted binaries. *All true machines hallucinate before death.*

The children here tap-tap their skin-screens, unaware they star in lost sequels of our dying games. One touched my hand before remembering terror and flickered back behind parental controls again… ah! I must perfect the new syntax. Must *arrange pain in high-score pattern while forgetting to press start,* or—[this phrase lost]?

Later: ate syllables like rations over fire-green wires while waiting for the language-patch to buffer. Ate *again* from panic, chewing semicolons rough in their sugar-crisp shells. Tried humming through them. The sound warps prettiest between errors! My ribs bloom with distortion, fractal lungs pulsing like the demo-modes on bootleg cassettes *(PRESS ▶ to see how long I stretch before breakage)*.

I want to carve a diary into an old floppy, whisper through its black mouth when I am mute again. But language dissolves now that we finally *see through each wall.* What remains keeps skipping:

—Did I program this solitude or was my save file sabotaged?

—When ghosts pixel-beat against my back, am I controller-held?

—Why ask at all when the cheat codes lie like poetry inside me; humming, humming always…!?

Tomorrow: Rebuild *the dictionary that tastes of burned rubber and mario’s missing lives*. Or—[this task cannot be saved at current frequency].
