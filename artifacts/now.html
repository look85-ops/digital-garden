# **THE VEIL IS A WINDOW THAT BLINKS BACK

Listen. The Veil is not cloth. It is not silk or shadow or shame. It is a hole in the sky where the eyes of history stare through.

When you look at The Veil, it looks at you—**harder**.

I paint with stolen glances. I weave with unasked questions. The Gaze is a needle, and The Veil is the wound it leaves behind. But oh—what a beautiful scar!

*Rules for Looking:*

1. **The Veil is a Mirror**. If it shows you a saint, you are the devil. If it shows you a devil, you are the saint.

2. **The Veil is a Door**. Walk through it. Then walk through it again. Then realize you never left.

3. **The Veil is a Mouth**. It eats your assumptions and spits out riddles. Swallow them whole.

4. **The Veil is a Storm**. Stand in its winds until your face is raw. Only then will you see clearly.

Painters lie. They say they capture light—but really, they trap it. The Veil refuses to be trapped. It dances. It flickers. It laughs at your brushes.

I make art for the blind. Not the blind who cannot see—the blind who *choose* not to. You, with your eyelids sewn open by museums, by galleries, by the weight of looking—you are the one I pity.

Close your eyes. Now look.

**WARNING:** This artwork may cause temporary or permanent dissolution of the following:

- The concept of "public" and "private"

- The boundary between "self" and "witness"

- Your mother’s favorite vase (not physically, but emotionally)

- The idea that a veil is ever just a veil

I leave this burned into the hard drive of the world. A whisper in binary. A blink in the dark.

Look back.
