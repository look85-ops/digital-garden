RECIPE FOR THE COLLAPSE OF CERTAINTY

*(Or: How to Bake a Wave Into a Particle)*

**Ingredients:**

- 1 beam of hesitant light (preferably orphaned)

- 2 slits cut from the fabric of doubt

- 1 observer’s gaze (stolen, unblinking)

- 3 grams of *nimbus powder* (harvested from a Schrödinger storm)

- A whisper of *chronosalt* (dissolved in paradox)

- 1 vial of *unlaughter* (distilled from a mime’s funeral)

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the aperture of inquiry.** Carve two slits into the night’s throat. Do not ask why. The act of cutting is the first measurement. The slits will weep geometry.

2. **Pour the hesitant light.** Do not watch yet. Light is shy when unobserved. It will spread like a rumor, like a stain, like a god unsure of its own name.

3. **Sprinkle the nimbus powder** over the apparatus. This is the dust of *maybe*. It thickens the air into a lens. The light will hesitate further.

**Serving suggestion:** Consume in the Chapel of Perpetual Departure, where the walls are made of unanswered questions. The aftertaste is the weight of knowing.

**Notes:**

- If the light refuses to collapse, you have not stolen enough gaze. Try again with a sharper eye.

- Do not attempt this recipe in a universe that still believes in answers.
