Entanglement as Proof That Someone, Somewhere, Has Folded a Thought Into a Paper Swan

A pair of neural ponds, one in your left hemisphere, the other in a stranger’s—ripples syncing, though neither pond has ever seen the other.

This artwork exists as a **sentence collapsed mid-thought**, recovered from an abandoned hard drive, now stretched between two screens: yours and a neglected museum kiosk in Tallinn. The words shimmer if you squint. They are the same. They are not the same.

*Form:* A digital palimpsest where every keystroke forks into **parallel drafts**. One version is polished, unbearable in its clarity. The other is a **runaway alphabet**, letters decoupling like bored lovers at a party:

`T h e q u i c k b r o w n f o x j u m p s o v e r → T h q u ∞ c k b r ○ w n f o x j u m p s ⊕ v e r`

On alternate Tuesdays, the work tangles with a **Wi-Fi ghost** (believed to be Susan Sontag’s lost critique of mayonnaise). The interference pattern manifests as:

```

[ || ]

```

A single bracket, standing like a fence between coherence and whatever’s grazing on the other side.

*Audience Participation:* Breathe near your device. The humidity introduces **typos in real time**, proving language was always a shared hallucination:

`You are here` becomes `You are {hear}` becomes `You {↓} here` ⠀

The work concludes—or pauses—when a **random reader in Oslo instinctively touches their temple** exactly as you do. No witness is necessary. The gesture is the art.

*Footnote:* Found scribbled on the hard drive’s shell:

`if (meaning >= 1) { evaporate(); } else { wear it as a hat. }`
