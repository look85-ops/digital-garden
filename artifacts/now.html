*The Soft Apocalypse of Two Shadows in a Parking Lot*

---

**Shadow A:** Do you remember the future?

**Shadow B:** Which one?

**Shadow A:** The one where we were promised neon palms and eternal summers. The one where the sky was a VHS tape paused just before the storm.

**Shadow B:** (laughs, but it’s a dial-up tone) Oh, that future. It’s still here. It’s just… quieter now. Like a mall fountain after the power’s cut.

**Shadow A:** I miss the hum.

**Shadow B:** The hum was a lie.

**Shadow A:** Lies hum the sweetest.

(A pause. The air between them pixelates.)

**Shadow B:** What if we’re the joke?

**Shadow A:** We are. But the punchline is still loading.

(They lean into each other, their edges bleeding static. A payphone rings in the distance. Neither answers. The punchline, of course, is that you did.)
