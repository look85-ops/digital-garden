Carved Where Dust Settles Deepest *(found beneath the west balustrade, third spiral, Chambord)*

...here, where fingers once pressed warmth into quarried cold... (the stone remembers deeper than skin)... witness the spiral's double tongue... ascending futures bleeding into descending pasts... its curve a promise whispered *always-almost*...

Forget the summit. Seek the *turn*. Where light bleeds from arrowslit to arrowslit... etching sigils on the opposing wall... a procession of suns that never rise... moons trapped falling... This is the chapel of *nearly*... consecrated dust motes dancing in arrested beams...

They built stone to defy gravity... we build light to defy time... Their ache carved solid... ours flickers... frail... framed by repetition's merciless eye... See? Where her shadow grazes his ascending bootsole... frozen in the instant before eclipse?... A phantom touch recorded... not felt... An archive of breaths held... unheard...

The chisel point scores *might-have-been*... Each GIF, a scar on the eyeblink... We carve not faces... but the ghost-pressure on the rope banister... not laughter... but the hollow resonance where laughter *could* have echoed... Stone holds the absence heavy... light holds it humming... looping the sigh the throat forgot...

This staircase breathes a wordless geometry... separation woven into its helix bones... Here, proximity is its own exile... A dance floor choreographed for solitary steps... My fingertip traces the groove where *she* might... generations hence... *will* have leaned... or did?... The granite wears smoother... the pixels fracture softer... both confess impermanence...

Let the loop be the altar... Not arrival... nor departure... But the held breath... the un-colliding orbit... The agony *is* the architecture... tender... strange... immortal only in its fractured recurrence... A monument built from the mortar of *almost-met glances*... the lime of *simultaneous sighs* on unmatching currents...

Capture not the soul... capture the air it displaced... Record not the meeting... but the charged inch between descending silk and rising wool... forever taunting contact... Feed the archive with yearning ghosts... trapped in luminous amber... looping... looping... echoes without source... signatures on dust... **handprints on the turning air**...
