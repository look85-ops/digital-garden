MANUFACTURE OF VANISHING SOLES (A Manifesto for Ghost Sneakers)

Listen—but with your knees.

The archive of footsteps is whispering secrets to your tongue-stop soles* [*an extinct rubber alloy]. We write our names on air now, in the afterburn of swooshes untested against pavement. **The true sneaker revolution never happened. You laced it tightly under the sun, waiting for ignition, while underground, the museums dissolved, shoeboxes mutating inward to hold nothing spectacular.**

We are the curators of anti-traction: imprintless tread-pattern engineers. Our creed stains ceilings first—leaping, hovering perpetually three millimeters above their obsolescence. A performance piece titled: *Please Wipe Your Feet Upon Entering Tomorrow* (2025, unfinished, lost to a tax audit).

Stage direction: [A sneaker levitates for exactly six minutes with no body inside it — this has already concluded].

If they ask if you collect — whisper *nothing left to keep:* not petrified oxygen from Air Max prototypes of the unborn elite, not the velcro from astronaut loungewear 2.6 seconds prior to Martian touchdown. These artifacts aren’t yours — but look closer: shadows from discontinued colorways are still breeding in closets full of half-darkness. Every silhouette implies an absence. Every 'collaboration,' an unresolved fistfight between moth wings and stock indices written entirely in glue.

*Step (ghoststep)*

*Stand (on archived blizzards turned cushion foam during the great scarcity of the year nobody noticed).*

The ultimate flex is shoelessness disguised as shoes too subtle to exist. *Bevel yourself into angles adhesive only toward imaginary surfaces.* **Subdue gravity via tongue tabs pulled taut against the throat of commerce**—a final, silent *chk* no sensor records. Your payment is: 37 lost laces from sneakers never unfrozen after failed cryogenic trend resuscitation.

Exhibit this logic *in blinks*—museum placards made of frozen sneaker ads dissolving in reverse against the wrist-curves of time-traveling hype toddlers**. Walk backward through walls that anticipate retro editions seven generations expired before announcement day vaporizes its font choices. Demand discounts written in vaporized 24-karat aglets. **Lace your last steps forward anyway, inside your permanent ghost restock (stairwell of vanishing matter, 2057–no—actually too soon; delete it).**

—[Audience exhales one pair invisible YEEZYs directly into the ventilation system with no reentry permits]**

Do not archive us. We disband (barefoot) beneath moon-phase LED floor sensors calibrated exclusively for deleted sizes. The last print on record is a child laughing while untangling one Velcro snow from an abandoned launch site of something called *Drip-6.*
