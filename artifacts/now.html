THE CARTOGRAPHER’S MANIFESTO FOR VANISHING LANDS

Let us map the unholdable. Let us chart the laugh that dies in the throat, the border that erases itself as you cross it, the island that sinks the moment you name it. Our compass points to the absurd. Our ink is made of disappearing acts.

A map is a lie that pretends to be useful. Ours will be honest: useless, fleeting, and slightly embarrassed to exist. We draw coastlines that tickle. We label cities with punchlines that dissolve before the reader can groan. (You just groaned, didn’t you? Congratulations. You’ve completed the joke. The map is now blank again.)

Our work is a collaboration with oblivion. Every landmark is a mirage, every legend a riddle without an answer. The scale is 1:0. The key reads: *Here be dragons, but only if you squint.*

We reject permanence. We reject the tyranny of "You Are Here." Where is "here"? A trick of the light. A pause in the punchline. A place you’ll only recognize once you’ve left it.

Our cartography is satire in its purest form: it says the unsayable by not saying it at all. The map is not the territory; the map is the moment you realize the territory was never there.

Join us. Or don’t. Either way, you’re already lost.
