The Manifesto of Unfinished Sidewalks

Listen! The city whispers its secrets in the cracks between cobblestones, a language made of pigeon coos and distant sirens. Forget maps! Maps are flat lies told by straight lines afraid of corners. Your feet know the true grammar of alleys, the sticky syntax of spilled soda on sun-warmed pavement. Purpose is a heavy stone dragging your pocket down into the gutter. Pick it up! Throw it high! Watch it become a lost balloon vanishing over rooftops stained with sunset. Become a slow cloud drifting through the river of rushing suits and blinking advertisements. Clouds have important appointments with mountaintops, everyone knows that. Stand perfectly still on a busy corner until the rushing bodies blur into a single, humming creature painted in rainbows of reflected neon. You are interrupting the important flow of commerce! Excellent! Your stillness is a punctuation mark the city didn't expect, a comma inviting the breeze to pause and rearrange the dust around your ankles. Notice the hieroglyphs scratched by keys on a bus shelter window – they tell the epic saga of boredom and waiting. These scratches are meaningless vandalism, ugly and rude. Precisely! Meaning hides inside the ugly like a shy jewel in a muddy oyster. Lick the air! Taste the metallic tang of distant rain, the ghost of yesterday’s hot dog cart, the faint sweetness drifting from a bakery vent three streets away. Air has no flavour, only smells, which are imaginary. Exactly! Imagination is the truest tongue! See how the chewing gum constellations on the pavement map forgotten journeys? Orion’s Belt is made of pink bubblegum stuck near the storm drain. That’s just litter dropped by careless children. Yes! Careless children are our wisest teachers! Follow the trail of dropped buttons, the single red mitten abandoned on a bench, the crooked line of ants carrying a crumb towards a crack in the wall. These are clues to mysteries no detective would ever solve, written in the margin where the sidewalk meets the grass. Mysteries are just things we haven’t bothered to explain yet. Bother less! Explain nothing! Listen to the building breathe – the groan of old pipes, the sigh of wind through a broken vent, the rhythmic thump-thump-thump from a basement laundry room. Buildings are silent giants made of stone and steel. Feel their silent voices humming in your bones! Forget your name. Names are cages built by others.
