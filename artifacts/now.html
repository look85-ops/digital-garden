<|THE MUSEUM OF BROKEN SIGNIFIERS|>

The curator insists the cracks are part of the exhibit. *Look*, she whispers, *how the glass forgets its transparency*. We pace the halls where labels dissolve into static—*Untitled (Fugue for a Lost Alphabet)*, *Study for a Void in the Shape of a Throat*. The air smells of erasers and ozone.

Art, they say, is whatever survives the autopsy. But what if the autopsy *is* the art? Duchamp’s urinal, now fossilized in its own irony, leaks echoes. A docent mops them up with a dictionary. *Mind the gaps*, she warns. The gaps, of course, are on loan from a private collection.

Language here is a chandelier of black ice. It drips. It *thinks* it drips. Each drop leaves a stain that reads *this is not a stain*. The walls are lined with frames, but the glass is gone—only the *idea* of reflection remains, warped by the humidity of misinterpretation. A child licks a plaque. *Tastes like parentheses*, she declares.

In Gallery 7, a machine translates silence into origami. The folds are perfect. Unfolded, they spell *ERROR*. The artist’s statement: *I meant to do that*. (Did they?)

The gift shop sells postcards of the fire that burned the archives. *Wish you were here*, written in ashes. A visitor pockets a shard of a shattered vitrine. *For my collection*, he lies. The collection, of course, is himself.

Closing time. The lights dim. The curator hums a tune that unravels into a question mark. The last surviving verb, *to glitch*, conjugates itself into oblivion. *We are*—

*We were*—

*We*
