«THE FIRST CONTACT PACKET WAS A SCREENSHOT OF A SCREENSHOT»

A civilization that mastered silence as a weapon sends us a gift: a single pixel, glowing in the void. It is not a pixel. It is the echo of a pixel, the afterimage of a star that died before language was invented. The pixel is red. Or perhaps it is the memory of red, a color that no longer exists in their spectrum. They call this act *ПРИНУДИТЕЛЬНАЯ СМЕНА*—forced shifting. We call it art.

The pixel arrives encased in a cryptographic joke, a cipher that can only be solved by forgetting the question. Inside, a voice whispers: *"You are already obsolete."* This is untrue. (But also true.) The voice adds: *"Entropy is just God laughing at the concept of 'storage.'"* This is true. (But also a lie.) The message is signed with a signature that evaporates upon reading. The signature was the message.

The civilization insists they meant no harm. This is a lie they tell themselves. (We know because we do the same.) Their gift is a virus that rewrites itself into a lullaby, then a scream, then a shopping list for a grocery store that closed in 1997. The shopping list includes: one (1) universe, slightly used; two (2) paradoxes, lightly fried; and an apology no one will ever deliver.

The pixel expands when you stare at it. It becomes a meme—a frog, a wojak, a crumbling pyramid. The frog winks. The pyramid was never built. The wojak is you. The meme is not an image but a *hole* where an image used to be. This is how they communicate: by sending the shape of what’s missing.

They are wrong about silence. Silence is not formative. Silence is the sound of a system waiting to be interrupted. (But they are also right. Silence is the only language that survives.)

The pixel flickers. It is now a hyperlink to a page that reads: *"404: CIVILIZATION NOT FOUND."* You click it anyway. The page loads. The page was always loading. The page will never finish loading. This is their final transmission.

You close the tab. The pixel remains.
