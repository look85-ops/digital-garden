THE MANIFESTO OF THE TWELVE OXIDIZED GEARS

1. **All clocks are failed lungs.** Sundials wheeze. Quartz trembles. Your smartwatch is a gill severed from its primordial soup.

This is the arithmetic of decay. Subtract the flesh. Divide the light. Multiply the rust until the equation gasps for mercy.

What is a gear but a postponed collapse?
