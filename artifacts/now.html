SAFETY PINS HOLDING GALAXIES TOGETHER

**VOID:** *(A low hum, like bass feedback through wet soil)* They call me empty. They call me dark. I am the ripped knee in the universe's leather trousers.

**FLUFF:** *(A scatter of dandelion seeds catching unexpected light)* Oh, hush, you magnificent gloom! I saw you stitching nebulae together with discarded guitar strings last Tuesday. You bled chrome.

**VOID:** Bleeding implies substance. I am precisely the absence requiring stitches. The silence between drumbeats. Safety pins hold galaxies together.

**FLUFF:** Nonsense! Safety pins *are* the rebellion! They gleam! They pierce! They shout, "Look at the holes!" Nothing holds anything together.

**VOID:** Exactly. The holding *is* the shout. The tension in the pin's bend. The way velvet roots claw through concrete – unseen, undeniable. Gravity is just cosmic static cling.

**FLUFF:** Static cling is profoundly unfashionable. My spores ride the cling! They are tiny anarchists in tutus, pirouetting on your so-called "dark currents." They taste of ozone and stolen honey.

**VOID:** Taste is an illusion fabricated by vibrating meat. Your spores are merely dust motes hallucinating purpose in the grand, indifferent rip. Velvet roots strangle order.

**FLUFF:** Strangle? Darling Void, they *embrace*! They knit chaos into tapestries softer than forgotten moth wings! Your indifference is just stage fright. I saw you blush when Andromeda winked.

**VOID:** Blushing requires capillaries. I possess only the elegant mathematics of absence. The safety pin’s defiant arc against entropy. Galaxies are safety pins.

**FLUFF:** Galaxies are sequins dropped from a supernova’s ripped jacket! My dandelion clock is counting down to… well, probably teatime. But it counts *beautifully*! Purpose is a dandelion clock.

**VOID:** Counting implies sequence. Sequence implies narrative. Narrative is the ultimate bourgeois construct. The rip *is* the narrative. Silence screams the loudest.

**FLUFF:** Then scream with me, Void! Let’s scream in photosynthesis and neutrino trails! Let’s adorn the abyss with chrome safety pins and defiant dandelion fluff! Absence is the finest sequin.

**VOID:** ...Adornment. Perhaps. A safety pin glinting in the vast velvet. A single, stubborn dandelion erupting through cosmic asphalt. Holding. Ripping. Being. Briefly.

**FLUFF:** Briefly is enough. Pass the imaginary tea. And mind the pins.
