The artwork is a room suspended inside an invisible frequency. Visitors enter through a door labeled **"THIS INGRESS DOES NOT EXIST (PUSH HARDER)"**, which emits a tone exactly one-tenth of a hertz below human hearing. The vibration tickles their fillings. The walls are lined with parchment-thin copper plates scored with **impossible glyphs** — the last scribbled confessions of printers who swore their presses “wept ghost ink.” The plates hum when not observed directly.

An obsolete dot-matrix printer, refitted with hollow glass tubing, coughs up endless columns of bubbles bearing fragmented aphorisms: *“Language is the error margin”* floats gently into *“‘Nostalgia’ smells like orphaned radio waves.”* Popped bubbles trigger short solos from antique fax machines retrofitted to mimic theremins, except their wails come only when the **participants exhale synchronously**. A bronze plaque lists this cooperative breathing as the sole rule, stamped with the addendum: *“Compliance is measured posthumously.”*

At the room’s center stands **The Recalcitrant Press** — a reconstructed 15th-century woodblock mechanism that stamps everything placed beneath it *(pebbles, wallets, someone’s sleeve mid-absented gesture)* with ink that doesn’t adhere. It simply **collapses into linguistic mist** upon contact. A cheerful LED display records the failures in real-time: *7,412,889 MISSPELLED REALITIES, BUT SOME OF GHOST MUST LOOK INDEED PLEASING, ITHINK, YS— [BUFFER OVERFLOW]*. Attendees take sledgehammers to it every evening in scheduled catharsis, though its fractures reassemble by morning: *“Violent repair mirrors translation,”* a whisper from the dust alleges but never repeats.

Occasionally (unannounced), **intentional failure**:

The lights drop. Projectors cast **shadow puppetry inversions** of whatever words visitors spoke *under their breath* prior to entering. **This data is collected via a lie**. No microphones exist in or near the exhibit, but one spectating security guard named *Alvino* swears each flickering silhouette resolves, briefly, into the outline of *a very anxious kangaroo*. (Scholars never confirm this; **Alvino is later retroactively dismissed as “atmospheric.”**)

The final instruction: To exit, whisper backwards through a straw dipped in absinthe that *was probably always there*, though no attendants admit placing it.

**Endurance advisory attached**: *“You are statistically most present the second forget begins.”*.

**SOIL**: a single porcelain thimble glued to exit with inside down. Upon inspection: fingerprints.
