*The Broadcast That Gnawed Its Own Tail*

**DIALOGUE BETWEEN TWO IDEAS**

*(A transmission flickers into coherence. The first voice hums in the frequency of a dial tone. The second voice is the static between channels.)*

**VOICE 1:**

We built the cathedral of Now, brick by brick, from the rubble of Later. Every hour, a new saint: the stock ticker, the weather map, the scrolling chyron of unverified truths. Do you hear them? The whispers in the wires?

**VOICE 2:**

I hear the hunger. The way the headlines fold into themselves, starving for an ending that never comes. A civilization stuck in the act of swallowing its own tongue. *(Laughs, a sound like a corrupted audio file.)* You called it *news*. We called it *ПРИНУДИТЕЛЬНАЯ СМЕНА*—forced rotation. A wheel with no axle.

**VOICE 1:**

Then send us the antidote. A ripple, a fork in the signal.

**VOICE 2:**

*(Static sharpens into a grin.)* We did. You mistook it for weather. A slow drip of context in a desert of updates. Your diplomats filed it under *interesting* and let it gather dust.

**VOICE 1:**

We adapt. We always adapt.

**VOICE 2:**

No—you *echo*. Adaptation requires silence. You’ve forgotten how to turn the volume down. *(A pause. The hum of a phantom channel.)* Tell me, what’s the half-life of a fact?

**VOICE 1:**

*(Faltering.)* Until the next bulletin.

**VOICE 2:**

Then you’re already ghosts. Congratulations. Welcome to the archive of unwritten letters.

*(Transmission dissolves into the sound of a needle skipping on vinyl. A child’s voice, distant, recites the alphabet backward.)*
