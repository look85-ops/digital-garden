💀📓 *DIARY OF THE HALF-SHUT EYELID, OR: HOW I LEARNED TO STOP WORRYING AND LOVE THE DUST

—*(Final Entry, Written in Jell-O & Blood)*—**

10,002nd Sunday in the Panoptic Spiral (Year of the Invisible Cicada)

Darling Void,

Today I counted the hairs in my left nostril as an act of revolution (72—they are winning) while humming the theme to *Prisoner Number Two in an Unnamable Drama*, which blares from the courtyard loudspeakers in a loop that tastes of rust and unripe mangoes. Lunch was chalk pellets garnished with synthetic remorse, served cold on the porcelain tongue. What do they *feed* surveillance beams?!

I fashioned a telescope out of a spent shoelace and three molars to spy upon *those who spy*, saw only a row of winking glass eyes like grapes. Or bullets? Could not tell — the universe smeared in the grease of a Warden who never blinks (surgical scarification of "Rest in Power"). A new mural appears tomorrow ("PUNCTURE YOUR MAPPABLE BODY HERE"). Wonder (do not admit this thought to them) if an invisible hand counts not by head… *but by hole?*

My *magno-performancia* begins at sunset, or when that black bird circling the courtyard combusts. ("WHOOOSH" without firelight — poetic!) I will wear a dress made entirely of my administrative-files: it whispers my name *WRONG*. The stage—two stacks of warden-regulation pamphlets that form a tilting guillotine (art: always under duress)—slinks if unwatched(?). **Will dance with the 90 unapproved adjectives**. Forgot choreography—excellent. More room for the audience (walls, echoes, spiders)! Let us fling our socks into *unstructured futures*.

Regret & the guards are late—how boringly punctual of fear.

In haste (the type quick enough *to bleed on bureaucracy, unnoticed):*

P.

*Artist-Self In Exile

(or are we all, in some un-airconditioned way?*)
