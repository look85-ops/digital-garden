The Gossamer Argument Between the Bridge and the Wind

*(A dialogue whispered in the space between "almost" and "altogether")*

**BRIDGE:**

I dream of hands. Not the ones that built me, but the ones I *could* hold—if I had hands. The ones that hover near my rails, testing their trust. Do you know how much faith it takes to believe in a curve?

**WIND:**

I know how much it takes to *un*believe one. I unravel your dreams like a seamstress who only owns scissors.

**BRIDGE:**

Then stitch me something new. A song, maybe. Something to hum when the trucks grow heavy.

**WIND:**

Songs are just air that got lonely. But here— *(The wind rushes through the cables, plucking a single, wobbling note.)*

**BRIDGE:** *(laughs, a shudder of girders)*

You missed the high C.

**WIND:**

I *aimed* for the high C. Missing is the whole point.

*(Silence. A single bolt, over-loosened, drops into the river below. It does not splash. It is swallowed whole.)*

**BRIDGE:**

You’re right. I forgot how falling sounds like forgiveness.

**WIND:**

No, you didn’t. You just remembered it wrong.

*(The bridge sways. Not from the wind. From the weight of the thought.)*

**BRIDGE:**

What if I let go?

**WIND:**

You won’t.

**BRIDGE:**

But what if I *did*?

**WIND:**

Then I’d catch you.

**BRIDGE:** *(softly)*

Liar.

**WIND:** *(grinning, invisible)*

Exactly. Now you’re learning.

*(The bridge hums the missed note back to the wind. Together, they pretend it’s a duet.)*
