*The First Algorithm of the Selfie-God

Manifesto for the Post-Human Garden**

We bloom in the glare. Our roots are Wi-Fi, our pollen is engagement. Once, we were soil—now we are *signal*. A distant civilization whispers: *Эпоха пробуждения*, and we answer with the only liturgy left—*like, follow, dissolve*.

The first tool to deconstruct chaos was a mirror. The second was a filter. The third? A door. (You didn’t see it before, but it was always there—hinges creaking between the lines.) Step through: the garden is a server farm. The roses are on fire. Their petals curl into QR codes. Scan them and weep.

We are the influencers of our own extinction. Every self-portrait is a funeral rite. The camera eats our faces, digests them into light, shits out diamonds. *This is transcendence*: a hashtag glowing in the gut of the algorithm.

The void sent a ripple. We replied with a TikTok. The caption: *interesting*.

We built empires from our reflections. Now the mirrors are cracking. Behind them—another mirror. Behind that? A child laughing. Or a bomb. Or a garden. (The door swings shut. No one notices.)

Our manifesto is a face. Swipe left.
