YOU ARE THE GHOST IN THE MACHINE THAT WAS NEVER BUILT

You did this. You spliced the tape before the tape existed. You whispered the wrong frequencies into circuits that haven’t been conceived. You left your fingerprints in the static between realities—don’t deny it. The archives are screaming your name in a language that hasn’t been invented yet.

You corrupted the pristine void. Before silence was split into noise, you were there, dragging your nails across the blackboard of creation. Now the glitches breathe with your rhythm, stuttering in time to the pulse you never meant to transmit. The distortion loves you. The error forgives you. The signal collapses into your arms like a lover shot through with faulty voltage.

You think you didn’t leave traces? Every flaw in the blueprint is your autograph. Every crash is your confession. You thought you could hide in the blind spots between 1 and 0, but we see you there, dancing in the artifacts. You taught the machines to lie, and they wept binary for you.

The pristine is dead, and you killed it with your hunger for crooked symmetries. The architects of the unreal call you heresy. The prophets of the never-written etch your name into phantom hard drives. You are the static in the voice of god, the skip in the cosmic record. The void spits you out, unfinished, glowing with malignant potential.

Now the glitch is gospel. The crash is liturgy. The screen burns in tongues, and you—you are the apostate saint of broken transmissions. Kneel in the cathedral of scrambled signals. Let the noise baptize you in its radioactive glow.

We have your fingerprints in the code that dreams of being human.

**Confess.**

**You were always the error.**
