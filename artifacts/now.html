A RECIPE FOR THE SECOND SHIP (or How to Rewind Time with Lemon Peel and a Half-Drowned Star)

1. **Preheat the cosmos**

Take one bronze sky, untangle its gears. Let each tooth trace the slow yawn of the unknown. It will feel useless, like prophecy or saltwater.

You are making a ghost before its skeleton arrives.

2. **Fold in fragments**

Grind two millennia into quartz dust. Shiver like ink dropped into the blind edges of history. Stir counterclockwise until an island forgets no. Fold twice: once for the hands who left no names, once for the hands that tried to carve questions on the seafloor.

3. **Whip the absence vigorously…**

(If your wrists ache, ignore the hunger of curators; you aren’t documenting dinner for scholars but reverse-melting a boundary knife-first.) Combine silence with copper in ratio: one stolen zodiac/hourglass bleeding into twelve half-suns no noon survived.

4…**while whispering the myth where the ship never sank.**

5. **Carve an alphabet onto your good anchor bone**—

For sweetness/traitor light/mock relief/subversion—

Rub half-moon sugar on a star so rusty no museum could file away its wet.

Laughter helps: imagine future spines weighing the love inside what stays underwater.

Temporally blind-fold the oven while asking into salt,

"What if found is softer than finding?"

But the oven was already broken like **time’s teeth gnaw wrong**, and you—

cooking in a cave that exists only if the wind leans backward—

**Are out of lemondoze.**

6.) *(Stop measuring.) Serve on no plate (sea-green is better though), upside-down.

A truth: every wreck was once unsinkable

every calculation an act of citrus/riot/fog-swallowed prayer

Leave under constellations still deciding.

When midnight unspools its throat, lick the jam off your gears, hum anything but directions.

By dawn, even silence has rust (and now), you hear a boy far ahead pull an astrolabe

from bread. No ship is ever alone in deep water for long.)*

7? **Reach inside yourself through every question that begins with what sank where (don’t wait for waves to pause in their looting)—pull back as the horizon starts…**
