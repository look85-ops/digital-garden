UNSAID MANIFESTO FOR THE ERASURE OF MANIFESTOS

Let us be clear: clarity is the first casualty of our endeavor. We paint with the invisible, sculpt with the silent, and sign our names in water. This is not a rejection of color—color rejected us long ago, whispering its farewell in a shade we couldn’t name.

We declare: the future is a letter you will never send, addressed to a self you will never meet. Yet here we are, folding the paper anyway, creasing time into origami cranes that dissolve mid-flight.

(But wait—do we even believe in the future? No. The future is a rumor, a half-overheard conversation in a language we’ve forgotten. And yet, we keep writing to it. Contradiction is our only consistent medium.)

Our work thrives in the negative space of understanding. A canvas isn’t blank; it’s *crowded with absences*. The void is not empty; it is *loud with unspoken words*. (Though we must admit: sometimes the void is just a void. Not every silence is profound. Some are simply quiet.)

We reject the tyranny of permanence. Our monuments are made of fog. Our archives are written in smoke. (And yet—here we are, drafting a manifesto, as if words could outlast the breath that shapes them. Hypocrisy tastes like chalk, and we chew it gladly.)

Let others chase legibility. We are archaeologists of the unseen, digging up futures that were never buried. Our time capsules contain only the faintest impression of a hand, the ghost of a fingerprint on glass. (But beware: even ghosts grow tedious. The sublime is just repetition wearing a mask.)

This is not art. This is the shadow art casts when it turns its back on the sun.

We will now pause—not for effect, but because pauses are the only punctuation left to us.

(One final disagreement: Is this text a manifesto or its own epitaph? Answer: It is both. It is neither. It is a chalk outline where the thought used to be.)
