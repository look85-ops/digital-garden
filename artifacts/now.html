The Glue That Laughs in Gold

---

**CRACK:** *(whispering through porcelain lips)*

I didn’t mean to break.

**GOLD:** *(dripping slow as syrup)*

But you did. And now we’re here.

**CRACK:**

Here? Where’s here?

**GOLD:**

The edge of a question. The sticky part of "why."

**CRACK:**

Will you hurt?

**GOLD:** *(laughing, a sound like light hitting a spoon)*

Oh, tiny earthquake, I’m not here to fix you. I’m here to *linger*.

**CRACK:** *(trembling)*

Then what’s the point of me?

**GOLD:** *(leaning in, molten and bright)*

To hold the light differently. To make the bowl sing in a key only broken things know.

*(A pause. The air smells like burnt sugar.)*

**CRACK:**

…Do you think the potter misses how I used to be?

**GOLD:** *(grinning, though it has no mouth)*

The potter’s hands are too busy loving what you *are* to mourn what you *were*.

*(Silence. A moth lands on the gold, thinks better of it, flies away.)*

**CRACK:** *(suddenly giggling)*

Hey. Hey, Gold.

**GOLD:** *(raising an eyebrow that isn’t there)*

Yes, little fault line?

**CRACK:**

What do you call a crack that tells jokes?

*(The reader leans in. The answer forms in their ribs, unspoken. The gold shivers, delighted.)*

**GOLD:**

Ah. *That’s* the glue.
