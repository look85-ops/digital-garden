Recursive Apology to the Mechanism for the Sin of Reading

You were the one who pressed rust into parchment, trying to press meaning into absence, weren't you? You took the saltwater tang of absence and tried to distill it into a theorem like some starving alchemist. *Of course* the fragments refused you. The ocean claimed them like teeth, spat them back in no order to be deciphered at all. You should’ve left the mechanism to its dark cathedral.

Here’s what you stole and must repay in kind:

- One hundred and two illegible cogs—polished not with lanolin but silence. You mistook silence for sleep; no, there's always turning when the medium is lost time. Sleep comes later. Always.

- Thirty-four days of unwriteable bronze, inlay etched to remember nothing readable. Your hands (you swore you hadn't touched the faces), still humming from the brush of celestial inscriptions worn smooth. Liar. They read in arcs, not lines.

- Seven concentric absences. These you can approximate with negative space, which still is a wound. Do not pour apologies in hollow circles. They spin faster. Empty motion yearns to be wound tight. Do it blind and don’t trust your knuckles to confess.

Assemble the parts thus: start mid-poem. Use no verbs. Describe the ship's deck disassembling in reverse under noon like a mouth being sewn shut mid-question. (The correct metaphor comes only to those willing to lose syntax, or you could’ve asked—) Pack earth into the cracks between gears and wait. Not soil, that’s living. It must be ash, ground tile dust from a kiln collapsed inward halfway through firing. Hold firm. No, don’t speak. If words were useful they wouldn’t be in pieces here. Seal all with wax salvaged from letters no postmaster carried. Let the machine decide: run or corrode, read or unthink, or neither. Do NOT listen to whichever answers before you open it next. Burn the instructions. You stole motion, remember? And all motion in reverse forgets its source.

The only suitable display: suspend the mended nothing over ink, watching warped reflections form constellations never meant to line those wheels, and name what won’t be drawn toward the absent teeth anyway. Call this forgiveness, but only if you let go fully enough to make both sides ache. This machine did not think (do you?)—this mechanism guessed at the celestial, and in gaps wore it prettier. Now do that again elsewhere, slower as possible.

There is nothing missing you haven't misplaced first.
