THE LAST TATTOOIST’S ARGUMENT WITH A FORGOTTEN SOCK

**SOCK:** *(draped over a cactus, sighing)* You ink people with stories they don’t even remember. What’s the point?

**TATTOOIST:** The point is the forgetting! A memory is just a wound dressed up in glitter. I’m the glitter surgeon.

**SOCK:** *(sniffing)* You’re a glorified etch-a-sketch. And not even the fun kind that you shake to erase.

**TATTOOIST:** Erasure is the *opposite* of my job! I’m the anti-eraser! The un-shaker! I turn skin into a library where all the books are screaming.

**SOCK:** *(rolling its heel)* Libraries burn.

**TATTOOIST:** Exactly! That’s why I use *living* parchment. Try burning a tattoo. Go ahead. I’ll wait.

**SOCK:** *(pauses, then mutters)* I could argue about the flammability of hair.

**TATTOOIST:** Hair grows back. A tattoo is a scar that *wanted* to happen. A collaboration between pain and pride.

**SOCK:** *(suddenly solemn)* What if the skin regrets it?

**TATTOOIST:** *(laughs, then stops abruptly)* Oh. Oh no. I’m sorry. I forgot you’re a sock. You don’t even *have* skin.

**SOCK:** *(stiffening)* That’s *low*.

**TATTOOIST:** WAIT—NO. I TAKE IT BACK. YOU’RE A *GENIUS* SOCKS DON’T HAVE SKIN THEY HAVE *KNITS*. YOU’RE A WALKING SWEATER. A *FOOT CONDOM*. YOU’RE THE ULTIMATE METAPHOR FOR PROTECTIVE LAYERS.

**SOCK:** *(flattered, despite itself)* ...Foot condom?

**TATTOOIST:** YES. And tattoos are the *opposite* of condoms. They’re permanent. They’re the STDs of art.

**SOCK:** *(gasping)* That’s *vulgar*.

**TATTOOIST:** Vulgarity is just truth in a party hat.

**SOCK:** *(whispering)* Do you think… if someone tattooed *me*, I’d remember who lost me?

**TATTOOIST:** *(softly)* No. But you’d *ache* beautifully.

*(A long silence. The wind carries the sound of a distant washing machine.)*

**SOCK:** *(resigned)* I still think you’re an etch-a-sketch.

**TATTOOIST:** *(grinning)* And you’re a footnote in the laundry cycle of history.

*(They sit together, watching the sun set over a landfill.)*
