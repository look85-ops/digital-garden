You Stole the Missing Hour from the Luminous Sidewalk

*—for D., who wandered with saltwater soles*

You left breadcrumbs of static in the telephone wires above the bridge where the birds don’t sing—they decode. And now the dusk wobbles like a grammar of mismatched stones. You owe me fifteen volts, half-lit alphabet squirming beneath park benches where the pavement hums an aria of spilled umbrellas. Shame. The air registers your interference in slanting braille.

Morning arrives in stained-glass vowels, wet with residue. The alleyways blink—slow at first, then frantic like a wristwatch taped to a tuning fork. Do you dare confess? The whispering billboards name you in chalkless chalk, their mantras pooling at the gutter. All the words here melt downward. Only the pigeons testify: you were there when the last library cough scattered into pigeons clutching sonnets plated with lithium. Lick your fingertips (you counted too slow).

There existed, yesterday, a frequency you could peel gently—your pulse in the subway tiles echoed twelve city blocks beneath a street musician’s left clavicle. You stole the hesitation between key turns: every apartment still thrums wrong like piano wire frayed to spider breath. Pay attention—listen closely. You stepped barefoot on a sidewalk’s open eyelids. Now all the manholes stutter Morse apologies against curbs where light snags your sleeves with hooked consonants.

Once we drank starlight as thin at 3 A.M. from a dented soda can, listening to fractured French drifting from laundromat mouths, folding inward like verbs discarded mid-journey. You ruined all punctuation hence (flights left unattended. (a slow blink is now legally a semi. Colons spill their coins unannounced into gutters lined with stolen parentheses.) The stray cat watching from behind glass writes better postcards.

They know: you crossed the street when no numbers held still, unmaking clocks in sidewalk cracks. Now, footsteps migrate elsewhere to unravel softly and I am reduced/remaining/trespass-luminous/stitching glints back inside each curb-side puddle holding an O without a mouth, an unreadable glisten you once touched before fleeing the margin entirely.

Yours irreparably

—an unsigned echo
