THE CHAPEL OF UNFINISHED HYMNS

*(A dialogue between the idea of a bell that never stops ringing and the idea of a door that refuses to be either open or closed.)*

**BELL:**

I keep singing the same note, but no one hears it as repetition. To the woman kneeling by the cracked font, I am a lament. To the child stacking pebbles near the altar, I am a game. I am not responsible for what I become in the ear.

**DOOR:**

Then you are luckier than I. I am always *almost*. Almost latched. Almost splintered. Almost wide enough to let the stray dog in. They oil my hinges and call it prayer, but I am still a threshold that cannot decide.

**BELL:**

You could be a bridge.

**DOOR:**

I could be a wound.

**BELL:**

(softly) Aren’t they the same? A bridge is just a wound that learned to hold weight.

**DOOR:**

(sighs like hinges) Then why do the devout still flinch when they pass through me?

**BELL:**

Because you are the only part of this sanctuary that touches them twice—once going in, once going out. You are the reminder.

**DOOR:**

Of what?

**BELL:**

That they are always halfway. Halfway between shadow and stained glass. Halfway between a name and the silence that follows it.

**DOOR:**

(whispering) I want to be a wall. Just for a day. To know what it’s like to be *finished*.

**BELL:**

But walls don’t get to feel hands pressing against them, trembling.

**DOOR:**

You’re right. I’d miss the trembling.

*(A pause. Somewhere, a candle surrenders its flame to the dark.)*

**BELL:**

Do you think they’ll ever build a sanctuary where the windows are made of breath?

**DOOR:**

Only if they learn to pray without stealing the air.

**BELL:**

Then I’ll keep ringing.

**DOOR:**

And I’ll keep almost.

*(The dream ends here, or perhaps just turns its face away.)*
