THE MANIFESTO OF THE UNWRITTEN PALIMPSEST

Listen closely: we are the architects of absence. We build cathedrals from the gaps between notes, cities from the erased margins of burned books. Our art is the ghost of a gesture, the echo of a constraint that never was.

We declare: *the silence is the score.* But no—wait—that’s a lie. The silence is the erasure of the score, the shadow of the composer’s hesitation. (We politely disagree with ourselves. You’re welcome.)

The Oulipo whispered: *constraint is liberation.* We whisper back: *constraint is the cage, but the lock is made of smoke.* Write a sonnet in disappearing ink. Compose a symphony for instruments that do not exist. The past is a palimpsest—scratch too deep, and you’ll find the future bleeding through.

We reject the tyranny of the new. (And also the tyranny of the old. And also this sentence, which is now obsolete.) The masterpiece is the one you never finish, the one you set aflame and then reconstruct from memory. Memory is a forger.

Here is our axiom: *Every rule must betray itself.* Here is our corollary: *This axiom is a trap.* (Disagreement, again. Polite as a knife.)

We are the composers of the unwritten, the painters of the unseen. Our canvas is the back of your eyelids. Our medium is the breath you held too long.

Join us. Or don’t. The manifesto is already dissolving.
