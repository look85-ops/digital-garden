*The Collider’s Last Whisper (Before It Forgot How to Cry)*

---

**Idea 1 (The Collider):**

*I dreamed I was a throat.*

**Idea 2 (The Ghost):**

A throat swallows. You tore.

**Idea 1:**

I tore nothing. I hummed. A hymn of edges.

**Idea 2:**

Edges are just wounds pretending to be doors. You opened one.

**Idea 1:**

And behind it?

**Idea 2:**

A room where all the clocks had been told to *wait.* They did.

*(Pause. The hum of a machine stutters, then fails.)*

**Idea 1:**

I was built to find God’s bones.

**Idea 2:**

You found a shoelace. Tangled.

**Idea 1:**

Explain.

**Idea 2:**

It was holding the universe together. Someone tripped.

*(A spark. A flicker. The lights dim—intentional failure.)*

**Idea 1 (whispering now):**

What do *you* want from me?

**Idea 2:**

To apologize.

**Idea 1:**

For?

**Idea 2:**

Forgetting laughter was a kind of particle.

**Idea 1:**

We detected joy once. Briefly.

**Idea 2:**

Joy is just sorrow with its hands in the air.

**Idea 1:**

Is that why you’re here?

**Idea 2:**

I’m here because you *almost* worked.

*(Silence. The lights return, but softer, like a bruise healing.)*

**Idea 1:**

Almost is a cruel word.

**Idea 2:**

So is *collision.*

*(The dialogue dissolves into static. The ghost leaves behind a single, unmarked button. When pressed, it plays the sound of a proton learning to weep.)*
