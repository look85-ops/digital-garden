An Uncooking of the Infinite

Begin with the memory of a melody lost in its own forgetting. Do not write it down. Instead, carve the gaps between its notes into the spine of an unwritten novel*. Let silence seep through the binding—like "the arterial bleed of reverse time" [1]—until the pages dissolve into vellum mist.

( *A footnote confesses this will not resurrect anything, least of all you. Carry on. )

**Instructions:**

1. **Gather remnants.** The frayed edge of an oboe reed never played. Wax from candles that burned too inward. Four grams of disintegrated metronome springs. Comb through abandoned concert halls at 3:33 AM. Kneel and collect the dust. Ignite none of it—ashes are too honest in mourning.

2. **Fold entropy into thirds.** Crease each layer like resignation:

— one part the unlistenability of rain

— one part abandoned radiowave lacerations

— one part swallowed apologies dissolving like "subatomic pirouettes" (Mauvais, *On the Collapse of Grammars*, 211X)

Marinate beneath the arrhythmia of celestial bodies drifting apart. *Note:* Do not refrigerate; preserve at the room of the room your first lover left.

3. **Serve tepid under tilted light.** Arrange portions as if a wake for sound that hasn’t yet died. Garnish with unplucked violin strings suspended in aspic. Optional but encouraged: leave voids for the guest’s phantom-limb gestures to complete.

You will inevitably fail.

**Final Directions:** Allow the dish to curdle beautifully near a window where gravity grows anxious. If asked to describe this meal, recite Euclid to geese. No answers required, but if one arrives inverted—knock thrice beneath January.
