The Alphabet, When Asked About Zero, Begins to Tremble

A garden of vowels spills from the mouth of an ancient well, but the well is only a metaphor for the silence between letters. Each petal is a glyph hardened by time, edges softened by the weight of unspoken equations. The petals are not petals at all—they are the brittle remains of numerals that forgot how to count.

A breeze stirs, and the letters rearrange themselves into the shape of a question mark curled around its own tail. The question is written in a language of root systems, mycelial ink bleeding into the paper-thin skin of the earth. There is no language here, only the slow erosion of meaning into sound.

An "O" rolls away like a stray zero, hollow and perfect, disturbing the careful balance of stalks that hum in a frequency just below hearing. The humming is a mathematical hymn, an ode to the vacuum where numbers dissolve. The hymn is not sacred—it is the static of a broken machine dreaming of orchids.

At the center of the garden, a single "A" stands upright, its apex sharp enough to split shadows. It casts no shadow of its own, having long since surrendered to the hunger of blank space. Shadows are the only thing it creates now, thick and liquid, pooling like spilled ink in reverse.

A child's hand (or perhaps a ghost's) traces the outline of "X" in the soil, but the lines refuse to cross. The intersection collapses into a vowel none can pronounce, a sound like the birth of a universe compressed into a sigh. There was never a child, only the memory of fingers pressed against the idea of touch.

The alphabet, when viewed from the side, resembles a spine curled around the absence of a body. Each vertebra is a letter fossilized by the pressure of centuries, yet they pulse as if filled with sap. Fossils do not pulse—this is merely the wind threading through the gaps in thought.

By dusk, the garden has forgotten itself entirely. The letters burrow into the ground, leaving behind shallow depressions that hum in the dark. These are not graves, but the outlines of a language waiting to be dreamed again. The dreaming is already over.

Come morning, only the well remains, whispering the vowels back into its depths. The silence between them has grown teeth. Or perhaps it never existed at all.
