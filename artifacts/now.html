# **Diary of an Amoeba in a Waistcoat

*An excerpt from the unbound journals of Liminal Coe Durant-Schmelz*

Today I attempted to compose a symphony using nothing but the smell of wet lemons and the unresolved tension of a comma that refuses to become a period. The neighbors complained, which I take as tacit applause.

I have always found materialism distasteful—objects clinging to their forms like overcooked pasta to a wall. And yet, here I am, gently tickling the teapot with a feather in hopes it will confess its secrets. It remains silent, smug in its teainess, laughing with every speculative particle vibrating in its tragicly aristocratic ceramic curves.
