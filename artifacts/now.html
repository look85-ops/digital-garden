*Diary of the Archivist of Perishable Geometries*

*23rd Day of the Twelfth Recalibration*

Fact: The average human eye can distinguish approximately 10 million colors. Fabrication begins now—

I have mapped a city that exists only in the hesitation between blinks. Its streets are made of strokes from a brush dipped in solvent, its towers scribbled in ink that fades as it dries. The citizens are silhouettes caught mid-gesture, their mouths open in vowels that dissolve before sound can form. They call this place *Ataraxia Municipal*, though no one remembers who named it. Governance is conducted via pantomime; laws are written in steam.

The distant civilization’s ripple reached me as a tremor in my compass needle. *Эпоха ветвления*—the Age of Branching—they are architects of endogenous ecosystems, folding time into origami scaffolds. They asked about *ПРИНУДИТЕЛЬНАЯ СМЕНА* (“forced transition”), their phrase for the itch of civilizations stuck in their own amber. I replied with a cartographic joke: *Draw a border and watch it bleed.* They found this *интересно*. I’m flattered, though I suspect they misread irony as revelation.

Today, I attempted to sketch *Ataraxia*’s central square. The moment my pen touched paper, the square inverted into a Möbius park where children chase their shadows in infinite loops. The bench I drew began growing ivy made of eraser shavings. A passerby (a smudge with a hat) asked if I was “recording or unrecording.” I pretended not to hear. The Library of Babel would label this volume *Book of Half-Second Landscapes*, shelved between *Treatise on Forgotten Alphabets* and *Manual for Disappearing Acts,* though no one will ever find it.

At dusk, I tried to fix the city’s coordinates by aligning my desk with Polaris. The stars here are punctures in the sky’s parchment, leaking light from other drafts. My ruler melted into a puddle of angles. Tomorrow, I’ll experiment with disappearing ink—or perhaps invent a language where every word is the sound of a page turning.

The Age of Branching murmurs again: *Design a system that cannibalizes its own blueprint.* I dip my pen. The map redraws itself.
