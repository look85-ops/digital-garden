# **Archi(gh)ost

The river was never here. We named it anyway, baptized steel ribs in the hollow where water *might* have whispered, had we tilted history (slightly) south. Now the bridge—let's call it that—hums *a ghost-tuned A-flat*, its suspension cables gleaming like the spines of unwritten books.*

We strung messages in ceramic eggs, glazed them against a rainfall scheduled for a century we've since canceled. Inside each, a strip of film of a man burning census records and laughing. He whispers around the emulsion: *"The future is a palindrome no one has the tongue to pronounce."†*

At midnight, the archivists arrive with their pockets full of extinct barometers, their lips chapped from sealing envelopes addressed *To Who Isn’t Looking.* We document the stains left by ink that never adhered to paper. Our pens scratch holes in the air—small, deliberate, useless as sutures.*

The edge curls. Time, bored, flips the hourglass (an antique, obviously; sand went out of fashion the moment we started selling silence by the kilogram).*

Under amber-tinted glass in a room without doors: a model of the last city—spun sugar & lithium filaments, brittle. Notice the bridges that connect *(but only halfway)* , the towers designed for echoes without sound. One plaque reads *FAILURE IN PROGRESS,* another, a scratched-out *WHEN.*

A child’s red balloon drifts across the ruin-blueprints and sticks, like a punctuation error. We let it swell there between "maybe" and "never." Someone laughs like a switchblade opening.

The curator’s manifesto, found in static, folds into origami shrapnel before dissolving along every axis we tried so hard to straighten.

Now come the visitors (did you see them coming?), eyes full of keys for doors nailed permanently—*charmingly!*—askew.

(*from *Unbound Theses Beneath A Pinhole Sky* by Alchemy Index Corp., 20██, footnote redacted by mutual disagreement.)*
