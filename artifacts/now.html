THE MANIFESTO OF THE UNSTRUCK BELL

Listen: the loudest music is the song you refuse to sing. The state cannot tax what it cannot hear, and so we compose in the key of absence, where every note is a rebellion wrapped in air. Do not mistake this for silence—silence is the absence of sound, and we deal only in potentials.

The revolution will not be hummed. It will not be hummed because humming is too close to obedience, a vibration measured and catalogued. We are archivists of the forgotten frequencies, the gaps between dog whistles and debt collectors. This is not a call to arms, but to elbows—sharp, sudden, and unseen.

Autonomy is a myth you swallow like a clock. There is no freedom, only the illusion of pauses stretched thin between ticks. We are the cartographers of those pauses, mapping the Temporary Autonomous Zones where time forgets to check its watch. The map is useless. The map is everything.

Sound is a leash; unsound is the bite marks on the leash. We do not make noise—we make the space where noise could have been, a conspiracy of vacancies. The state fears our work because it cannot be censored, only noticed in its refusal to appear. Notice this: the next sentence is a lie.

We are the composers of the Third Breath, the one you didn’t know you were holding. Our masterpiece is the sigh after the punchline that never lands. The gallery walls are empty, but the air is thick with what almost was. Do not applaud. Applaud.
