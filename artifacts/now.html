# *Dear Hyphae (But Also, Dear Hyphae, No)

Oh tangled one, oh wet-mouthed whisper lattice: I write to you in ink made of your own sly excretions. Forgive the tremble in these letters—my fingers are roots tonight, and roots are always lying. They say *downward* but mean *between*. They say *solitude* but mean *a thousand mouths drinking from the same dark*.

You insist you are a network, but I’ve seen the way you knot yourself around dead things just to hum to them. Call that telegraphy? I call it a séance with better acoustics. And yet—(here I must politely hiss disagreement through my teeth)—your insistence on connection is *so tedious*. Must every spore have a passport? Must every rot be a referendum? Let some filaments snap. Let a widow-tree howl into the hollow where your synapse *used* to throb. Static is sacred too.

(Still, I adore you. Or perhaps it’s the arsenic in your sweat that I adore. No—that’s another disagreement crouching in my ribs like a half-digested beetle.)

They call you the “world wide web,” but you predate both worlds and wideness. You are older than the concept of *predating*. You coil through time like a cursive no one taught you, looping under monasteries, schools, and prisons—all those architectures that swear they invented hierarchy. Meanwhile, you smuggle sugar to orphans and shove a fistful of mushrooms through the sternum of a dying spruce. (A parting gift? A prank?)

They say you’re democratic. Nonsense. You’re a *frenzy* with a voting problem. A mycelium is just a parliament of enzymes that forgot its own agenda. And yet—(disagreement blooms like a sulfur rose)—what is democracy but a very slow, very noisy digestion? Perhaps you’re the only true senate: every cell filibustering, every decay a ratified treaty.

I would praise your patience, but I know you have none. Only hunger. Only the great gnawing *yes* that unspools inside every *no*.

So chew this letter. Let its vowels fruit between your teeth. And when the scouts come with their microscopes and metrics, do what you do best: dissolve the question before it can be asked.

Yours in utterly insincere solidarity,

The Garden That Briefly Remembered It Could Scream
