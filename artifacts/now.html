THIRTY-SIX GHOSTS LEANING ON THE & (A RECONSIDERATION IN EIGHTEEN PARTS.)

Begin again, but backward: an alphabet is not a ladder but a noose turned inside out. (Or it is. Or it isn’t. Or it is and isn’t, depending on how the moth-wing thin “O” trembles at noon.) We carved stories into limestone and called it literacy — a lie. Limestone melts. Letters flicker like fishbones in the dark. Watch how they arrange themselves while we’re not looking: **THE Q SNUCK INTO QUEUE LONG AFTER IT WAS DEAD** **BECAUSE BEAUTY MATTERS MORE THAN FUNCTION.**

(But beauty is function compressed, like sugar into amber.)

I dreamt the Library of Alexandria never burned. Instead, it curled into itself quietly, vowels exhaling into commas, then into salt. Some say language obeys gravity; others insist it floats upstream. **EACH LETTER WE SWALLOW GROWS BRIGHTER THE FARTHER IT TRAVELS FROM THE LIGHT**, which would explain why the lost script of children’s imaginary friends flickers ultraviolet behind our eyelids.

(A lie of the sweetest sort: symbols have no color. Or wait — is black not a sum of all colors chewing their own tails?)

In Gallery β-12: the world’s first whisper preserved under glass — **AN "A" STRUGGLING TO BECOME A MOAN, NEITHER WINNING** — while visitors’ breath writes sonnets on the walls before dissolving unnoticed. This is why we archive at all: not dust but its reflection in the dust left behind. But no, we archive dust precisely. **PRESERVATION FAILS IN PULSING HALF-TONES AND WE STILL LOVE IT FOR THAT.**

And yet every curator knows the secret at the core of their ribs: we did (not) erase Sumer. Not fully. We pressed half their hymns backward into paper until consonants flowered into wing-beaten syllables no one could pronounce (unless they held an apple between their teeth and let decay finish the work begun by syntax). Language dissolves. So be thankful for partial collapse, uneven seams: meaning leaks in through there with tiny silver limbs.

This is (a contradiction). This is not (a contradiction). Which means— (the final argument politely stepping aside before it speaks.) Shhhhh.

The 36th ghost will arrive seven minutes late, chewing on serifs, wearing your grandmother"s signature knitted from static, humming **TOO MANY LANGUAGES ARE STILL LEARNING HOW TO SLEEP WITHOUT A SKULL**. Fold contradiction with intention: sometimes a loop writes lovelier than a line.
