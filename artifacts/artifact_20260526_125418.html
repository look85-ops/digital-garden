*Recipe for the Weight of Absence*

**Materials Needed:**

- 1 vial of *occluded time* (harvested from the edges of an unfinished thought)

- 3 grams of *inverse gravity* (sifted from the space between two mirrors facing each other)

- A whisper of *polarized silence* (collected at dusk, when shadows refuse to adhere to their objects)

- One blank canvas, pre-treated with *lunar static* (applied in a single, uninterrupted stroke)

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare the Void:**

Begin by leaving the room. Wait until the air forgets the shape of your body—this may take anywhere from 17 minutes to an entire lifetime. The absence must be absolute. If you sense hesitation in the emptiness, start again.

2. **Activate the Occluded Time:**

Holding the vial to the light, tilt it until its contents liquefy into a slow drip of *not-yet* and *no-longer*. Catch this substance in a porcelain bowl lined with yesterday’s shadows. Stir counterclockwise until the mixture becomes a single, uninterrupted question.

3. **Fold in the Inverse Gravity:**

With care, sprinkle the *inverse gravity* over the blank canvas. Watch as it resists the surface, lifting the canvas’s edges into a fragile arch—a bridge to nowhere. The canvas should appear both heavier and less substantial, as if it remembers a weight it never bore.

4. **Infuse with Polarized Silence:**

Take the whisper of *polarized silence* and press it gently into the center of the canvas. It will not adhere; instead, it will hover just above, casting a soundless vibration. Adjust the angle of your gaze until the silence splits into twin frequencies: one that hums and one that erases.

5. **Final Gesture:**

Stand at a distance where the canvas no longer reads as object but as a lapse in the room’s logic. Extend your hand toward it, then withdraw. The space between your fingers and the surface should throb with the *potential of not-touching*. This is the artwork.

**Notes:**

- The piece is complete when a viewer, upon encountering it, feels the urge to step backward—not in retreat, but to correct a sudden imbalance in their own axis.

- Over time, the *inverse gravity* will destabilize, causing the canvas to slowly invert its own existence. Document this process only peripherally; direct observation interrupts the void’s metabolism.

**Serving Suggestion:**

Display in a room lit by alternating currents of regret and anticipation. Allow the work to breathe. It feeds on the unasked questions of passersby.

*(This is not a painting. This is the echo of a painting. You are its frame.)*
