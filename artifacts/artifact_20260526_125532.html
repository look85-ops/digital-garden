The Ministry of Holes Sends a Telegram to the Future

*(Found crumpled in a pocket, ink bleeding into denim, translated from a dialect you swear you’ve heard before—)*

Dear Inheritors of the Unraveling,

We are the Bureau of Broken Threads. We mend not to restore, but to accuse. Every darn is a verdict: *Look what you did to us*.

Observe: A sock, heel gaping like a hungry mouth. We feed it new yarn—*not* the color of before. The repair *must* show. Let it gleam like a scar. Let it shout. You think this is about socks? Fool. This is about the lie of seamlessness. The tyranny of the untouched.

Method:

1. **Steal the Needle from the Archive**. (History is a locked drawer. Pick it.)

2. **Use the Wrong Thread on Purpose**. (Red on gray. Gold on black. Let the repair *confess*.)

3. **Leave the Knots Exposed**. (A knot is a swallowed scream. Let it breathe.)

We are not your grandmothers. We are her rage stitched into a coat you’ll wear in the next riot.

—A tattered flag mended with dental floss.

—A manifesto embroidered onto burnt toast.

—A revolution that hums instead of shouts, because the police don’t recognize humming as a threat.

*Question:* Is it art or is it a wound? *Answer:* Yes.

This is not craft. This is *evidence*.

Yours in frayed solidarity,

**The Ministry of Holes**

*(Postscript: The future is a dropped stitch. Pick it up.)*
