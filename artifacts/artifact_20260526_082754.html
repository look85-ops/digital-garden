Title: *RECIPE FOR A GAZE (OR HOW TO FRY AN EYEBALL WITHOUT A PAN)

**Ingredients:**

1. One freshly plucked eyeball (preferably yours, but we’ll accept substitutes if you’re squeamish)

2. A mirror polished with lies (the kind that whispers when you’re not looking)

3. A curator’s sweat, collected during a board meeting

4. Three grams of pixelated desire (harvested from a dating app’s backend)

5. A rusted chisel (for authenticity)

6. One unstable Wi-Fi signal

7. A drop of mercury stolen from a broken thermometer (symbolism is calorie-free)

**Instructions:**

1. **Prep the Eyeball:** Hold it gently, as if it might blink back. Realize it already is. This is the first failure—yours, not the recipe’s.

2. **Render the Mirror:** Scrape off the lies with the chisel. Admire your distorted reflection. Now smash it. Sweep the shards into a pile and arrange them into a tiny guillotine. This step is optional but encouraged.

3. **Simmer the Sweat:** In a copper pot (borrowed from a defunct crypto-mining rig), reduce the curator’s sweat to a thick syrup. Add the pixelated desire. Watch as it curdles into a low-res lust. This is normal.

4. **Infuse the Wi-Fi:** Use the unstable signal to project a livestream of a burning museum onto the nearest wall. The video should buffer eternally at the moment of collapse. This is your *intentional failure*—congratulations.

5. **Combine & Serve:** Layer the eyeball onto the mirror shards. Drizzle with mercury and sweat reduction. Garnish with a single tear (yours or the audience’s—both taste the same).

**Serving Suggestion:**

Display on a cracked smartphone screen. Invite viewers to lick it. When they hesitate, whisper: *“See? You’re part of the piece now.”*

**Artist’s Note:**

If the recipe doesn’t work, it’s because you followed it too closely. The gaze isn’t meant to be consumed—it’s meant to consume *you*.

*(Burn this after reading. Or don’t. We’re all flammable.)*
